What the rule cannot do
It counts, and nothing more. It cannot say which vertebra is the extra one, so it flags the column rather than the level. A column carrying both an extra and a missing vertebra sums to 22 and passes. And its error is the counting error compounded over the whole column: one missed body anywhere turns an ordinary spine into a flagged one.
