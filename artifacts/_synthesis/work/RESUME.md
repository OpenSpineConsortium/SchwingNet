## What this folder holds

- tools/: the scripts that made artifacts/_synthesis/.
  - extract.py writes the plain text of every artifact, with e-mail addresses and phone numbers replaced before any reader sees it.
  - verify_quotes.py checks a quote against that text and the page's own files.
  - merge_contradictions.py joined the sweeps and merge_resume.py the results the first account's journal held.
  - prep_verify.py wrote the skeptics' input files, one per group of clusters and one for the numbers.
  - merge_skeptics.py keeps a workflow's results from its journal, merges the skeptics' verdicts into the record at each candidate's index, and puts a second reading in place of an earlier number verdict; every merge checks its input first.
  - sample_review.py draws the review's sample and writes the re-checkers' input files; run on the record as it stood at 1c656d4, it draws the review's 58 verdicts again exactly and checks them against out/review-137.json.
  - apply_review.py applies the review of the skeptics' verdicts, keeping each overturned verdict under first, and with --followup a later reading of verdicts the review left out, keeping what a ruling replaced under followup.before and naming under followup.rounds the group rounds that changed the row.
  - reread_numbers.py writes the follow-up's input, every number collision on the confirmed list that the review left out, again from the record as #137 merged it (fde5b06), and joins the follow-up workflow's answers into out/reread-confirmed-numbers.json; it writes the group judges' input from that reading too, and joins their rulings into the same file, and the same for the second round, whose judges and readers set each cluster row the narrow rule marked beside its group's finding, and for a third and a fourth pass, whose readers did the same for the rows each widened or sharpened rule adds; it puts the editor's words on hints and notes in the reading too.
  - check_verdict_quotes.py checks that each verdict quotes its own candidate's artifacts.
  - redact.py replaces what the folder's rule keeps out of the records and pages with an editor's words in square brackets.
  - render_contradictions2.py writes contradictions.md and one page per cluster under contradictions/, marking the cluster rows that give the figures of a group the follow-up ruled again together, each with its note on whether its instruction agrees with the group's finding, differs from it or is about another quantity, and why.
  - render_inventory.py, render_clusters.py, render_toc.py and textbook_toc.py made the other files.
  - synth.py keeps the synthesis current, its docstring giving the usage of each of its commands (status, concepts, report, plan, join and stamp); synth_state.py sets today's trees and dataset hashes beside the stamp, synth_concepts.py builds the concept ledger and checks every text against it, synth_reads.py says what a change needs read and joins the reads' answers into the records, synth_actions.py holds the steps of .github/workflows/synthesis.yml that are code (whether a run may read, each session's arguments and brief, the pull request's words), synth_schemas.py the JSON schemas the sessions answer in, and synth_base.sh lays the open synthesis pull request's reads on main and makes them again for the join.
- briefs/: what the sessions of .github/workflows/synthesis.yml are told: common.md, which every brief opens with, then read.md for the reader and finder of a new or changed artifact, skeptic.md, recheck.md for the re-checker told to overturn the skeptic and judge.md for the judge of a dispute.
- aliases.json: the names a text calls a dataset by, for a record under data/sources/ whose name gives none or the wrong ones; empty until one is needed.
- workflows/: the scripts of the follow-up's five workflows, the re-check with its judges (reread-confirmed-numbers.js, whose re-checkers' brief is the review's with the one added sentence and the changed-page note the reading's account names), the group re-rule (regroup-confirmed-numbers.js), its second round (second-regroup-confirmed-numbers.js), the third pass's readers (more-cluster-rows-confirmed-numbers.js, whose brief is the second round's readers' with one sentence added to its account of the work) and the fourth pass's (fourth-cluster-rows-confirmed-numbers.js, the third pass's brief with one more sentence), each run with the args.json that reread_numbers.py writes beside its inputs.
- out/clustering.json: the 14 clusters. out/toc-hand.json: the proposed table of contents.
- out/sweep-A-raw.json and out/sweep-B-raw.json: the finders' candidates for clusters 2 to 13 (372 and 269 candidates, Outreach included), with whatever verdicts came back first.
- out/contradictions-all.json: the merge of every sweep; out/clusters-verify-0-1-6.json: clusters 0, 1 and 6 as first verified; out/numbers-partial.json: the 247 number collisions and the 15 verdicts of #136's first run; out/numbers-verify-batches.json: the 87 number candidates the second run read.
- out/journal-results.json: what the first account's runs returned before they were stopped. out/journal-results-2.json and out/journal-results-3.json: the skeptics' verdicts of 2026-10-23, nine groups and then the tenth, as their journals held them.
- out/in-numbers.json: the 87 number candidates in the order the second run's indices count. out/in-reread.json and out/reread-results.json: the 10 number verdicts read again and their new verdicts. out/review-137.json: the review's sample, each re-checker's reading and each judge's ruling. out/reread-confirmed-numbers.json: the follow-up's 55 readings, its 20 rulings, the group re-rule of 15 of its rows in five groups and the second round's of 13 rows in three of them, with the reviews' points each judge was given, the notes on the cluster rows the rules marked, 49 from the second round, 26 from the third pass and 5 from the fourth, of which the page shows the 66 the present rule marks, and the editor's words on two hints and five notes (#155). work/editor-corrections.json holds those words as the editor wrote them.

## Keeping it current

Since #166 (pull request #167, 2026-10-23), .github/workflows/synthesis.yml keeps this folder current as artifacts, research notes and dataset records change, so a change is checked within a day, or within minutes where a person or an App merged it, and read by the run that finds it where that run may read, not by a day of reads by hand. A merge GitHub's auto-merge makes starts no workflow, so what it lands waits for the daily run at 06:41 UTC, or for Run workflow. Research notes and dataset records are checked by script alone: only an artifact, a verdict or a concept check becomes a Claude read. Its header says when it runs and when it may read; this section says what it does, and how to do each step by hand.

The stamp, out/state.json, says what the records were read from: for each artifact the git tree of its folder at the commit its record was read at, and for each record under data/sources/ the hash of its bytes; and what was already known when it was stamped: the quotes of each inventory record its text did not hold then, the verdicts whose quotes stand on another page than their own, and every concept check the texts gave. `SYNTH_SYMPHONY=<a checkout of symphony-template> python3 -I -B tools/synth.py status`, from this folder or anywhere, sets today's trees and hashes beside it and names each artifact and dataset record that is new, changed or gone; checks every verdict's and every record's quotes against today's texts, so a verdict whose words its page no longer holds is found by script; checks every text against the concept ledger and lists the checks the stamp has not seen; and says what each needs read. `report PATH --status F` writes the same as the Markdown of the issue labelled synthesis, from the JSON that `status --json F` wrote, under a marker made from the report as written, scrubbed, less the run's date and heads; the workflow rewrites the open issue's body whenever its first line is not that marker, so the report's own words reach the issue as its findings do, and leaves the issue as it is otherwise; `concepts` writes the ledger, out/concepts.json and ../concepts.md, whose opening says how a text is checked. None of these runs Claude, and `--without symphony-template` checks this repository alone, keeping the stamp's word for the other's artifacts, where no checkout of it can be made.

The reads are Claude's, and the workflow runs them. `plan DIR` writes their inputs; the workflow's sessions read them with the briefs under briefs/, answering in the schemas of tools/synth_schemas.py; and `join PLAN ANSWERS BATCHES SUMMARY` takes what came back into inventory.json, out/clustering.json, the contradictions record and the numbers table (out/numbers-partial.json), keeping each inventory record a later read replaces in out/inventory-history.json, renders the inventory, cluster, contradictions and concept pages again, and stamps the artifacts it read, the concept checks it settled and every dataset record's hash as it stands. It does not render table-of-contents.md, the proposed plan: a new artifact stays out of it until a person places it in a chapter (tools/textbook_toc.py) and renders the plan again (After #136). A run reads at most three new or changed artifacts, each by one session that writes its record and then, as the finder, sets it against its cluster and the numbers table, giving at most 10 cluster candidates, 10 number collisions of the kinds a skeptic reads and 20 of the others; then every verdict item, at most 24 planned ones and whatever the finders kept, up to 20 each, is read in batches of 12, the planned items and each finder's candidates in batches of their own, by a skeptic, then by a re-checker told to overturn the skeptic's verdict and its hint, and, where the re-checker disagrees, by a judge, as the review of #137 and the follow-up of #155 read theirs (tools/synth_reads.py names the limits). A finder's candidate is kept only where the script finds its quotes in the artifacts it names, and only the fields its schema gives are copied from it. What does not come back, or what the limits leave, waits for the next run: an artifact whose read failed is not stamped, so each run reads it again, which the day's cap bounds; a planned item with no verdict stays planned; and a finder's candidate with no verdict is a row the page marks as not read yet. Number collisions of another scope or another quantity get no skeptic, as the first readers' did not, and the page says so.

Three rules hold in what a run writes. Every record, page, issue and pull request body goes through tools/redact.py first, so what its rules name, a login, the owner's first name, a grid volume's path, a form's link, short or long, and the rest its docstring lists, is replaced by an editor's words wherever an artifact prints it, and the quote check matches around those words; what the rules do not name, another person's name or a network address written out in full, passes, so the owner reads the pull request before the tap. No cut leaves a piece of what is taken out, which no pattern would find again: extract.py moves a cut of an artifact's text back to the start of an e-mail address, a phone number or anything the rules name that it would split; the concept checks rid every text of addresses and phone numbers and scrub it as they read it, so no passage, no words a check keeps and no check's key holds any of it, whole or in part, and the ledger and its page are not written where either holds an address, a phone number or a credential's shape; and the report scrubs a passage or a quote before it cuts one to its length. Since a check's key is made from its passage as the scrub leaves it, a change to what the scrub takes out renames the keys of the checks whose passages hold what the change adds or drops, and the look would count each as a check gone and a new one, both listed in the issue and the new one read again where it is an artifact's check that gives a settled figure as superseded or open: the change's pull request, up to date with main and changing no text and no record the concepts are made from, runs `synth.py rekey <a checkout of main>`, which refuses where the two checkouts' texts or those records differ, or where either holds a change to them that is not committed, a link or a submodule among them or a folder no tree holds, pairs the checks main's tools give with the ones its own give, by concept, kind, source, line, outcome and value where those name one check on each side, and renames the stamp's keys, so the look counts as new only the checks no pair carries. The join refuses, besides, an answer, a record, a verdict or a finder's candidate, that holds an e-mail address, a phone number or a credential's shape. Every record is written in the layout the first reading wrote it in (synth.save: ASCII with escapes, an indent of one, a final newline only where the file had one), and a record the join adds takes its place in the order of the two artifacts/README.md indexes, so a run's diff holds only what it changed. And a run gives one answer whatever the hash seed, which `python3 -I` draws afresh in every process: nothing whose order reaches a result is a set left unsorted, and tests/test_synthesis.py runs the dataset checks of two texts against two records that share a name under four seeds, and fails where two of them differ, as two did before the first stamp was made.

`stamp --at REPO=REF` records what the records were read from at the commits named, and `stamp --checks` takes every concept check the texts give now as seen, once a person has read them. The first stamp, of 2026-10-23, was made from the records as #155 left them, read at SchwingNet 8dc1d8c and symphony-template 1355772. A dry run of a whole run on those records, with made-up answers, found nothing left to read at the next status, and the three faults it found are fixed and tested.

The reads run on the repository secret CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN, the token `claude setup-token` makes, so they spend that account's plan limits and bill no API; the owner's choice of 2026-10-23, that the reads start on their own, needs it, as an exception to the project's rule that no Claude credential is kept anywhere. It is the repository's one Claude token, so once it is set @claude (claude.yml), the coordinator (coordinator.yml, weekly and on an epic) and any worker registered in Actions run on it too. The repository variable SYNTHESIS_READS set to off stops every read, while the look still runs and the issue still says what waits; SYNTHESIS_READS_PER_DAY sets the day's runs (10 unless it says otherwise, 50 at most); Run workflow starts a run at any time and skips the six-hour pause after three failed runs, but not the day's count. A run whose sessions bring nothing back writes nothing and fails, and still counts toward the day. Otherwise the run adds what it read to the one open synthesis pull request, or opens it, from a branch synthesis/<run> that is one commit on main; the SymphonyBoard App opens it, so it waits for the owner's tap on owner-ok, which each later push asks for again, and merges by itself once its checks are green. Where main's rules do not require owner-ok, the pull request is a draft, made one again by each push, for a person to mark ready; and a Reject is kept, since no run builds on a pull request whose owner-ok failed on its head.
