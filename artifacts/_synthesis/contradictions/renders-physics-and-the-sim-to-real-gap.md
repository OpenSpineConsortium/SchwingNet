What each artifact says, in the same order:

1. A, lift-before-label-jzjq8t: Training on the real frame beats training on its DRR twin: 4.89 vs 7.83 mm; so build the registration route, keep the DRR only as augmentation and fine-tune on real frames. ("The DRR at the registered pose is a by-product you get for free, but training on the real frame beats training on that DRR (4.89 vs 7.83 mm landmarks on DeepFluoro, Rapuri 2026 )."). B, what-is-left-after-physics: In the same table, DRRs with +/-2.5 degree view augmentation were the best arm at every threshold (4.87 / 6.02 / 9.73), beating real-frame training (4.89) and the generator; physics plus pose augmentation wins everywhere. ("DRRs with +/-2.5 degrees view augmentation were the best arm at every threshold ( 4.87 / 6.02 / 9.73 ). A generator wins where the detector is already confident and loses where it is not; physics plus pose augmentation wins everywhere.")
2. A, ninety-three-pixels: Physics alone explains 80.9% of a real frame's attenuation variance (NCC 0.899); a per-frame surface takes the calibrated model to 93.9%. ("80.9% of a real frame's attenuation variance (NCC 0.899), same band shape in every specimen"). ("The stored DeepFluoro frames also turned out to be log-compressed display images; read correctly, physics alone explains 86% of a real frame rather than 80%, and the calibrated renderer about 90% of a held-out one.")
3.
