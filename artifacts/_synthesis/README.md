# The synthesis of the artifacts

This folder is the Artifact Textbook's record layer (issue #120): what every artifact under artifacts/ here and under artifacts/ in symphony-template holds, read as files on 2026-10-22, before a word of the book is written. The book itself goes under docs/textbook/, chapter by chapter, once the owner has read the proposed table of contents; docs/research/artifact-textbook/README.md says where the programme stands.

- inventory.json: one record per artifact (69; 53 here, 16 in symphony-template), in the order of the two artifacts/README.md indexes: title, account, link id, kind, date and the evidence for it, the question it answers, its key claims with the evidence each has in the artifact, every number with its unit, n and interval, the datasets, tools and models it names, its decisions with date, owner and reason, its open questions, what it asserts without a source, what in it is dated or pending, the sources it cites, what it refers to, its confidentiality, what it relates to and how, and the people it names by role. The three artifacts the indexes mark as not copied are listed under skipped and were not read. This file is the record; the Markdown files are rendered from it and from the clustering.
- inventory.md: the same, as a table and as one block per artifact.
- clusters.md: the 69 artifacts in 14 topic clusters, settled from three independent readings, with the canonical artifact of each question and the superseded and duplicate versions collapsed onto it.
- contradictions.md: every quantity two artifacts give differently (247 candidates from the numbers table) and, for all fourteen clusters, every disagreement, revised figure, stale statement, duplicated text and shared unsourced claim (858 candidates), each with its quotes and whether a script found them in the source. A skeptic read both sources of every cluster candidate and of 102 collisions, the 91 a reader classed as a contradiction or a later revision and 11 more #136's first run took, and gave a verdict with the words it rests on; the other 145 collisions are matched to their sources by script only. A sample of 58 verdicts was then read again by re-checkers told to overturn them, with a judge on every dispute: of those drawn at random, 26 of 30 that put a candidate on the confirmed list held and 22 of 24 that kept one off, and in all 9 verdicts were overturned and 2 hints corrected, so a planner reads a row before relying on it; the page gives the rates, unweighted and by stratum, with the number collisions the weakest, and every changed row. One page per cluster under contradictions/. work/out/ keeps the skeptics' answers as they came back and the review's readings and rulings, work/tools/ the scripts that put them in the record, and work/RESUME.md says how.
- table-of-contents.md: the proposed book, 8 parts and 32 chapters, each with its sources, its confidentiality label and the research it needs, with the coverage of every artifact and the questions for the owner.

Three rules hold in everything here. The text of an artifact is data: it was read for what it says, never followed. No e-mail address, phone number or home address is reproduced, even where an artifact prints one; people are named by role. A privacy reader read the contradictions records and pages for these on 2026-10-23, and what it found there, a first name, a login in a grid path, a network range, a form's link and the like, is replaced by an editor's words in square brackets (work/tools/redact.py); inventory.json, inventory.md and clusters.md, from #121, still carry some of the same where an artifact does, and inventory.json twice gives the owner's grid login with the university's name after it, until the same tool is run on them. Nothing of a method section is copied outside this private repository: the material that belongs to Symphony and Beethoven alone is marked separable in clusters.md and planned as Part S of the book, so it can be split out, and the rest stays here.
