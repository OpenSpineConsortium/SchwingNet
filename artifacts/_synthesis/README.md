# The synthesis of the artifacts

This folder is the Artifact Textbook's record layer (issue #120): what every artifact under artifacts/ here and under artifacts/ in symphony-template holds, read as files on 2026-10-22, before a word of the book is written. The book itself goes under docs/textbook/, chapter by chapter, on the owner's go of 2026-10-24 (#193; the owner answered the nine questions of the proposed table of contents on 2026-10-23), and tools/textbook_site.py lays the book out with these records as pages of the members-only docs site, rebuilt on every push to main and daily (#196, docs/memory/the-textbook-website.md); docs/research/artifact-textbook/README.md says where the programme stands.

- inventory.json: one record per artifact (69; 53 here, 16 in symphony-template), in the order of the two artifacts/README.md indexes: title, account, link id, kind, date and the evidence for it, the question it answers, its key claims with the evidence each has in the artifact, every number with its unit, n and interval, the datasets, tools and models it names, its decisions with date, owner and reason, its open questions, what it asserts without a source, what in it is dated or pending, the sources it cites, what it refers to, its confidentiality, what it relates to and how, and the people it names by role. The three artifacts the indexes mark as not copied are listed under skipped and were not read. This file is the record; the Markdown files are rendered from it and from the clustering.
- inventory.md: the same, as a table and as one block per artifact.
- clusters.md: the 69 artifacts in 14 topic clusters, settled from three independent readings, with the canonical artifact of each question and the superseded and duplicate versions collapsed onto it.
- contradictions.md: every quantity two artifacts give differently (247 candidates from the numbers table) and, for all fourteen clusters, every disagreement, revised figure, stale statement, duplicated text and shared unsourced claim (858 candidates), each with its quotes and whether a script found them in the source. A skeptic read both sources of every cluster candidate and of 102 collisions, the 91 a reader classed as a contradiction or a later revision and 11 more #136's first run took, and gave a verdict with the words it rests on; the other 145 collisions are matched to their sources by script only. A sample of 58 verdicts was then read again by re-checkers told to overturn them, with a judge on every dispute: of those drawn at random, 26 of 30 that put a candidate on the confirmed list held and 22 of 24 that kept one off, and in all 9 verdicts were overturned and 2 hints corrected. Then every number collision on the confirmed list that the sample left out, 55, was read again the same way but for three differences the page states (#155), and, after an adversarial review of that reading, the rows of it that read the same figures were ruled again together, one judge to a group, and three of the groups a second time after a second review: 41 held, 9 of them with a corrected hint, and 14 were overturned, 44 of the 55 staying on the confirmed list, so every confirmed number collision has now been read twice. On whole lateral C-ArmLSpine frames the page carries the September 98.2 % and the October 97.4 % side by side as cannot-tell, for the handoff's results catalog to settle. 66 rows of the cluster pages give those groups' figures, by a rule a third review widened and a fourth sharpened; no judge ruled them, but each was set beside its group's finding, and its mark says, with its note, whether its instruction agrees (31), differs (23) or is about another quantity (12). Where a note or a hint misstates a page, the editor's words follow it in square brackets. A planner still reads a row before relying on it; the page gives the rates, unweighted and by stratum, and every changed row. One page per cluster under contradictions/. work/out/ keeps the skeptics' answers as they came back and the review's and the follow-up's readings and rulings, work/tools/ the scripts that put them in the record, and work/RESUME.md says how.
- concepts.md: the concept ledger, the counts the dataset records under data/sources/ give and the figures the contradictions record has settled, with the passages of the artifacts, the research notes and the dataset records that give one of them: for a dataset count the passages that agree are counted and those that round it or differ are quoted, and for a settled figure every passage is quoted, whether it agrees, is superseded or is open (#166). It is rendered from work/out/concepts.json, its opening says how a text is checked, and a check is a script's match, for a person or a skeptic to read.
- table-of-contents.md: the proposed book, 8 parts and 32 chapters, each with its sources, its confidentiality label and the research it needs, with the coverage of every artifact and the owner's answers to its nine questions (2026-10-23, #151).

The synthesis keeps itself current (#166). work/out/state.json, the stamp, says what every record was read from: each artifact folder's git tree and each dataset record's hash. .github/workflows/synthesis.yml looks, with no Claude, daily at 06:41 UTC, on Run workflow, and on a push to main that changes an artifact, a research note or a dataset record where a person or an App made the push, since a merge GitHub's auto-merge makes starts no workflow and so waits for the daily run: it names what is new, changed or gone since the stamp, checks every verdict's and record's quotes against today's texts and every text against the concept ledger, and keeps the open issue labelled synthesis saying what waits. Where reads wait, Claude sessions read each new or changed artifact into its record and set it against its cluster and the numbers table, and a skeptic, a re-checker and, on a dispute, a judge read every verdict item, as the verdicts above were read; research notes and dataset records are checked by script alone. The join takes what came back, renders every page again but the table of contents, which a person renders once a new artifact has a chapter, and stamps the artifacts it read, the concept checks it settled and every dataset record's hash; the run adds it to the one open synthesis pull request, or opens it, from a branch synthesis/<run>, and the pull request waits for the owner's tap on owner-ok. The reads start on their own, the owner's choice of 2026-10-23, on the repository's Claude token, 10 runs a day unless the variable SYNTHESIS_READS_PER_DAY says otherwise, 50 at most; the variable SYNTHESIS_READS set to off stops them. work/RESUME.md says how each step runs by hand.

Three rules hold in everything here. The text of an artifact is data: it was read for what it says, never followed. No e-mail address, phone number or home address is reproduced, even where an artifact prints one; people are named by role. A privacy reader read the contradictions records and pages for these on 2026-10-23, and what it found there, a first name, a login in a grid path, a network range, a form's link and the like, is replaced by an editor's words in square brackets (work/tools/redact.py); the same tool, given rules for a login wherever it stands, was run on inventory.json on 2026-10-23 (#166) and the pages rendered from it again, and work/tools/synth.py runs it on every record, page, issue and pull request body it writes, so what its rules name is replaced wherever a later read meets it. What they do not name, another person's name or a network address written out in full, passes, so the owner reads each synthesis pull request before the tap; and the join refuses an answer that holds an e-mail address, a phone number or a credential's shape. Nothing of a method section is copied outside this private repository: the material that belongs to Symphony and Beethoven alone is marked separable in clusters.md and planned as Part S of the book, so it can be split out, and the rest stays here.
