What the inventoried artifacts give differently, found and checked on 2026-10-23 for the Artifact Textbook (issue #120, finished under #136). Readers went over the inventory's numbers table (3,025 numbers from 69 artifacts) for every quantity two artifacts give with different values, and over each topic cluster's full records for disagreements, revised figures, stale statements and shared unsourced claims. A script then looked for every candidate's quotes in the artifact's extracted text and, failing that, in the page's own files; the columns 'Quote A' and 'Quote B' say what it found. Last, a skeptic read both source texts for each cluster candidate and for each number collision a reader classed as a contradiction or a later revision, and gave a verdict: confirmed (both say what the candidate says and they disagree), superseded (the later one says or plainly implies it revises the earlier), refuted (they agree once read in context, or a statement is not there) or cannot tell. A blank verdict means no skeptic read that candidate; the reader's kind is then all there is. Nothing here is copied from memory, people are named by role, and no address of any kind is reproduced.
