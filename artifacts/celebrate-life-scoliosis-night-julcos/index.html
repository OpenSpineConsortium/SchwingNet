Celebrate Life Scoliosis Night

Celebrate Life

A soccer night for kids with scoliosis

Braces on. Braces off. Your call.
Most nights, a curve is a list of things you can’t do. This one is a team, a ball and two hours of nobody telling you to sit down.

Simultaneous with the
Detroit City Fieldhouse
Turf or Treat event!

Candy, costumes, all indoors Two indoor fields, side by side. One is given over entirely to trick-or-treating; the other is where we play. Costumes encouraged for players and families — and yes, a brace fits under a costume just fine.

Who plays

Every kid under 18 living with scoliosis or a spine condition — bracing, being watched and waited on, or after surgery with your surgeon’s OK.

Families welcome on the sideline.

Who coaches

Professional soccer players from Southeast Michigan, on the field with the kids for the whole game.

Teams are picked so everyone plays. Nobody sits the bench.

Eating

The Fieldhouse restaurant upstairs is open all evening and is where the watch party happens. Outside food and drink aren’t permitted in the facility, so come hungry and eat upstairs.
