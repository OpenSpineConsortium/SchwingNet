Request 8 · the most appealing

Radiopaedia: seven multi-frame spine fluoroscopy cases

rID 24796 (a rotational C-arm spin around a vertebral body stent), rID 90103 (a 61-projection oblique lumbar puncture series), rID 21585 (a lateral L1 vertebroplasty series) and four lumbar and S1 injection cases. Among the very few public multi-frame spine fluoroscopy series; native DICOM from the contributors would add pixel spacing, C-arm angles and frame timing.

  1. Open radiopaedia.org/licence in your own browser and read the current machine-learning terms.
  2. If the page links an application for non-commercial machine-learning use, file it and paste letter 8 as the project description. Otherwise email the licensing team; published studies cite license@radiopaedia.org and an approval by the licensing committee under "Non-commercial use of Radiopaedia content for Machine Learning". Confirm the address on the page first.
  3. Expect a non-refundable fee: the draft recorded USD 250, which could not be confirmed from public sources on 17 October 2026.
  4. Do not save frames until approval arrives. Their terms bar scripted downloading, and the licence is non-commercial: validating the 510(k) product would need a separately negotiated licence.
Before you send any letter
  • Commercial use. Every letter says non-commercial academic evaluation. Change that sentence and ask for a commercial licence if the frames might ever validate the level-confirmation product.
  • IRB. Ask the WSU IRB whether de-identified data received under a data-use agreement falls under your existing not-human-research determination, or needs its own.
  • Training. Letters 1 to 3 promise you will not train on the data. Delete that sentence if you might fine-tune.
  • Co-authorship. Letters 1 to 4 offer involvement or co-authorship; check it against your authorship plans.
  • YouTube copies. Letters to video uploaders say copies were saved for screening and will be deleted on request. Delete that sentence only if you are prepared to leave those copies unpermitted.
