# 16 Method decisions and what would reverse them

Label: confidential-method. Results may be shown, mechanism may not, until the provisional is filed; this chapter stays in this private repository and is copied nowhere.

This chapter is the decision log for everything Part III holds: for each method choice, its date, who made it (by role), its status, what it rests on and what would reverse it, so that a planner can tell a settled choice from one that only looks settled because nobody has tested it since. The decisions are numbered M1 to M23. [Chapter 24](24-decision-log-data-licences-and-splits.md) indexes them by number and repeats none of their content, and it and [chapter 25](25-decision-log-evaluation-reporting-programme-and-operations.md) log the decisions about data, splits, evaluation and operations; a pull request that changes a method decision should name its number. The chapter answers which decisions are locked, in effect, to revisit or done, by whom and when; what each rests on and what would reverse it; which were already overturned and by what; and how counting and naming live together in version 1.

## How the log was built

The 69 records of the artifact inventory carry 513 decision entries (the plan counted 512). The method entries, spread over about thirty records, consolidate into twenty-three decisions, merged where two records made the same choice for the same reason. An undated artifact is dated by what the record says of it: the decisions page after the board it supersedes (11 September), the detector field report before the context ladder (13 September), the two-anchors design before the research directions that rank it (8 October), and the residual page after the physics page it corrects (21 September).

Owners are named by role: the owner, by the owner's recorded word; the research group, the "we" of the superseded naming page's "two things we settled this week" (M10 and M11); and a working session, a Claude session working for the owner whose choice stands until the owner overrules it, as most September decisions do. Status is locked (fixed and not moved since), in effect (current, with a condition for revisiting), to revisit (the record already holds a reason to reopen it), done (carried out once) or overturned (reversed by a later record, in word or in practice). Every decision was checked against the retraining plan of 20 October, [docs/research/retraining-plan.md](../../docs/research/retraining-plan.md), whose record of the owner's words replaces earlier choices where they differ: the reported stack is context, not a baseline; the first model the workflow trains to work is version 1; nothing is rerun from scratch when a dataset is added; and seeds, folds and ensembles wait until version 1 works.
