# 13 Renders, physics and the sim-to-real gap

Label: confidential-method. Results may be shown, mechanism may not, until the provisional is filed; this chapter stays in this private repository and is copied nowhere.

This chapter states where SchwingNet's synthetic training frames come from and why the map from CT to fluoroscopy is calibrated rather than learned. For a planner it settles five things: that render resolution does not explain the detectors' failures on whole fields, though scale does on magnified ones; how much of a real frame the calibrated renderer explains and what the residual is made of; that no frame should be lifted to 3-D before naming, while registration can label real frames; which synthetic-to-real routes were refuted and on what evidence; and the three direct tests still owed and how they must be read. The detectors are [chapter 12](12-detectors-the-architecture-and-the-version-1-training-plan.md), naming [chapter 11](11-naming-from-morphology-context-and-a-sweep.md), the transitional spines that CT editing would make [chapter 14](14-transitional-anatomy-two-anchors-disagreement-and-abstention.md), FluoroCap's panels [chapter 15](15-fluorocaps-evidence-decoder-and-the-level-model.md), and the decisions this chapter feeds [chapter 16](16-method-decisions-and-what-would-reverse-them.md).

## How to read the numbers here

Variance explained is the share of a real frame's pixel variance that a render reproduces, in attenuation space unless display space is named; it measures likeness, not detection. Nearly every figure is Reported, since the result files behind the artifacts were not read here; the exceptions are the C-ArmLSpine naming figures that the repository's demo plan tables with their record lines and counts. The 21 September status board, [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/), is superseded in this cluster by [What Is Left After Physics](../../artifacts/what-is-left-after-physics/), so its numbers are a record, not a final value, and the board is linked where they are used. One figure bounds every single run below: three seeds on one held-out specimen spanned 26 points of sacrum recall.

## Where the training frames come from

A render traces cone-beam rays through a segmented CT with polyenergetic, multi-material attenuation, applies the device's terms, and projects each vertebra's mask through the same geometry to give its box and label, so its truth is exact by construction. On 21 September the board listed sixteen render corpora, only four with a pixel-spacing field; four had 82.7 to 85.6 percent of their renders from subjects in the cranial detector's training set, which hand-written normalisers had reported clean. It counted 156 of 1,939 TotalSegmentator subjects with both C2 and the sacrum and found VerSe regional, one mask in 120 spanning C2 to the lumbar spine (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). The OR Readiness Ledger renders from 142 whole spines, and how 156 became 142 is unsettled ([the renders contradictions](../../artifacts/_synthesis/contradictions/renders-physics-and-the-sim-to-real-gap.md), row 32).

The renderer's defects were found by looking: the first synthetic set carried stripes from the aggressive randomisation tier and blank frames from zero-count pixels clipping to an attenuation of 13.8. Overnight on 15 to 16 September the scatter term was found putting 50 to 82 percent of the spine's pixel value into a blurred copy of the bowel and the photon budget leaving 0.9 to 12 photons at the vertebra; both were fixed, to be tested by one small matched comparison ([Overnight: The Honest 99%](../../artifacts/overnight-the-honest-99-kantj8/)) that no page reports, so renders made before the fix carry the defect and fidelity is unmeasured ([the FluoroCap contradictions](../../artifacts/_synthesis/contradictions/fluorocap-the-corpus-its-pipeline-and-its-audits.md), row 26). Two traps sat in the data: 160 of DeepFluoro's 366 real frames are stored upside down, which held the first real-frame scores near 40 percent (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)), and the spacing of `verse_drr` is suspected about 15 percent short, so no millimetre measured on it is quotable (a record, not a final value; the superseded 20:12 build of [SchwingNet Figures](../../artifacts/schwingnet-figures-jshksq/), row d).

## Resolution and the film ladder

At the protocol's 140 mm field the corpora give 93 px (`ts_drr`) to 175 px (`ctsp_drr`), where the board bracketed a C-arm at 350 to 1,400 px for the same field (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)); the [FluoroCap Reality Check](../../artifacts/fluorocap-reality-check-czudvb/)'s vendor ranges, which the contradictions page carries as the C-arm band, are 0.151 to 0.194 mm pitch on a flat panel, 1,536 to 1,954 px across the whole detector of about 30 cm, and 0.22 to 0.30 mm on an image intensifier (row 59). The 1,536 to 1,954 px span the whole panel, not a 140 mm field, which at those pitches is roughly 720 to 930 px, a figure computed here and given by no source. The board tested the worry, a detector trained at 1024 px handed a 93-pixel frame, before paying for a re-render: the film-trained detector, scored on real films coarsened 1, 2, 3 and 6 times, from 836 to 139 px for the field, kept per-vertebra recall at 99.14 to 99.57 percent at confidence 0.05 and 98.28 to 99.15 at 0.25 (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). Its "0.00 points" is the 0.05 column; at 0.25 the coarsest rung is 0.43 points down, and no n or interval is given.

So resolution does not explain the detectors' failures on whole fields; it matters elsewhere, by task (rows 27 and 28). Raising the naming crop from 48 to 96 px lifted lumbar one-vertebra naming from 80.7 to 85.4 percent on films and from 73.7 to 82.8 on CT ([Level Context Ladder](../../artifacts/level-context-ladder-xd7eltc/)). A detector that never saw C-ArmLSpine finds 91 percent of its vertebrae on whole frames, 36 percent on a magnified three-vertebra field and 19 on one, while the same-device control keeps 82 ([OR Readiness Ledger](../../artifacts/or-readiness-ledger/)). The crops are upscaled windows: what fails is a scale the detector never saw, not a pixel count.

The board's C2 arm agrees. Of 2,593 walks to C2 by a fully trained cranial detector only 6 ended correct, 0.23 percent (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)), yet its C2 box lands on C2 in 218 of 219 renders and it finds C2 on 99.5 percent of full renders and 31 percent of 93-pixel sweep frames: C2 is inside the frame on only 15 percent, so the field voided the number. The ledger adds that render-trained arms find 85 to 88 percent of C2 on 934 validation renders and none on film (row 37). The re-render the board priced, about 0.3 mm per pixel with corpora about 25 times larger, was left to the owner, and no record says whether it was decided, run or dropped (row 50).

## DeepFluoro as the calibration set

DeepFluoro is 366 real frames of six cadaveric pelves on one Siemens Cios Fusion, each with a 1 mm CT and an exact pose; the registry adds 2,659 vendor frames, 2,293 of them unused with no written criterion ([SchwingNet Data Registry](../../artifacts/schwingnet-data-registry/)). It cannot test counting, since its vertebrae are one merged class over two or three lumbar levels and all 14 landmarks are pelvic, but it carries the sacrum on real frames, and because it pairs each frame with the CT and pose that made it, it is the one corpus that can calibrate the renderer pixel for pixel. The board reprojected the 14 shipped 3-D landmarks into the 2-D ones at 0.00 px over 111 frames and all six specimens.

The board's calibration is a record, not a final value ([Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). Physics alone explained 80.9 percent of a real frame's attenuation variance, with one band shape in every specimen. Nine device terms, among them receptor blur, halo, vignette, heel and scatter, fitted leave-one-specimen-out, gained a median +4.1 points (range -3.1 to +8.0), were stable across folds, and gained +4.3 with the scatter width boxed to 6 to 16 px, the calibration applied. Fitted on near-AP frames they held on the most lateral (+4.6), and terms fitted inside three tilt bins scored +0.0, -0.2 and -0.4 against the global ones, so they are applied unchanged within the 36.5 degrees the data span. The photon budget fitted at a median 2,918 per 0.78 mm pixel, where the builder had assumed 4,000, the 79th percentile. With 0.3 to 0.5 percent of variance stochastic the ceiling is 99.5 percent, and a per-frame quadratic surface took the calibrated model to 93.9.

Two corrections stand against these figures. The stored frames turned out to be log-compressed display images; read correctly, physics alone explains 86 percent rather than 80 and the calibrated renderer about 90 percent of a held-out frame ([Counting Levels Under Fluoroscopy](../../artifacts/counting-levels-under-fluoroscopy/)). The contradictions page carries 86 and about 90 as superseding 80.9 (rows 1 and 2), and 93.9 predates the correction too ([the detectors contradictions](../../artifacts/_synthesis/contradictions/detectors-architecture-and-the-training-plan.md), row 54). The concept ledger instead marks 80.9 percent current ([the ledger's line](../../artifacts/_synthesis/concepts.md#row-12-share-of-a-real-deepfluoro-frames-variance-explained-by-the-physics-renderer-alone)), because the October seed-grant proposal repeats it, but that paragraph is the September text, copied word for word into the Retrospective Fluoroscopy Protocol (row 42), with no word of the correction. Read 80.9 as pre-correction and 86 as corrected, and quote neither as final until the run behind the proposal is read. The gain is unsettled too: the ledger's Cios Fusion card, from a fit that reproduces the board's folds to 1e-3 per term, gives held-out R-squared from physics to fitted as 0.899 to 0.905, +1.3, but its baseline equals the board's NCC (0.899), not 80.9, so the two may be different measures, and the page carries +4.3 until the card file states its metric (row 8). The decision hangs on neither, since every term belongs to the device.

### Calibrated is not the same as matched

The board's verdict was calibrated, not learned. The next day's ledger declined a different claim, that frames styled by sampling a device card fall inside the real domain: on the Cios card the noise-power distance was 0.111 against a real-against-real floor of 0.015, the grey histogram 3.1 against 0.14 ([OR Readiness Ledger](../../artifacts/or-readiness-ledger/)). Both claims stand (row 9). The card sampler had its own defect ([Counting From The Sacrum](../../artifacts/counting-from-the-sacrum/)): it draws 51 of 114 device-parameter pairs from a Gaussian copula conditioned on water-equivalent thickness, but a clip applied to 8 to 11 correlated terms at once saturated instead of truncating, so renders reached only 1.7 to 5.1 percent of each device's joint distribution and 94.9 to 98.3 percent of frames carried a pinned term. A replacement reaching 99 percent is built and not adopted, more coverage not being automatically better training data, and its A/B has no outcome anywhere (row 10).

## What is left after physics

[What Is Left After Physics](../../artifacts/what-is-left-after-physics/), the canonical page, sets four real DeepFluoro frames beside calibrated renders of each specimen's own CT under the same pose, fits a twelve-coefficient quadratic smooth field (window, scatter pedestal, heel, vignette) to the difference, and splits what remains by spatial scale against the quantum-noise floor. Shares are percent of the real frame's attenuation variance inside the exposed field; the bands are not additive.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17-1882/000, near AP | 83.0 | 91.6 | 0.42 | 0.31 | 0.30 | 0.45 | 0.83 |
| 17-1882/050 | 80.1 | 87.1 | 0.12 | 0.32 | 0.54 | 0.80 | 1.71 |
| 18-1109/047, worst specimen | 54.6 | 86.9 | 0.11 | 0.45 | 0.82 | 0.82 | 1.09 |
| 18-2800/016, oblique | 90.1 | 97.1 | 0.08 | 0.09 | 0.12 | 0.18 | 0.21 |

The smooth field is most of the gap: twelve numbers with no edge in them take 18-1109/047 from 54.6 to 86.9 percent, its dark right half being collimation and vignette, not anatomy. After the field, the page says, the 2 to 16 px bands sit at 0.1 to 0.8 percent each, two to seven times the noise floor, so the board's "nothing local to learn" was too strong, and the contradictions page drops it (row 3). Read row by row, the table bears the withdrawal out on two frames of four: on 050 and 047 the bands are 2.7 to 7.5 times the floor, on 000 they sit at or below it (0.7 to 1.1 times), and on the oblique 016 they are 1.1 to 2.3 times it.

The residual has three parts. The thin lines along every cortical edge are the real frame being sharper than the render, since the CT is 1 mm voxels magnified 1.5 times onto 0.19 mm pixels; the mottle in the bowel is gas that moved between the CT and the fluoroscopy. Measured by region instead ([Counting Levels Under Fluoroscopy](../../artifacts/counting-levels-under-fluoroscopy/)), the collimator border ring, a fifth of the field, holds about two thirds of the residual, because the real frame falls off through the penumbra where the render stops at a hard edge, while inside it cortical edges agree within a few grey levels. The two readings answer different questions and both are carried (row 4).

Could a network learn it? The field is "yes to learn, no to predict": the device sets it per exposure, so a model given only the CT has no information about it, and a generator that learns it from pairs learns the training device's habits, which is fitting the nine terms and a window again and does not transfer. The cortical sharpness can only be invented, and an image-similarity score cannot tell invented texture from real; it is the one place a learned term could help a detector and the one place it could move a label. The bowel is unknowable. Learning to project is impossible as a deterministic map and, as a learned distribution, is what randomisation already is.

## What holds real-frame recall down

The board's first recall on real fluoroscopy came from a two-class detector, vertebra and sacrum, trained on 597 calibrated synthetic frames of 48 whole-spine subjects under 14 real poses: the sacrum on 76.9 percent of 365 real frames, 47.8 to 96.2 by specimen (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). The ledger assigns that figure to the `anchor_none_r2` generation and gives 64.0 percent for the v2 generation every real fine-tune started from, both carried with their generation (row 7). Three seeds on one specimen gave 51.4, 77.5 and 56.8 percent, wider than any difference between the board's single-seed arms.

The population-matched control settled the cause, whichever of its figures holds. [Counting Levels Under Fluoroscopy](../../artifacts/counting-levels-under-fluoroscopy/) gives, for a detector trained on renders of DeepFluoro's own CTs with six specimens held out in turn and three seeds each, the sacrum on 90 to 97 percent of real frames against a constant-box prior of 5 to 60; trained on renders of living whole-body CTs it finds 64 or 77 percent. The renderer is not the limit: embalmed pelves in air are not living abdomens, and the labels differ. The seed-grant proposal gives about 96 to 98 percent; the contradictions page carries 90 to 97, tied to named runs (row 5), and the concept ledger marks every value open ([the ledger's line](../../artifacts/_synthesis/concepts.md#row-10-sacrum-recall-on-real-deepfluoro-frames-detector-trained-on-renders-of-deepfluoros-own-cts)). A third figure, 88.5 to 91.3 percent at confidence 0.05 over three seeds, is the ledger's clean render-only number for one specimen, after it ruled a 100 percent figure not the method because that detector's initialisation had seen the test specimen's poses and device terms fitted on its pixels; which renders trained the clean detector is not stated, so it cannot be placed (row 6). These are detection results: a fixed median sacrum box hits the truth on 13.5 percent of DeepFluoro frames, against 85.7 percent on C-ArmLSpine, where "sacrum found" partly reflects how surgeons centre the field (row 38).

Beyond DeepFluoro the record says renders carry naming and not detection. On films a detector trained on 6,650 renders reaches 71.67 percent per-body recall against 99.14 for one trained on real film, and with its boxes count exactness fell from 100 to 30.06 percent on 79 held-out spines ([AIDaS Seed Grant Proposal](../../artifacts/aidas-seed-grant-proposal/); [Counting From The Sacrum](../../artifacts/counting-from-the-sacrum/)). On C-ArmLSpine the pages disagree about a renders-only detector: the demo plan gives 0.3 percent of 287 vertebrae at confidence 0.74, 31.7 at 0.25 and 7.0 with no frame of the device ([docs/research/demo-plan.md](../../docs/research/demo-plan.md)), the [Any-View Spine Plan](../../artifacts/any-view-spine-plan/) 0.3, and the ledger 17.1, 2.4, 31.7 or 54.7 by truth convention, and the contradictions page cannot tell which to carry until a results row names convention, threshold and render generation together ([the detectors contradictions](../../artifacts/_synthesis/contradictions/detectors-architecture-and-the-training-plan.md), row 6). With other devices' real frames in training the demo plan gives 80.5 percent at 0.74 (231 of 287, [74.1, 86.8]) and the ledger 90.6 at 0.25 under outline truth, the renders page carrying the ledger's figure until the run behind 80.5 names its convention (row 12). Every renders-only value sits well below both. Naming is the other side: the level encoder, whose only intraoperative appearance came from renders, names lateral C-ArmLSpine frames zero-shot at 96.4 percent (595 of 617, [92.3, 98.4]), within about a point of the model fine-tuned on the device (97.4 percent in the demo plan, 98.22 in [Counting From The Sacrum](../../artifacts/counting-from-the-sacrum/), a pair the record has not settled: [the renders contradictions](../../artifacts/_synthesis/contradictions/renders-physics-and-the-sim-to-real-gap.md), row 13), while AP falls to 43.7 percent (276 of 631), a counting error, not an appearance one ([docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md](../../docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md)). The demo plan's summary is the sentence to keep: synthetic training transfers for naming, and detection needs real frames of other devices.

## Randomisation, and the arms only the board holds

For strong domain randomisation, the lever the literature said was unpulled, the board built eight arms on 21 September from 11,007 labelled real films, a 2 by 2 of strong randomisation against the fluoroscopic noise term, a scale arm and three noise levels, with labels byte-identical across arms and 79.7 percent of images changed (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). At uneven epochs their recall ran 0.975 to 0.986, but each arm is validated on data degraded as its training was, so in-domain validation cannot show transfer; only `dr_eval` can, and no artifact reports it. Lift Before Label's earlier "your renderer is already here" therefore overstates the renderer as it then stood: physics at the DeepDRR level, strong randomisation only from about 21 September, and a card sampler reaching a twentieth of its distribution or less (row 10).

## The refuted routes

Four ways of making a render look real were weighed or tried, and each failed on named evidence.

### Adversarial translation

On SyntheX's six DeepFluoro specimens CycleGAN scored 6.62 mm and ADDA 6.41 against 6.44 for no adaptation and 6.46 for training on the real frames: indistinguishable, CycleGAN's point estimate worse, both needing real fluoroscopy to train. Paired diffusion was 7.5 mm worse at low confidence and pix2pix hallucinated geometry (a record, not a final value; [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)). What Is Left adds that CycleGAN helped about 1 mm under weak randomisation and nothing under strong, the randomisation absorbing what a translator would supply. The GAN was not built.

### The project's learned refiners

The project did train learned arms, six folds by three seeds against one baseline on the same folds ([Counting Levels Under Fluoroscopy](../../artifacts/counting-levels-under-fluoroscopy/)). From a 64 percent baseline a high-pass refiner reached 72, a training-free Fourier low-band swap from real frames 74 and a full RealDRR-style refiner 72: all the same sign, each smaller than the seed spread, none within fifteen points of the matched control. On the control's own renders they added two to five points. On the 366 real frames the refined render agreed with the real frame slightly less than the plain calibrated one, 68.1 against 69.3 percent of display variance, better on one frame in five: it sharpens, which the detector gains from, and it invents, which the pixels pay for. The proposal's "results are pending" is stale (rows 23 and 24).

### Diffusion img2img

FluoroGen restyled renders with SDXL image-to-image, a Canny ControlNet and a fluoroscopy LoRA ([docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md](../../docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md)); its lane in the [FluoroCap Pipeline Map](../../artifacts/fluorocap-pipeline-map-h9rmbs/) is historical (row 25). At strength 0.45 to 0.65 the output looked like fluoroscopy and had lost the vertebrae; the array was cancelled after 12,896 images, and no strength was usable, 0.2 leaving the render unchanged and 0.35 already smearing end-plates and pedicles ([FluoroCap Overnight Report](../../artifacts/fluorocap-overnight-report-xnhgdo/)). One thread is loose. `synth_rare`, 12,929 frames with 1,763 L6 and 86 T13, carries exact level truth in its dataset record ([data/sources/fluorogen_synth_rare.json](../../data/sources/fluorogen_synth_rare.json); [Fluoroscopy Data Atlas](../../artifacts/fluoroscopy-data-atlas/)), while the FluoroCap contradictions rule it the cancelled array's output, its "exact" a correction to make and its file count to settle, 12,896 against 12,929 (row 25), and the CT-priors note calls the frames diffusion-styled renders whose edges can disagree with their labels. A count and a look at the frames settle it.

### Unpaired translation and super-resolution

CUT carries every label over exactly by construction, but its v1 trained toward the FluoroCap panel directories as they stood, the modality classifier calling only 2,991 of 18,119 panels spine radiographs, so v1 is no test of translation; v2, restricted to those panels, was queued on 15 September and is reported nowhere ([FluoroCap Overnight Report](../../artifacts/fluorocap-overnight-report-xnhgdo/); row 26). The 8 October roadmap lists SDXL, CUT, CycleGAN and ADDA translation as giving no gain in any arm ([Research directions from the data holding](../../artifacts/research-directions-from-the-data-holding-kcyckb/)); for CUT that rests on an invalid v1 and an unreported v2. Super-resolution was rejected without a run: the [FluoroCap Reality Check](../../artifacts/fluorocap-reality-check-czudvb/) found no controlled radiograph study showing it restoring task-relevant detail and budgets rendering down to the measured pixels per vertebra with blur augmentation instead, and What Is Left adds that detail below the CT's resolution is not in the input, so whatever a network adds there is invented.

### What the refutations add up to

Every surviving source in What Is Left's verified pass (105 claims extracted, 25 checked, 10 kept, each a single run) agrees with the project's arms: the residual after physics is low-frequency and per-image, and the measured remedies were calibrating the renderer, randomising over the field, or swapping the lowest band per image from real frames, never a learned generator, which at best matched those. The closest analogue is Fourier domain adaptation (Yang 2020), whose 11 by 11-bin box gains as much as nine times its width, 44.6 against 45.0 mIoU from a source-only 36.6; in DeepDRR (Unberath 2019) physics took real-frame landmark error from 78.2 to 24.7 mm, scatter only the last 2.2 of it; and in Rapuri 2026 a generator won only where the detector was already confident. The refutation is narrower than it sounds: the workstream P note records translation helping thoracolumbar 2D/3D registration, from 10 to 90 percent success with CUT and feature alignment though still about three times the error of real-pair training (Jaganathan 2023), and topogram segmentation (Zhang 2018) ([docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md](../../docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md)). What is refuted is learned DRR-to-fluoroscopy appearance on the pelvis once physics and randomisation are in.

## Lifting to 3-D before naming

[Lift Before Label?](../../artifacts/lift-before-label-jzjq8t/) answers from four literature sweeps of 15 September with 173 findings. Its verification pass never ran, so where the Reality Check cites the same paper its verified figure is carried (row 52). The answer is no, not from one view. No paper reconstructs a radiograph to 3-D, then classifies and reports a gain over the 2-D classifier, except chest X-ray to CT angiography for embolism, which does not transfer, since here the generator would learn from the classifier's own CTs. The closest work, Jecklin 2024, needed 3,317 pose-paired real frames from 10 cadavers to lift a DRR-trained lumbar reconstruction from a surface score of 0.08 to 0.63-0.73, below its 0.79 DRR ceiling, in the Reality Check's verified reading; Lift's "F1 0.84 against 0.86" is cannot-tell (row 20), though the workstream P note, which read its papers twice, gives both measures from one table. The objection of principle is that single-view lifting completes the frame from a prior, and the prior completes an L6, an absent twelfth rib or a corpectomy defect toward the typical spine, which is exactly what decides the label; reconstruction benchmarks degrade most on fractures, implants and cohort shift (Kausch/Shakya 2023).

What 3-D knowledge buys goes into the 2-D model instead: physics in the renderer, off-axis view sampling (Fok 2024), two-view fusion where both views exist (ProVLNet 2025 on biplanar DRRs; Huang 2022, +15.5 points AP and +35.0 lateral on cadaver long films), correspondence pretraining of the crop encoder from DRR pairs (+1.4 to +2.0 points, Dabboussi 2025), and a pose head only as a late fine-tune, co-training having more than doubled landmark error (Mo 2025). Do not build a reconstruction stage, and treat any 3-D representation as pretraining measured on real held-out frames. The October CT-priors note agrees, and the Any-View plan warns that a synthesised lateral paints a typical spine where a listhesis shows only on the true lateral, so a generated lateral is a display aid, not a film (row 39).

## Registration as a label source

Registration is the other half, and it is yes: registering the patient's CT to the frame and projecting the CT's labels puts an exact level on every visible vertebra of the real image without counting. LevelCheck put the right level on 398 of 398 radiographs of a 178-patient series within 30 mm, and a registration network trained on real projections reached 99.4 percent success against 66.2 from simulation (Jaganathan), as [Lift Before Label?](../../artifacts/lift-before-label-jzjq8t/) reports. Lift's line that registered real frames beat their DRR twins needs one qualification: in Rapuri 2026's Table 1 training on the real frames gave 4.89 mm against 7.83 for DRRs of the same CTs at the registered poses, but DRRs with +/-2.5 degrees of view augmentation were the best arm at every threshold (4.87, 6.02 and 9.73 mm), so the real frame beats only the unaugmented twin, and all three arms are carried (row 0). The twin stays as augmentation and the real frames are for fine-tuning, which is how pairs have paid; paired against unpaired translation bought at most one point.

Budgets differ by purpose (row 30): hundreds to low thousands of registered frames over at least 10 to 20 patients to fine-tune, since no learning curve over registered real frames exists for any spine task, and the Reality Check's 3,000 to 5,000 frames from 800 to 1,500 patients as a year's collection target. The failure modes are the spine's periodicity, whose characteristic miss is one vertebra, supine-to-prone deformation, instruments and bowel gas; Lift's warning that registered labels favour frames that register well is unsourced and carried as a caution (row 48). The project's DiffDRR and DiffPose harness, running on 15 September, reports no outcome. In October registration is a labelling route, not a product: COBRA's real projections are to be labelled from the planning CT with exact geometry, and its simulated projections at the same geometry are the one kind of pair that measures the simulation gap directly ([docs/research/xr-from-3d/README.md](../../docs/research/xr-from-3d/README.md)), while C-ArmLSpine's poses are the project's own unmeasured registrations, so their residuals enter workstream P only above a pose-quality bar.

## Editing the CTs before rendering

Editing a labelled CT before rendering gives exact truth for what the real sets lack. Hardware insertion is mature and validated on real frames, SyntheX's tool Dice being 0.92 trained on simulation against 0.41 trained real-to-real on 264 cadaver frames (Gao 2023), so it stays, with the 10 to 28 point residual Lift expects budgeted until real instrumented frames arrive. Lesion synthesis helps 3-D segmentation but has no DRR-downstream number. Whole-CT generation loses once rendered, 5.82 mm against 4.89 for real frames and 4.40 for 2-D diffusion (Rapuri 2026), so no CT generator is built. Editing a CT to make an L6, a sacralised L5, a T13, an absent twelfth rib or a corpectomy is unpublished, cheap with the project's label maps and worth a week, but provable only on real frames with CT-confirmed levels, so its evaluation waits on registration; the pool is 802 CTs and 801 distinct subjects, whether an id is one person still open (row 33). Recognition gaps are wider than geometric ones, Lift warns: a surgical-phase recogniser fell from 93.8-99.2 percent in simulation to 67.6-71.7 on cadaver frames, 257 of them by the [FluoroCap Reality Check](../../artifacts/fluorocap-reality-check-czudvb/), which gives both ends of each range (Pelphix, never a detection citation, row 19). Rare classes are hard even inside simulation: the rare-class bank `physics_rare` scored 45.1 percent in random-window naming against 93.9 on ordinary renders ([Research directions from the data holding](../../artifacts/research-directions-from-the-data-holding-kcyckb/)), a gap between renders, not between renders and real frames.

October made editing workstream N, issue #70, after the talk and scored apart from the real arm, and on 21 October the owner asked that anomalies be called once on CT and carried to X-ray by renders whose labels say L6 exactly ([docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md](../../docs/research/ct-priors-for-2d-detection.md)). Mixed training is the default because pretrain-then-fine-tune lost 25.6 anomalous points, a record, not a final value ([Research directions from the data holding](../../artifacts/research-directions-from-the-data-holding-kcyckb/)), the same roadmap's open list saying that default rests on a leaked-bank arm not known to have been re-run clean. And no synthetic-to-real ratio may be claimed, since none was swept ([docs/research/state-of-knowledge.md](../../docs/research/state-of-knowledge.md)).

## The October programme and its tension

Workstream M, issue #69, is the physics renderer ablation, physics renders with everything that is not anatomy randomised against the current styled renders and plain DRRs, because in the CT-priors note's words the renders-only detector's figure "may be the renderer's fault and not synthetic data's"; it comes after the talk, behind an issue for its new dependency. E harmonises devices while keeping shape, its randomisation layers before the talk. P, issue #135, carries naming from the lumbosacral gold to C2 by whole-spine renders, physics noise, a shape-preserving denoiser and generative translation, keeps a translated render only when its geometry still matches the projected labels, and ends in a reverse ablation that removes one ingredient at a time ([docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md](../../docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md); [docs/memory/from-3d-to-x-ray.md](../../docs/memory/from-3d-to-x-ray.md)). It grades the upward transfer Inferred and untested: automatic exposure at the shoulders, scatter through the chest and the mandible over C2 appear in no lumbosacral pair.

The tension is that P reinstates generative translation a month after the September record refuted it on the pelvis. The two reconcile only on the evidence's own terms: keep P's translation only if the reverse-ablation arm without it loses by more than the seed spread, seeds averaged and persons as folds, read beside the rejection rate and realism of the kept frames that P's design already reports.

## The three direct tests

What Is Left states its own limit, that the decomposition is pixel variance and connects to no miss, and names three tests with no number anywhere in the record: sample the fitted twelve-coefficient surfaces of the 366 real frames as an augmentation on renders, FDA's swap made parametric; correlate the missed frames with their fitted coefficients and view angle; and fit and divide out the surface on each real frame at inference. The low-band swap of 21 September, 74 percent against 64, is the first test's measured relative; the parametric sampling, the correlation and the division remain unmeasured (row 51). All three must be read with seeds averaged and specimens as folds, because the literature's effect sizes for such remedies (+3.5 Dice, +/-0.2 mm, +0.18 Dice) are smaller than this detector's 26-point seed spread at one seed. The 21 September runs are the template, six folds by three seeds against one baseline on the same folds, with `tools/deepfluoro_anchor_report.py` printing the spread beside every mean; an arm inside the spread shows no effect however steady its sign, as the refiners showed. This chapter adds two rules: score each arm on renders of the test population and of living CTs, since population moved recall more than any appearance remedy, and report pixel agreement beside recall, since a remedy can raise one while lowering the other. The correlation needs no training and is the cheapest.

## What we know

The CT-to-fluoroscopy map is calibrated, not learned: nine device terms fitted leave-one-specimen-out on DeepFluoro's exact geometry are stable across folds and need no per-angle version, and what they leave is mostly a twelve-coefficient smooth field the device sets per exposure, which no model of the CT could predict. Physics alone explains 80.9 percent of a real frame by the board's reading (a record, not a final value, from [Ninety-Three Pixels](../../artifacts/ninety-three-pixels/)), which the ledger marks current, and 86 percent once the log-compressed frames are read correctly, which the contradictions page carries. After the field the residual is cortical edges finer than a 1 mm CT, moved bowel gas and the collimator border, several times the noise floor on two of four frames, so "nothing local" is withdrawn. On the board's film ladder resolution did not limit detection on whole fields, while magnified fields cost an unseen device's detector most of its recall and crop resolution helps naming. Population, not appearance, held DeepFluoro's real-frame recall down: every appearance remedy tried moved it less than the 26-point seed spread, and the learned refiner made the image slightly less like the real one. GAN translation, FluoroGen img2img and super-resolution are refuted or rejected on named evidence, and CUT v1 was no test. Renders carry naming and not detection. No reconstruction stage should be built; registration labels real frames, which beat only the unaugmented DRR twin; hardware editing is mature, transitional editing new and untested, and whole-CT generation loses.

## What we do not know

No artifact reports `dr_eval`, the matched comparison after the scatter fix, the sampler's A/B, CUT v2, the registration harness, the fate of the re-render or issue #69's three arms, so the renderer's fidelity after its fixes is unmeasured, and none of the three direct tests has a number. The record has not settled the variance figure (80.9 against 86), the calibration's gain (+4.3 against +1.3), the matched sacrum figure (90 to 97 against about 96 to 98, with 88.5 to 91.3 unplaced), C-ArmLSpine detection by a renders-only detector or with other devices' real frames, the TotalSegmentator count (156 against 142), or what `synth_rare` is. Whether styled frames can reach the real noise floor, whether a correction learned on lumbosacral pairs travels to the thoracic and cervical spine, whether transitional edits transfer to real frames, and how a level label's gap shrinks with registered real frames are all untested. Lift Before Label's literature was never verified, and the research the plan owes, checking the pass against the FDA, SyntheX, DeepDRR, N4 and diffusion papers, confirming SyntheX's 6.62, 6.41 and 6.44 mm as landmark errors and checking the 0.00 px geometry against the IPCAI 2020 release, needs a network this chapter did not have.

## What would change our mind

If sampling the fitted surfaces as augmentation, or dividing them out at inference, raised real-frame recall by more than the seed spread with seeds averaged and specimens as folds, the smooth field would be a detection lever and not only a pixel one; if misses tracked the field's coefficients or the view angle, appearance would re-enter the account that population now holds. If #69's physics arm lifted renders-only detection on C-ArmLSpine far above the styled arm, the renderer rather than synthetic data would explain the render-only failure, and the re-render would reopen. A CUT v2 or P translation arm that beat no translation by more than the seed spread on real frames, geometry checked, would confine the refutation to the pelvis under strong randomisation. Styled frames at the real noise floor that taught a detector to find vertebrae on an unseen device would weaken "detection needs real frames". A learning curve reaching parity at tens of registered frames would shrink the collection budget, and registration labels found wrong on frames that register poorly would turn the bias caution into a finding. Rendered transitional edits that raised L6 naming on real CT-confirmed frames would make editing the main route to the rare classes, and a reconstruct-then-classify gain on real frames would reopen lifting.
