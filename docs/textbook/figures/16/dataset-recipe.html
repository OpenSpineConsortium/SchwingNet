SchwingNet · choosing what to keep

A recipe for the final dataset

The goal: name the vertebral level on a limited-field X-ray anywhere from C1 to the sacrum, robust to anomaly, rotation, acquisition machine and domain. This page sets your own results, the published papers and the benchmarks beside each of those five demands, turns them into a recipe of dataset strata, and lets you trial what to keep.

How to use it
  1. Read the evidence for each demand. Each card says what was found, how sure it is (Measured is your record, Published is a paper, Inferred is reasoning), and what it asks of the dataset.
  2. Open the strata on the bench. Every set starts at the recommended call. Untick a set to drop it, or tick one to keep it; the side panel updates the size, the coverage along the spine and the warnings.
  3. Copy the selection string at the bottom into spine-datasets' storage workflow. It opens a request pull request, and nothing moves until you merge it.
