The amount of each data type, by corpus

Persons or images per corpus from the dataset records, coloured by data type (paired 3D-2D, annotated 2D, synthetic 2D, CT volumes, unannotated 2D), filtered by reach and licence class. Read which types are large and which are a handful of persons.

MeasureReach
Type

Bars are on a logarithmic axis, since the corpora run from one phantom to hundreds of thousands of images. Images count what each record counts (films, frames, projections, clips, CT volumes); the unit is in each bar's tooltip. Sub-sets (the two COBRA vendors) and the anomaly-harvest umbrella are left out of the sums. Source: python3 tools/data_figures.py write over data/sources/, data/registry/, data/licences.json and corpora-overlay.json.
