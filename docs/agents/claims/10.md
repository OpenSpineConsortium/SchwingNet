## Plan

The registry of task #10 lands as a queryable catalog, not a pile of files: one registry file per source under data/registry/ naming its identity level, its person-key rule and its shards; a shard manifest shape with content hashes, study-local person keys and per-frame facts (level, anatomy class, device, view angle, exposure, source, acquisition); the person-keyed hash split with a stated salt and ratio and the frozen test-person list under data/splits/; and tools/catalog.py, which builds a SQLite catalog from the registry and the manifests (extending the holding index's model in spinesurg-ct-nnunet data_platform/db) and answers queries by those facts and by embedding similarity, so a training run names a query and reads only its answer. The pure rules (the shapes, the split hash, the query predicates) go in src/schwingnet/catalog.py under beethoven-qa's gate with tests; C-ArmLSpine and the sweeps are registered first; the design is docs/research/data-catalog.md. CPU jobs on the grid, when any, wait for the owner's word and number.
