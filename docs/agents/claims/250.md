## Plan

Build a retrieval layer over the textbook as swappable, measured blocks in the style of the training blocks: src/schwingnet/rag.py holds the parts (chunk, tokens, index, retrieve, answer, embed) with version 1's defaults and the pure functions that build a plain-data index and score a question against it; tools/rag.py builds the index, asks it, evaluates it against docs/textbook/rag/questions.json and fails below the floor written there, wired into scripts/check.sh. tools/textbook_site.py builds the index into the site and gives every page an Ask the book box, a JS mirror of the scoring held equal to the Python by a shared fixture; the artifact edition answers through the viewer's own Claude with the page's search as a tool. The briefs tell a session to ask the book first; the memory, the research note and the chapter on the workflow's plug points record it.
