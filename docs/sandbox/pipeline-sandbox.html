Pipeline sandbox

Build a deep learning pipeline from blocks: rest the pointer to the right of a block and the sandbox grows the block that likely comes next, pipes the output into it, and works out the types, the grid jobs and the request as you go. A run goes only through a request the owner approves with a job count; this page sends nothing to the grid.

Pick a source from the palette, then hold the pointer to the right of it.

Hold the pointer to the right of a block: a ghost of the likeliest next block appears and is added, piped from the block, when its ring fills. The wheel or the arrow keys pick another suggestion, 1 to 5 add one at once, Escape cancels; a click or a tap there adds the first at once. With a card focused, Shift and the right arrow do the same. Drag a card to move it, drag from a right-hand dot to a left-hand dot to connect two blocks.

How to read this
block
one step of a pipeline, described by a manifest under tools/sandbox/blocks/: what it takes, what it gives, its params, its job and what runs it today.
port
where a type enters a block (the dots on its left) or leaves it (the dots on its right); an edge pipes one output into one input.
type
what passes along an edge: data, a model, a checkpoint, metrics or a report, with its axes and sizes, its dtype, its targets, its parts, whether the person key rides along, its licence and the sources it came from. A model keeps the type of the data it was built for, which is how it adapts to that data.
fan
how many runs a block makes: the product of the values its own params and the params upstream vary over; a select block joins the arms back into one.
route
the experiment route, live today: one prefilled issue per run, which the experiment workflow answers with the gate word; or the pipeline route, a plan recorded as an issue until the owner's word builds the route that runs it.
job
what the grid would run for a block: CPU or GPU, how many submissions and tasks, and the hours each task asks for. No job is sent from this page.
digest
the SHA-256 of the pipeline's canonical JSON; the same pipeline gives the same digest here and in tools/pipeline_sandbox.py, so a request names exactly what was built.
