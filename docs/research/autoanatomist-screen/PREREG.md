## 1. The question

The auto-anatomist trains two encoders of one network, one reading a CT crop and one reading privileged channels (the target vertebra's mask and every vertebra's), fused as F = (1 - w) F_ct + w F_mask; under objective=mask its decoder gives the two masks back and a head names the level, so nothing is measured anywhere and the network's bottleneck is the latent a shape prior is fit on later (grid/autoanatomist/autoanatomist/README.md). The annealer takes the privileged channels down a ladder as lambda goes from 0 to 1, until they are a function of the CT alone, and hands the decoder from the mask branch to the CT branch as w goes from 1 to 0. The question: on real CTs, does a network trained with an anneal outline vertebrae from the CT alone better than the same network trained on the CT alone from its first step, and which placement of the two ramps does best? The screen answers the placement only by which arms pass the bar of section 4; it ranks no arm against another, and an arm that passes goes on to a longer run under a pre-registration of its own, which names any ranking before its send.

## 3. The measurements

At each reading (every 5 epochs of the screen's 50, every 10 of the smoke's 150, and always after the last), every validation crop is read by the CT-only path, the decoder at w 0 with no mask (train.evaluate_masks): the mean symmetric surface distance of the target vertebra's mask in millimetres (a crop with no surface on either side counts as the crop's longest side, and the count of such crops is kept), the hard Dice of the target's mask, the centroid error in millimetres and the share of crops named right. The primary measure is the mean surface distance at the last reading, the plans' measure distance_mm; the others are reported beside it and decide nothing. Each trial also writes its training loss by epoch, the seconds a training crop takes at a steady step (the median step's seconds over a step's mean count of crops, so the few steps that trace and compile a new shape count for nothing), its latents per crop and its saved session. Those seconds are the step's own time on the card: the host's gather of a step's batch and the readings fall outside them, so a wall-time budget is built from environment.json's train_and_read_seconds over the steps, never from the seconds a crop takes alone.

## 4. The hypotheses and their bars

Arms (the screen): lambda_then_w (lambda's ramp, then w's), joint (both over lambda's span), w_first (the spans swapped) and the null (schedule fixed, lambda 1, w 0 and the feature term's weight 0 throughout: the CT branch alone from the first step, trained on the mask and name terms, the privileged channels never seen as anything but the CT), each at seeds 1 and 2, eight trials of 50 epochs. The null takes no feature term because at w 0 the mask encoder takes no gradient and stays at its random start, so the term would pull the CT encoder towards a random network's features, a weight only the null would carry; in the annealed arms the term is the distillation from a mask branch that trained while w was above 0, and it stays to the last step. The mask branch learns only through w, so from the step w reaches 0 it takes no gradient (only the optimiser's fading momentum and its weight decay still move it) and the term distils from a frozen teacher fed the ladder, as KD-Net's frozen four-modality teacher is (docs/research/autoanatomist-search/prior-art.md): the last 15 per cent of lambda_then_w's steps (from step 2,040 of the screen's 2,400) and the last 45 per cent of joint's and w_first's (from step 1,320). In w_first lambda leaves 0 only at step 1,080, when w is already about 0.15, and stands at about 0.15 when w reaches 0, so the sum over the steps of w times lambda's change is 0.004, against 0.50 for joint and 1.00 for lambda_then_w: w_first tests a handover on clean masks followed by distillation from a frozen teacher fed the ladder, not the ladder's order, and its verdict is read as that. For each annealed arm, H: it beats the null. The bar is schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.decide: an arm beats the null when every one of its seeds' final validation distance_mm is below every one of the null's. With two seeds a side, an arm no different from the null passes by chance at most one time in six (both of its two finals below both of the null's, of the six orders of four exchangeable values). That is exact only where the four finals are exchangeable: a seed gives the arm and the null one start and one draw of batches, so where a seed moves both together a chance pass is rarer and the bar has less power too. A pass selects an arm for a longer run under a pre-registration of its own, and is never itself reported as an effect.

The smoke (an anneal and the null, one seed, 3 training CTs and 1 validation CT, 150 epochs, two trials in series on one card, then the join) tests the code path on the grid and times a crop on a GPU; nothing it measures is read as a result, and it answers no hypothesis.

## 5. What counts as failure

A trial that writes no entry, or one that does not hold (schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.entry_problems), is shown failed with its reason and never dropped; an arm with a failed trial on either side is undecided, not passed. A trial whose training diverged fails the same way: it stops with exit 1 at the first step whose loss, or the first reading whose weights or whose outputs on the CT-only path (the logits, the name logits and the latents of every validation crop), are not finite numbers (tools/autoanatomist_trial.py), and entry_problems refuses a reading whose training loss is not a finite number, since a diverged network outlines nothing and every crop would otherwise read the crop's longest side, a finite distance, as though it were a result. The screen is sent only after the smoke's two trials and its join end in exit 0, which the gate holds every send of the screen to (AUTOANATOMIST_AFTER in grid/gate.sh); a smoke whose join reports any failed trial stops the screen until the cause is fixed. A trial whose card JAX does not see stops before it trains (exit 4).

## 6. The analysis plan

The join (tools/autoanatomist_join.py) reads every trial's entry, writes DECISION.json with the rule, each arm's finals and its verdict, the curves of distance_mm at each reading per trial, the finals per arm, and a summary; the flow's end writes RECORD.json and the screen's CLAIM 2024 and REFORMS report (tools/report.py --study autoanatomist-screen). No number is tuned on the validation part beyond the decision above, and no other measure, reading or seed is chosen after the fact.

Known limits, stated before the run:
- The crops are centred on each level's true centroid, so the screen measures outlining and naming given where a vertebra is, never finding it.
- A person is a study: ENHANCE.PET's autoPET part holds 1,014 studies of 900 persons (docs/research/gold3-ct/README.md), and no record matches its studies to persons. The units link probable repeat scans by their anatomy alone, adjacent autoPET ids with vertebral volumes within 3 per cent, or one site's studies whose volumes agree within 5 per cent and whose spacings or rib volumes agree too, so a repeat scan they miss can still sit in training while its twin sits in validation, and the validation figures may then be kinder than a new person's would be.
- One population, ENHANCE.PET, and one network size; nothing is claimed beyond them, and populations stay separate strata.
- Normal spines alone: what the screen finds says nothing of the anomalous spines, which the latent shape priors' evaluation splits by family across its test and training parts (draw_test_reserve.py) for the runs after this one.
- Each reading is a mean over every validation crop, all levels together; the by-level figures are kept in each trial's readings and decide nothing.
- A crop's 64 mm along z, centred on the level's centroid, cuts off part of the target in about a quarter of the screen's crops: in 613 of its 2,640 crops the target reaches more than 31 mm from its centroid along z before the jitter, and in 309 more than 35 mm, mostly below the centroid at T6 to T9 and L1 to L3 (each label's top, bottom and centroid in data/gold3-ct/enhance-pet/facts.jsonl); the cut face counts as surface on both masks, so the measures describe the vertebra inside the crop, the distance reads lower than a whole outline's would, and the arms' differences may be smaller than they are. Every arm reads the same crops, so the decision is not tilted by it; a later pre-registration may widen z to 96 mm.
- A GPU's sums are not the same to the bit from run to run, and the array's trials land on whichever cards are free, so a trial run again ends within run-to-run noise of its first run, not on the same number.
- The feature term runs to the last step, so from the step w reaches 0 it distils from a frozen teacher (section 4); an anneal whose feature term ends with the handover, weighted by w or stopped at w 0, is not tested here, and testing it would take a change to the vendored network, new plans and a pre-registration of its own.

## 8. The second screen

- The code: the vendored package at bordercall 8e966e4 (grid/autoanatomist/VENDORED), which adds four words, each changing nothing where it is not named (grid/autoanatomist/autoanatomist/README.md, "The second screen's arms"), taken at their defaults. A send copies the code at the gate's commit, so a plan sent before this section's commit runs the package at afe1952 and one sent after runs 8e966e4; every arm of the second screen is read against the lambda_then_w trained in the same screen at the same commit, so no reading crosses commits.
- The arms (gold3_latent_screen2): lambda_then_w, the first screen's lambda-then-w and here the arm every other is read against (its null); cyclic, lambda-then-w twice, each pass in half the steps, going back between the passes to lambda 0.5 and w 0.3; adaptive, w on lambda-then-w's clock and lambda moved every 20 steps from its span's start by the error's own slope on the batch just trained, never behind a floor that reaches lambda 1 a tenth of the steps after lambda's span ends; overlapping, lambda-then-w with each rung of the ladder over half of lambda, a quarter apart, so the target's fade, the blur and the threshold move together in places; restraint, lambda-then-w with an adaptive biasing force at weight 1 that holds the target vertebra's predicted volume, as a share of the crop, towards the clean mask's, scaled by (1 - cos 2 pi lambda) / 2, which is 0 where the mask is whole and where it is gone and greatest half way; and ct_only, the first screen's null under a name of its own, the CT branch alone with lambda 1, w 0 and the feature term's weight 0 throughout. Two seeds each, 12 trials of 50 epochs (2,400 steps), on the first screen's rows (its 96 training and 24 validation persons, taken from its plan file), with the same crops, readings and measure.
- The hypotheses: for each of cyclic, adaptive, overlapping and restraint, H: it beats lambda_then_w, by the bar of section 4 with lambda_then_w as the null. ct_only is read against lambda_then_w the same way, and its pass would say the CT branch alone ends below the anneal. Five arms are read against one null, each passing by chance at most one time in six, so a chance pass of some arm is likely: as in section 4, a pass selects an arm for a longer run under a pre-registration of its own and is never reported as an effect, and the screen ranks no arm against another.
- The second smoke (gold3_latent_smoke2): lambda_then_w and the four changes to it, one seed, on the first smoke's three training CTs and one validation CT, 150 epochs (300 steps), five trials in series on one card, then the join. It runs the new words' code on a card and times a crop, answers no hypothesis, and is sent only once the first smoke's report ends in exit 0; the second screen is sent only once the second smoke's report ends in exit 0 (AUTOANATOMIST_AFTER in grid/gate.sh).

Known limits of the second screen, beside section 6's, stated before the run:
- The four words run at their defaults, chosen on phantoms in bordercall's tests and tuned on nothing here.
- Under objective=mask the restraint's variable is the target's volume share alone, since no measure is read.
- The adaptive schedule moves lambda by the batches a seed draws, so its path differs from seed to seed by design.
- The feature term of every annealed arm runs to the last step, as in section 4, so wherever w stands at 0, in cyclic between its passes as at every arm's end, it distils from a mask branch that takes no gradient.
