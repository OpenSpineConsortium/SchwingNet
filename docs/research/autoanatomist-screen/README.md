# The auto-anatomist screen: where it stands

Source: the thread "Restart the auto-anatomist probe", 30 October 2026. Confidential with the rest of this repository.

The screen asks whether annealing the auto-anatomist's privileged input helps the fully latent network name and outline real vertebrae on gold tier 3 CTs, under PREREG.md beside this file, frozen before any send. It runs as the launched flow autoanatomist (flows/autoanatomist.py, flows/README.md): each trial is one run of the flow on a card, and the join is one run on a CPU node that decides each arm against the null and writes the CLAIM 2024 and REFORMS report (tools/report.py with tools/autoanatomist_report.py).

- The plans: grid/screens/gold3_latent_smoke.json, two short trials in series on one card, an anneal and the null, then the join, which tries the code path end to end and whose numbers are read as nothing; and grid/screens/gold3_latent_screen.json, three anneal schedules against the null that never anneals, two seeds each, 50 epochs on 96 training CTs, read on 24 validation CTs by the mean surface distance. tools/autoanatomist_plan.py writes them from the gold 3 store and checks them against the frozen split, the test lists and the units of probable repeat scans, and PREREG.md's Covers line holds each by its bytes.
- The rows: normal ENHANCE.PET spines alone, the store's class for twelve rib pairs whose lowest ribs meet T12 with C2 to L5 named, confirmed by the masks, as the owner asked on 2026-10-30 at 09:43 UTC for the first run; the anomalous spines (four and six lumbar, T13, the rib anomalies) wait for later runs, split by family across test and training by the latent shape priors' evaluation. At 3abc2fa 781 rows are eligible (657 training, 124 validation).
- The split: data/splits/rule.json gives each person its part; no person on data/splits/test-persons.json or on the latent shape priors' reserve, data/splits/latent-prior-test-reserve.json (584 studies), is read, so both studies keep one person split; and every study of a unit of data/splits/latent-prior-repeat-groups.json (85 units) keeps its unit's part, a study whose own part differs left out. Both lists come from that evaluation's draw_test_reserve.py (bordercall PR 45) and reserve-report.json pins their inputs; each run's RECORD.json carries the sha256 of the rule and of the three lists.
- The grid words: `autoanatomist gold3_latent_smoke`, then `autoanatomist gold3_latent_screen` (grid/gate.sh, grid/autoanatomist.sbatch); the gate refuses the screen until the smoke's report ends in exit 0 (AUTOANATOMIST_AFTER). The owner's counts: the smoke is the one short GPU smoke of his count of 2026-10-30 03:20 UTC, and the screen is the two jobs of his word of 08:56 UTC, a GPU array of eight trials and a CPU join afterany it. A plan is sent once; it is sent again only once a fix has moved the checkout after a send that failed, or on his word in AUTOANATOMIST_RERUNS.
- The network: bordercall's auto-anatomist, its copy under grid/autoanatomist/ (tools/bordercall_vendor.py), a block the plans' words choose. The trials run on the grid's Python 3.11, in a venv over the train-gpu environment with the versions schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.PINS names (jax 0.10.2, flax 0.12.8, optax 0.2.8, orbax-checkpoint 0.12.8).
- How to read a result: the join's finals.png and curves.png, each with a line under it on how to read it, and SUMMARY.md, which gives under the verdicts the numbers the two plots draw, each seed's final value and each trial's value at every reading, so the gate's report carries them off the grid where the figures stay; RECORD.json names every file the decision read by its sha256. An arm beats the null only where every one of its seeds ends below every one of the null's; the screen answers which placement of the ramps does best only by which arms pass, and ranks none.

Where it stands on 30 October 2026: the smoke has been sent once, and its card failed it.
