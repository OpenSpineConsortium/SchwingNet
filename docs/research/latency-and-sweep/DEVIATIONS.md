# Deviations from the latency pre-registration

Source: the thread "Require Metaflow for every grid DL job", 2026-10-29. Each entry is dated and says what departs from PREREG.md beside it, or from the plans it restates (docs/research/retraining-plan.md, version 2, section 5; docs/research/demo-plan.md, sections 1 and 2), and why. PREREG.md is never edited; this file only grows.

## 2026-10-29, at the freeze: where the parts as built depart from the plans

1. One stage, not four models. The retraining plan's section 5 times the four models of the reported stack (the two detectors, the encoder, the head) and the whole frame path with `cine_latency.py`. The parts time the level stage's frame path alone with tools/latency.py. Why: version 1 trains the level stage alone over truth boxes (docs/research/v1-demo/DEVIATIONS.md, item 4), and each later stage is exported and gated the same way when it exists (docs/research/latency-and-sweep/README.md, "Not done here").
2. What parity compares. The demo plan's W6 asks for boxes equal, names and abstain flags identical and a posterior difference below 1e-5 on 50 frames. The parts compare each runtime's predicted level and softmax probabilities with PyTorch's on the CPU, on up to 64 validation crops, the validation witness frames' crops first and whole. Why: with truth boxes there is no detector whose boxes could differ, and the abstain flag is the tracker's, computed after the model from its probabilities, so equal levels and probabilities within 1e-5 decide it; the witness frames come first so parity holds on the very frames the evaluation page draws.
3. Where the timed frames come from. The plan's claim is per-frame compute over the sweep. The arms are timed over the catalog's test frames, 300 after 30 of warm-up, and each sweep reports its own latency beside them. Why: every arm is timed on the same frames whichever sweeps exist, and only their pixels and boxes are read, never a label.
4. One sweep, not two. The demo plan's section 1 names case134 and case39. Only case134 is swept. Why: case39 has no poses and so no boxes, and version 1 has no detector to make them; its sweep waits on its poses or on a detector stage (docs/research/latency-and-sweep/README.md).
