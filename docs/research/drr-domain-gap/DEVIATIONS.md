# Deviations from PREREG.md

Source: the thread "Compare synthetic FluXray against real fluoroscopy", 2026-10-27. Every departure of the study from its pre-registration (PREREG.md beside this file), dated, with its reason, in the order it was decided. None was decided after a number of the study had been read.

1. 2026-10-27, 14:47 UTC. The render groups hold 27 CTs, not the 250 the design planned (README section 2). The segmentation job, Slurm job 40799505, ended at exit 0 having run MOOSE on the 27 CTs its plan chose: the plan chooses among the TotalSegmentator v2.0.1 CTs with a SPINEPS mask on their own grid, and 27 had one. Asked whether to send the study on those 27, to run the mask-free groups at 250 first, or to hold for new segmentation jobs, the owner chose "Send it now on the 27 CTs". Each render group's tests therefore stand on 27 persons, so its intervals are wider than planned, and a group under five persons after a render passes CTs over is plotted and never tested, as the design already says.

2. 2026-10-27, 17:51 UTC. The analysis runs as units claimed by the tasks of a Slurm array on the H200 nodes, in place of one process on one GPU job: one prepare job (render, plan, images, features and each reading's head), an array of tasks that claim the units, and one merge job that assembles results.json, the figures and the summary. The owner's word was "cancel and shard requesting h200 / see the beethoven protocol for doing so". No statistic, constant, seed or reading changed: each unit is one contrast, null, structure measure or map, run with the study's seed of 0 from the same head, and tests/test_domain_gap_array.py holds the array's results.json equal to the one-process run's on the same inputs, apart from the run record.

3. 2026-10-27, 17:52 UTC. The study's first send, Slurm job 40802322 (sent 14:48 UTC at commit 38e5533, on a V100 with 4 CPUs), was cancelled on the owner's word after about three hours, while it was still in its analysis. It wrote features and no results.json or summary, and no number of it was read. Its feature files are rewritten by the new prepare job wherever one does not load whole.

4. 2026-10-27. The run now writes a record the design did not ask for, which changes no number: PREREG.md's hash is checked by the first line of every step of the job (exit 90 on a mismatch), every results.json carries a `_run` block (job, task, host, time, commit and whether the tree was clean, the script's and the code's hashes, the arguments, the inputs' hashes, the pre-registration's hash and the seed), the summary opens with five record lines, and PROGRESS.txt says what each stage and task is doing, as the owner asked at 17:53 UTC ("make sure you are logging").

5. 2026-10-27. The mask-free groups, flexray_drr_ct among the primary endpoints and the two FluXray raw groups on segmenter none, were added on the owner's question of 04:15 UTC, before the first send and before any number; README sections 2.1, 2.2 and 4 record them, and PREREG.md includes them, since it restates the design as the first send ran it. They are listed here because they came after the design's first writing of 2026-10-25.

6. 2026-10-27. The Lab Recipe asks that each bar come from a published value or a power calculation. The study's bar of 0.6 on the AUC's lower bound, and the MMD's q of 0.05 for the replication, were fixed by the design on 2026-10-25 as conventions, before any number; neither was taken from a published value or a power calculation, and the report says so beside the words.

7. 2026-10-27. The Lab Recipe's rule 14 asks for a decision gate between a timing run and a production array. No separate timing run is made: the prepare job is the array's own first step and writes the units' list with each unit's cost, so the array's tasks start only once it has ended, and a task that finds no list exits 0. The first array's own progress, read during the run, stands in for the timing run.
