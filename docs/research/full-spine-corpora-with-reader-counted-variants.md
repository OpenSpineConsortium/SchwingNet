# Full-spine corpora with reader-counted numbering variants

Source: the thread "Plan the FleXray audit for #391" of the symphony project, 2026-10-26, answering the owner's question of 2026-10-25 22:31 UTC ("can you find any more full spine corpuses with anomalies that are ground truth?"); searched again from nothing, five sweeps by venue, registry, modality, label schema and the project's own records, each candidate checked by a second reader against its paper's text, three gaps named by a critic and searched once more. Confidential: this note names held and requested datasets and stays in this repository.


## 1. Answer

Fourteen cohorts the records do not hold carry per-case reader counts that span both junctions, none of them open (every one is an e-mail request or a cohort application), and the best are Lodz 2026 (499 trauma CTs: L6 24, T13 13, four-lumbar 11, eleven-thoracic 10), Jeonbuk 2020 (1,340 whole-spine CTs, two readers), the VERIDAH-labelled TUM CT (1,536) and NAKO MRI (6,291), Tatara 2021 (880, counted from C2 on whole-spine films with CT) and Sabri 2025 (3,147 full-length films, 28 with thirteen rib-bearing vertebrae); the only open addition is Hofmann's VertebralBodiesCT-Labels over images already held (69 L6 and 14 T13 flags on 1,460 CTs), of which the records link 70 CTs.

## 3. Public images that would need our own reading

VSDFullBody (Zenodo 8270365, about 104 post-mortem whole-body CTs, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 CH, on the grid's fetch list; the records disagree on whether it landed); Healthy-Total-Body-CTs (TCIA, 30 adults, 135 series, controlled access, tool labels); MSK Database EOS kyphosis set (Tehran, 100 standing full-body EOS under a DUA, 5-case open sample); NAKO (about 30,000 whole-spine MRI by application; VERIDAH's 6,291 labels sit at TUM); UK Biobank Dixon MRI (C2 often absent; fee); SHIP (about 2,500 whole-body MRI); Wakayama (1,011 whole-spine MRI, request); held with tool labels only: TotalSegmentator v3 (142 whole-spine subjects), autoPET, CADS (granted, unfetched). Worth reading because each shows C2 or the last rib and the sacrum together, so a two-reader count makes truth the project controls, at roughly 3 to 10 percent of cases; VSD and TotalSegmentator need no request.

Pending: MrOS, SOF, TVGH-SpineXR, Harms (USD 5,000 a year; image policy unstated), Radiopaedia.
