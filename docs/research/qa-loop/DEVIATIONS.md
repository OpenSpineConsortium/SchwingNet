# The QA loop: deviations from PREREG.md

Source: the local session of 1 November 2026 that fixed the real arm after the loop's first grid runs, on the owner's word of that day ("fix the projection first and get that smoke test done"). PREREG.md is frozen and unchanged; each item below says what the code does that the PREREG did not foresee, and why.

1. C-ArmLSpine's masks are read whole. The PREREG places each case's landmarks on its SPINEPS masks with the oracle. The masks SPINEPS made from the CBCT carry the vertebrae's parts (41 to 53) and the sacrum's (70 to 74) but no spinal canal (61), and the oracle measures the parts about the canal, so with them every level of all 125 cases came back unread: no landmark, no run and no frame to read. The smoke (chain 40903176) and the loop (chain 40903197) of 31 October both stopped after one round on real naming's missing data for that reason, the training itself having converged (the loop's held Dice 0.937, distance 0.44 mm). tools/qaloop_samples.py now leaves out a subregion mask that holds no canal and reads each vertebra whole, as the oracle reads a mask with no parts. Read so, on three cases checked on the grid (job 40935650), L2 to L5 kept 89 to 100 per cent of their landmarks; L1 kept 22 per cent where its body touches the volume's edge, and one L3 lost its canal after the frame turned, both as the oracle flags them.
