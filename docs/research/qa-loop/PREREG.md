## 1. The question

Can one network and one small set of landmarks meet five checkpoints together: the network converges; it decodes every anomalous column class (outlines the class's key vertebra from the CT alone) and encodes it (its name head names the level, and its latent alone tells the class); a set of 3-D landmarks, projected as a C-arm sees them, fingerprints each lumbar level uniquely in one frame; that fingerprint is visible in real fluoroscopy; and the naming it allows runs within the latency budget? The loop (flows/qaloop.py) trains round after round and asks the five, with real naming as a sixth, after each round; a controller changes one thing for each failing checkpoint and goes round again, and the loop ends when every checkpoint is met, when a checkpoint cannot be asked for want of data, when no failing checkpoint has a change left, or when the plan's rounds run out. The answer is the end and its reason; where a checkpoint is out of reach, the record says which cells are beyond what one frame of the landmarks names, which is itself the finding.

## 2. Population and inputs

- The network's data: phantoms of six column classes, typical, lumbar_rib (a rib on L1), no_rib_t12 (no rib on T12), six_lumbar (an L6), four_lumbar (no L5) and t13 (a thirteenth thoracic), drawn by the vendored phantom (grid/autoanatomist/autoanatomist/phantom.py) from schwingcheck's correlated column draw, four levels a row from each class's start level (tools/qaloop_round.py, ROW_START), rendered at 1.25 mm with noise 20 HU and cropped as the auto-anatomist crops (48 by 56 by 32 voxels at 2 mm). The selection's rows and the gate's are drawn from seeds a million apart, so no phantom is in both; the gate's rows stay the same from round to round, since convergence reads their distance.
- The fingerprint's phantoms: each class's persons drawn the same way, each level's eighteen landmarks placed exactly from its drawn shape (the points where the oracle's chords end on a perfect mask, grid/autoanatomist/autoanatomist/specs.py), the column bent into a lordosis scaled by a factor drawn between 0.3 and 1.2, and projected through C-arm poses drawn about the anteroposterior and lateral views (yaw, tilt and roll within 10 degrees, the centre within 20 mm, the source 600 to 700 mm from the centre and 1,100 to 1,200 mm from the detector, 0.6 mm pixels on a 512 frame), with 2 pixels of noise on each projected point. The runs are every stretch of k consecutive bodies wholly in the frame, k from 2 to the plan's k_max, with the bottom anchor (the run ends at the column's last level and a sacrum point 15 mm below its last disc is in the frame) or none. The selection's persons and the gate's are a million seeds apart, and the gate's are drawn anew each round, so a change the controller made from one round's answers is judged on persons it was not chosen on.
- The real pairs: C-ArmLSpine (Zenodo 22768651, CC BY 4.0, class open in data/licences.json), each case's CBCT with its spot frames and the poses registered to them (pose_150/ where it exists, else pose/), under the data root's carmlspine folder. A case is read where both labellers of carmlspine/levels.json agree on at least two levels and flag no variant; its SPINEPS masks are read by the oracle on those levels, the landmarks taken to the scanner's frame and projected through each frame's pose, and the frame itself read as attenuation for the presence gate. The persons on data/splits/test-persons.json (the three L6 persons and the two demo sweeps) are never read; the rest are dealt by data/splits/rule.json (salt schwingnet-2026-10-21, validation share 0.1): validation persons are the gates', training persons join the selection's phantoms. No frame's file name, no header value and no person leaves the grid; the record holds counts, means and intervals.
- The smoke reads the real pairs where the data root holds them and three stand-ins elsewhere: typical phantoms rendered and read by the oracle, a frame splatted from their bone through a pose, and the pose perturbed by up to a degree and three millimetres as a registration's error. Every result asked of a stand-in says so, and its numbers say nothing of real fluoroscopy.

## 3. The checkpoints and their bars

Each round asks every gate the plan names; the bars are the plans' and the same in both.

- Convergence: the mean training loss over the last 200 steps within 0.02 of the mean over the 200 before, relatively, and this round's held-out mean surface distance (the gate's crops, from the CT alone) no more than 0.05 above the best earlier round's.
- Anomaly, decoded and encoded: on the gate's rows, for each class its key level's crops (six_lumbar L6, four_lumbar L4, t13 T13, lumbar_rib L1, no_rib_t12 T12; every crop of a typical row as normal), the mean hard Dice of the CT-only mask at or above 0.80, the name head's share named right at or above 0.70, and the recall of a nearest-class-mean probe on the latent alone, fitted on the selection's crops, at or above 0.60; a measure that is not a number fails.
- Fingerprint: the landmarks chosen by a greedy forward search from the pool (the eighteen less those the presence floor drops), the best pair first and one landmark at a time while the objective gains at least 0.005, up to the configuration's number; the objective is the mean over the two arms, phantom and real, of the mean cell accuracy under three-fold person-wise cross-validation on the selection's runs. The namer, per view, describes each body of a run by its chosen landmarks in the run's own frame scaled by the run's size, its log size and its gap to the next body, and scores every hypothesis (every placement of k consecutive levels in every column type, the bottom anchor fixing the last) by a Gaussian per view, type and level with a covariance pooled over the view and shrunk 0.2 toward its diagonal, each type weighted by its prior (typical 0.9, six_lumbar, four_lumbar and t13 0.05 each; lumbar_rib and no_rib_t12 are named as typical, since no landmark here sees a rib). Judged on the gate's runs, each cell (view, anchor, column type) passes where the Wilson 95 per cent lower bound of its accuracy is at or above 0.90 and the lower median of its runs' margins, the log odds of the true names over the best wrong ones, is at or above log 9 (2.1972). The ceiling is the same namer on every landmark of the pool, fitted and judged alike; a failing cell is beyond what one frame of the pool's landmarks names, at the selection's size, only where the upper Wilson bounds of its own accuracy and of its ceiling's are both below 0.90, so a cell that fails for want of runs is never called beyond.
- Presence: on the validation persons' real frames, each landmark of the fingerprint projected through the frame's pose and its edge strength (the gradient of the attenuation image smoothed at 1.5 pixels, the largest within 8 pixels) read as a percentile against 200 points drawn inside the same body's projected outline; a frame shows the fingerprint where its landmarks' mean percentile is at or above 0.6, and the gate passes where the Wilson lower bound of the share of such frames is at or above 0.6.
- Real naming: the fingerprint's namer on the validation persons' runs from the real frames (typical columns, no anchor), each view's cell held to the fingerprint's bars.
- Latency: the naming path, one run at a time (its features and every hypothesis's score against tables stacked before the clock starts), timed over up to the plan's latency_runs runs spread evenly over the gate's lengths, views and anchors; the 95th percentile at or below 97 ms on the node's CPU.

## 4. The controller

After each round the controller (tools/qaloop_gates.py, controller) reads the round's answers. Every gate passing ends the loop met; a gate without its data ends it stopped; the plan's last round ends it not met. Otherwise each failing gate makes one change, where it has one left: convergence trains on; anomaly, once training has converged, doubles the phantoms a class, as many as the host's memory holds, measured each round (the loop plan sets no other limit on them, the smoke a small one), then doubles the name head's weight; the fingerprint, where a failing cell is within reach, adds two landmarks, then, with every landmark of its range in, doubles its selection's persons a type, as many as the memory holds, measured the same way (the loop plan again sets no other limit); where every failing cell is beyond the ceiling it doubles the persons the same way, since the ceiling is measured at the selection's size and more persons fit the namer better, and only once the memory holds no more has it no change, the cells beyond one frame named as the finding; presence raises the floor a landmark's mean percentile on the selection's frames must reach to stay in the pool by 0.1; real naming adds two landmarks where its failing cells are within the real ceiling's reach and the fingerprint added none; latency takes two landmarks away. The name head's weight, the number of landmarks and the presence floor move within the ranges the plans give (in the loop plan up to 1.6, the whole pool of eighteen and 0.5), which are the space the controller searches and not a resource. A round with no change left ends the loop not met, the gates left with none said in words. Each round's space is measured as it runs (the bytes on disk and the peak memory of each block per unit: a pair's case, a phantom row, a fingerprint person, a round) and sets the next round's processes, the most rows and the most fingerprint persons a type it may hold, so neither the disk nor the memory fills; nothing else limits the loop.

## 5. What counts as failure, and the verdict

A round whose tool fails is run once more in its task; a second failure ends the loop failed with its reason, and end still writes the record. A run cut short by a time limit or a preemption is resumed by the next job of the same words at the round it was cut in. The run's verdict is 0 or 1: a loop plan ends 0 only where every gate was met and the report holds; the smoke ends 0 where the loop decided (met, or not met with its reason), every gate it asks had its data in every round, it went round at least twice unless the first round met every gate, and the report holds. The smoke's numbers answer no question.

## 6. The analysis plan

The flow's end writes RECORD.json (every round in words, the last decision, the files it read by their sha256), SUMMARY.md and the CLAIM 2024 and REFORMS report (tools/report.py --study qa-loop). No bar, prior or measure is changed after the freeze; the only things that change between rounds are those section 4 names.

Known limits, stated before the run:
- The phantoms' L6 is drawn as an L5 and a four-lumbar column simply lacks one, so a run that shows neither the thoracic levels above nor the count from the sacrum cannot tell those columns from a typical one, and their bottom-anchored cells may be beyond one frame.
- A run of two or more bodies is named; a single body in the frame is not.
- No landmark detector exists yet: the landmarks are the oracle's, placed on masks and projected, so the fingerprint says which landmarks would name the levels, not that a network finds them in a frame, and the latency is the naming path's alone, no network's.
- The phantoms carry no sacrum: the bottom anchor is a point 15 mm below the last disc.
- The real arm is typical columns alone, read where the two labellers agree; the L6 persons are test persons and a flagged variant is left out, so real frames say nothing of the anomalous classes.
- The poses registered to the real frames fit them imperfectly, so a landmark projected through a pose may sit off its true place on the frame, which lowers presence and real naming against what a perfect registration would give.
- A frame mirrored against its view's usual side is read as it is stored.
- The real gate's persons are the same in every round and the controller changes the loop from their answers, so the real gates are asked of persons the loop was steered by; only the test persons, never read here, would be a test.
- Each Wilson interval counts runs, and runs of one person are not independent; the persons are counted beside each cell.
- A row's crops end at its last level, so a crop's place in its row is a cue the network may use.
- The 2 pixels of noise on each projected landmark and the lordosis scale of 0.3 to 1.2 are choices, set before any run and tuned on nothing.
- The smoke runs on stand-ins where no C-ArmLSpine is at hand, phantoms rendered as frames, which say nothing of real fluoroscopy.
