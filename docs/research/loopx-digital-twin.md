# The Loop-X digital-twin cadaver: opened, posed and named

Source: the local session of 1 November 2026, on the owner's ask of that night for paired CT and fluoroscopy of the whole spine, opening the held zip of spine-datasets #170: Killeen and others, "Intelligent Control of Robotic X-ray Devices using a Language-promptable Digital Twin" (IPCAI 2025, arXiv 2412.08020), whose data the README of github.com/benjamindkilleen/IPCAI-language-digital-twin links (that repository holds a README and a licence and no code). Confidential with the rest of this repository. Every number below comes from a grid job's output under /rs/rs_grp_oschome/go2432/data/realxray_work/twin/ (export.json, pose.json, levels.json, ts/counts.json) or realx/twin/manifest.json; no image left the grid but the contact sheet beside this note.

## What the set holds

- The zip, 202410191.zip, 844,896,590 bytes, unpacked beside itself by tools/unpack.py under the size rule (job 40940277: 1,748 files, 1.4 GB, admitted against 12,770 GB of room). There is no README, no pose file and no mask in it: 1,748 DICOM files of one study, 2024-10-19, from a medPhoton ImagingRing (the Brainlab Loop-X).
- Four cone-beam CTs, 416 axial slices of 674 x 478 at 0.6136 mm each (413.6 x 293.3 x 255.3 mm), HU by the header's slope and intercept, taken one after another as the gantry stepped along the body. Together, once stitched (below), they run from both femurs through the pelvis, the lumbar and the whole thoracic column: 812 x 299 x 429 voxels at 1 mm.
- 84 X-ray files (DX, 16 bits, 0.3 mm imager pixels, about 1,385 pixels square at full field, smaller where collimated): two are the same pixels as another, and two are the CBCT's scouts, which leaves the README's 80 single shots. (30BB,2070) calls 34 of them PA, 42 oblique and 6 lateral. The projection matrices give a focal length of 1,246 mm at the median and the source 746 to 749 mm from the isocentre (export.json, decomposed).
- The geometry is in the private group 30BB, "Navigation Registration 1.0", which nothing in the release documents: per X-ray a 4 x 4 at (30BB,2073) whose rows 1, 2 and 4 are a projection matrix to millimetres on the image, a rigid 4 x 4 at (30BB,2055) to the image from the fixed patient array, the gantry's source and detector angles, and the gantry tilt at (30BB,2088); per CBCT the same rigid 4 x 4. Eight X-rays carry no (30BB,2055), so they were never registered to the array.

## How a frame's pose is read

The paper says only that every image was "acquired with navigation relative to a fixed patient array" and registered to the four CTs. Reading the tags took three findings, each tested on the images:

1. (30BB,2073) projects from the device's own DICOM frame, its origin the ring's isocentre: three X-rays (x080, x081, x082) were taken with the device within half a millimetre and half a degree of where a CBCT was taken, so their navigations cancel, and that CBCT's bone through the matrix lands on the X-ray's bone (edge NCC 0.29, 0.48 and 0.60 at the pose, the best shift 0 to 0.7 mm at the bone).
2. (30BB,2055) maps the patient array's frame to the image's navigation frame, which is not DICOM's: it is the reconstruction's voxel frame in millimetres, from the first voxel of instance 0 (the top slice), its third axis running against DICOM's z. Read as DICOM directly, the four CBCTs stack in the wrong order (the thorax below the pelvis) and their overlaps disagree (NCC 0.35, 0.37 and -0.002); read through the voxel frame they stack femurs, pelvis, lumbar, thorax, and each overlap of neighbours agrees at the navigation's alignment, NCC 0.67, 0.80 and 0.68, the best shift within 5 mm being 0 to 3 mm (tools/loopx_twin.py pose, the stitch's overlap check). Of the axis signs and origins tried on the untilted X-rays, none scores higher (mean edge NCC 0.235; 0.04 to 0.235 for the rest).
3. The composed pose, the device's projection over the world-to-device motion the two navigations give, holds for untilted shots and fails for tilted ones. Of the 67 X-rays that could be scored, the 21 untilted ones (gantry tilt under 1 degree) show a median edge NCC of 0.291 at the claimed pose, the best of the image shifts lying 2.62 mm from it at the median and at most 5.83 mm, at the bone; the 46 tilted ones (2.2 to 22.3 degrees) show 0.032, the best shift 5.3 to 28.4 mm away. Adding or removing the tilt about each device axis through the isocentre, or moving the navigation frame's origin, did not rescue them (mean NCC on the tilted frames 0.00 to 0.05); a tilt about a pivot searched on a 150 mm grid raised nine tilted frames from 0.02 to 0.15, which is a fit after the fact, not a reading of the device, and is not used.

The test: the stitched CBCT's bone (over 200 HU) splatted through each frame's composed pose, the gradient magnitudes of that and of the real frame's attenuation compared by NCC within the shutter near the bone, against the same NCC with the bone shifted by every whole step up to 96 pixels each way (the null). A frame's pose holds when the gantry is untilted, the NCC at the pose is at least 0.1 and at least the null's 95th percentile, and the best shift moves the bone by 5 mm or less. These bars were set after the scores were seen, so they describe this set and test nothing.

17 of the 84 hold (x000, x001, x002, x003, x004, x006, x009, x010, x015, x016, x018, x025, x026, x046, x080, x081, x082): the median NCC 0.302 at the pose against a null median of 0.105 and 95th percentile of 0.180, the best shift 2.62 mm from the pose at the median and 3.82 at most. The rest: 49 tilted, 8 never registered, 4 collimated frames too small to score, 4 untilted frames whose edges do not match (x005 and three laterals: x024 and x027 share the gantry angles of the two small laterals that hold but are full-field, x045 is at another angle; why they do not match is not known), 2 duplicates. The contact sheet, loopx-digital-twin.png, shows six frames that hold and six that do not, the DRR's strongest bone edges in red over each.

## Which levels each frame shows

TotalSegmentator 2.18.0 (one GPU job, 40940591, on the stitched CBCT) drew T1 to T12, L1 to L5, S1 and the sacrum, and no cervical level. The README says the specimen runs to T2; TotalSegmentator names its top vertebra T1, smaller than the rest (14,233 voxels against T2's 19,985), so the top of the column is cut and the names rest on TotalSegmentator alone. A second reading that owes it nothing, the bone beside each level past its transverse processes, finds 841 to 6,899 voxels a side beside T1 to T11, 398 and 484 beside T12, 112 and 115 beside L1, none to 5 beside L2 and L3, 336 and 2 beside L4 and 4,031 and 433 beside L5, where the iliac crest lies: ribs on every level it calls thoracic and none below L1, which agrees with its names but cannot by itself tell a short twelfth rib from a rib on a lumbar level.

Projected through the poses that hold (a level is seen where half its voxels fall inside the shutter): the eight full-field frames of the thorax show T1 to T12 and x001 T4 to T10; x015 and x018 show T9 to L4; x016 T9 to T12; x046 L2 to the sacrum; x081 T11 to L5; x082 L4 to the sacrum; the two small laterals no whole level. Of the lumbosacral column, then, four untilted PA frames with poses that hold show two or more of T12 to L5: x015, x046, x081 and x082. The tilted frames include many more lumbar and sacral views (levels.json lists them), which would be the set's real value once their poses are recovered.

## The arm built from it

tools/realx_pairs.py twin builds the set into realx/twin, in the pair format of the other external arms: one person, loopx_twin:202410191, a cadaver, gate-only with nothing to hold out; levels T12 to L5 (the typical column TotalSegmentator drew), 106 of their 108 oracle landmarks read, the sacral anchor from its sacrum; and the four frames above, all ap_real (manifest.json: 49 frames left out as tilted, 8 unregistered, 7 showing fewer than two of the arm's levels, 5 oblique, 4 below the edge bars, 4 too small, 2 duplicates, 1 not square). Its check sheet (realx_pairs.py check) shows the landmarks along the lumbar column of x015 and x081. It joins no loop: an arm enters the QA loop only under a new PREREG, as docs/research/qa-loop-external/README.md says.
