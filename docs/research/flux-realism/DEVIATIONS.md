# Deviations from FleXray's recipe and from PREREG.md

Source: the thread "Reproduce the FluXray dataset", 2026-10-29. The first heading lists where the reproduction cannot do what the FleXray paper (arXiv 2609.26756v2) did, each fixed in PREREG.md beside this file before any image was rendered and before any number was read, with its reason. The second lists every departure of the run from PREREG.md after its freeze, dated, in the order it was decided; a departure decided after a number was read says so.

## From the paper's recipe, fixed at the freeze

1. The filter's segmenter. The paper scores each edit with a U-Net trained on raw DRRs alone, which was not released. FleXray's member flux000, trained on no enhanced DRRs but on real X-rays too, is the nearest released stand-in and scores the paired filter here (README section 2). The share of edits it drops is reported against the paper's about 4 percent, and a very different share says the stand-in reads differently, so the kept set is not FluXray's.
2. The seeds. The paper gives none. Each edit's seed is schwingnet.realism.seed_for(subject, grid pose, arm), a digest, so a rerun draws the same noise; two arms are run so that what the noise alone moves is measured and a word that changes with the arm is not reported as a result.
3. The scale. FluXray holds 138,063 images; the reproduction draws 100 subjects and 4 poses each by a recorded seed, 400 samples and 800 edits, the plan's figure of 2026-10-26 (README section 7). So each pose group of the filter holds about 9 edits in place of about 1,534, and its q0.98 cut-off is noisier; the dropped share is read as description only.
4. The size compared. The paper edited at 1024 and stored FluXray at 256; the reproduction edits at 1024 and compares at 256, since nothing larger of FluXray exists.
5. The bars. The Lab Recipe asks that each bar come from a published value or a power calculation. The AUC lower bound of 0.6 and the MMD q of 0.05 are conventions this programme fixed on 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-26 and shares with the DRR domain-gap study; the geometry's 1 pixel is one pixel of FleXray's canvas; and 100 subjects is not a powered size. All were fixed before any number, and the report says so beside them.
6. Not in this count. The owner's count of 2026-10-29 02:35 UTC is three fetches and two GPU jobs, the edit and the statistics. No comparison with real X-rays or fluoroscopy is made (the DRR domain-gap study makes it), and the adapter's stages, lora and eval, are refused by the flow until they have a count of their own and are ported to the pipeline.

## From PREREG.md, after the freeze

All three were decided on 2026-10-29 at the adversarial review of draft pull request #456, before any image was rendered and before any number of the reproduction was read. PREREG.md and its sha256 stay as frozen; the code carries each departure, the statistics record it beside their rules (stats.json), and the report quotes this file where it restates the design.

1. The permutations are by sample, not by subject. Section 3 froze the classifier test's and the MMD's permutation p with "labels shuffled within each subject both sets hold". Every comparison of this design is paired: the kept edits and the raw renders each show the same 400 samples as FluXray's images, and the two seed arms the same 400 renders. Each subject carries 4 samples at different heights, offsets and rotations, so a shuffle within the subject moves a label from one pose's image to another pose's, and the null it draws is far wider than the pairing allows: the MMD's q would then almost never fall below 0.05, and its veto, which section 4 gives it, could never act. Where the two sets share samples, the labels are shuffled only within each sample's own images (both arms of an edit with FluXray's image of that sample, or a render with FluXray's), and a sample in only one set keeps its label. The k-nearest-neighbour score still leaves out every image of the image's own subject, and the split-half null, whose halves share no subject and no sample, still moves whole subjects.
2. FD, KID and the MMD run on min(64, the space's dimension) principal components. Section 3 says 64 components in every space, but the physics space has 57 features, so its 64 components are all 57; the flexray and imagenet spaces have more than 64 and use 64. No more components are taken than images pooled, which at 400 samples a set changes nothing. The number each comparison used is recorded beside it as "components", and the tables print it; the keys keep their names, frechet64 and kid64.
3. A subject is eligible only where 4 of its samples are in the ThunderDB at a pose on the paper's grid. Section 2 makes a subject eligible with at least one such sample and then draws 4 of its eligible samples, which a subject with fewer cannot give; the code took fewer without a word. The eligibility count, the 100-subject refusal before any GPU work, the draw and the record now count the same set, and a drawn subject with fewer than 4 refuses the run.

Decided on 2026-10-29 after the first edit job, 40865836, stopped at its input check before any GPU work, and the gate queued it once more as 40867482, which stopped the same way; PREREG.md and its sha256 stay as frozen, and no number the PREREG names changes.

4. Three packages installed beside the frozen pins, and the edit on an L40S card. flexray==1.0.2, a frozen pin, imports thunderpack in its ThunderDB reader, nanodrr in its renderer and kornia in its camera labels, but lists them only under its train extra, so the pins alone could not read FluXray. thunderpack==0.0.2, nanodrr==0.1.5 and kornia==0.9.0, the releases PyPI held on 2026-10-29, are installed beside the pins (grid/realism.sbatch, REALISM_EXTRA) and held by the tool as the pins are (schwingnet.realism.EXTRA_PINS), outside the frozen block, whose bytes PREREG.md froze. Every name the stages import from FleXray and diffusers is now tried at each stage's start (schwingnet.realism.FLEXRAY_IMPORTS), which found two more breaks the first job had not reached, the segmenter's import, which flexray 1.0.2 keeps in fxr.inference, and the read of FleXray's MOOSE config, a folder with no __init__.py read as a resource of fxr.configs; both are fixed, and the whole list imports in an environment of the pins and the three beside them over train-cpu's torch. The first job also ran on a V100 of 16 GB, which neither holds klein-9B in bfloat16 nor computes bfloat16 natively, so the edit asks the gpu class for galhp's L40S cards and refuses at its start a card under 42 GiB or without bfloat16, keeping section 2's bfloat16; the stats stage takes any card.

Decided on 2026-10-29 after a cross-check of the tool against FleXray 1.0.2's own code, before any image was rendered and before any number of the reproduction was read; PREREG.md and its sha256 stay as frozen.

5. Three readings brought to FleXray's own. The soft Dice: section 4 froze "the channels with any truth and a background channel", and the tool had read the background's probability as one minus the largest foreground probability and used no smoothing; FleXray's segmenter carries its own background channel, channel 0 of the 61 it names, and its soft_dice_score squares the denominator, adds 1e-7 to numerator and denominator and averages over the channels whose truth is not empty, so the tool now scores the model's channel 0 against what no other channel claims, with that formula, and the stats refuse a segmenter whose first channel is not named background. The projected masks: FleXray's renderer takes a hard mask where a channel's projected mass passes a threshold, 0.5 by its request's default, while FleXray's own training renders them at 0.0, its camera runtime's default, which the MOOSE config leaves alone; the raw renders and their masks are made at 0.0, as FluXray's masks were. The subject: FleXray's reader puts the sample's own key in a record's subject where the metadata names none, so a database with no subject would have read as one subject per sample; the tool reads the subject from the record's metadata by the reader's own keys (subject_id, subject, subject_key) and a record naming none counts as no subject, which the eligibility count then refuses. The organ and cardiac truth of ENHANCE.PET is not read: section 2 froze three truth folders (Vertebrae, Ribs, Peripheral-Bones), so the segmenter's organ channels have empty truth here and the soft Dice leaves them out, as FleXray's ignore_empty_labels does.
