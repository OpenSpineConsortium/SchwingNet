A single-member, member-managed Michigan limited liability company, formed under the Michigan Limited Liability Company Act, 1993 PA 23, MCL 450.4101 et seq.

## 1. Formation
1.1 Name. The Company is [OpenSpine Consortium LLC].
1.2 Formation. The Company was formed when its Articles of Organization were filed with the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs on [date].
1.3 Registered office and agent. As stated in the Articles, as changed from time to time.
1.4 Term. Perpetual, unless dissolved under Article 9.

## 2. Purpose
The Company may develop, license and sell software, medical imaging and other technology; take licences of technology owned by universities and others; provide related services; and engage in any lawful activity for which an LLC may be formed in Michigan.

## 3. Member
3.1 The sole Member is Gregory Schwing, holding 100% of the membership interests.
3.2 Initial contribution: $[amount] in cash and the property listed in Schedule A, assigned by a separate written assignment.
3.3 No Member is required to make further contributions.

## 4. Management
4.1 The Company is managed by its Member, who has full authority to act for it.
4.2 The Member may appoint officers or agents and delegate authority to them in writing.

## 5. Intellectual property
5.1 The Company owns only the intellectual property assigned or licensed to it in writing.
5.2 Nothing in this Agreement assigns, or obliges the Member to assign, any right that Wayne State University holds or may hold under its Patent and Copyright Policy (Statute 2.41.04) or any agreement the Member signed with it. Any such right comes to the Company only by a written licence, waiver or assignment from the University.

## 6. Liability and indemnity
6.1 The Member is not liable for the Company's debts, obligations or liabilities solely by being its Member.
6.2 The Company indemnifies the Member against losses arising from acting for the Company in good faith, except for fraud, wilful misconduct or a knowing violation of law.

## 7. Money
7.1 The Company keeps its own bank accounts and books, separate from the Member's.
7.2 Distributions are made when the Member decides, subject to MCL 450.4307.
7.3 Tax: while it has one member, the Company is disregarded for federal income tax unless the Member elects otherwise.

## 8. Transfers and new members
8.1 The Member may transfer any part of the membership interest.
8.2 A new member is admitted only by a written amendment signed by the Member. Before a second member joins, this Agreement is replaced by a multi-member agreement (voting, buy-sell, vesting, IP assignment by the new member).

## 9. Dissolution
9.1 The Company dissolves on the Member's written decision or as the Act requires.
9.2 On the Member's death or incapacity, [the Member's estate or designated successor, name] may continue the Company.

## 10. General
10.1 Michigan law governs.
10.2 Amendments are in writing signed by the Member.
