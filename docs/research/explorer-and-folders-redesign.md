# The Explore and Folders tabs redesigned

Source: the thread "Explorer modernization", 2026-10-20, from handoff part 6.2 (docs/checkpoints/2026-10-19/handoff.md) and the owner's word of 03:50 UTC in the OneDrive thread, relayed by the coordinator. The code is in OpenSpineConsortium/openspineconsortium.github.io (Explore merged in #98, Folders in #99) and its checks in OpenSpineConsortium/symphony-app (signin/panel-checks/e2e2; #50 and #51).

## Which explorer

The handoff's "Explorer" was read first as the Explore tab, the goal to epic to issue to pull request to run, job, step and log tree. The owner then said he meant the Folders tab: a native Windows File Explorer view of his OneDrive through direct Microsoft Graph, with the right side panel removed and the old connector-made scan never offered. The coordinator made Folders the main work and kept the Explore changes because they were already built.

## Explore: what changed

- Cards on the zoom stage the Dependencies tab uses (stageMake): no box scrolls; the wheel, a pinch, + and - zoom; zooming out past the whole level comes out one level, once a wheel gesture so a trackpad's glide stops there; a swipe or an arrow on the stage goes to the level beside; with a card in hand the arrows move between cards, zoomed or not, and a key held down steps sideways once, since each level below a task is a read.
- A level's cards are laid out in the column count, from 1 to 24, that draws them largest, and grown to fill the stage.
- A log is drawn at its own size on its first error, else its end; a plain wheel moves along it whether it fits or not; its width follows the stage; its lines copy from a button, since a drag pans.
- While the tab is shown the side column folds to its icons and the foot to one line; Panel, or a press on either, brings them back; on a phone the tab is the whole screen. The fold applies only once the signed-in app is shown, so the sign-in page at #explore still scrolls on a phone.
- The read budget is kept: reads below a task happen only on entry, a double click goes one level and not two, and none starts under the reserve of 100 calls.
- Checks: explore.test.mjs XP1 to XP19 pass; the mutants x01 to x40 are killed. An adversarial review of the first commit found ten faults (the fold locking a signed-out phone, a short log left by the wheel, a double click reading two levels, held keys reading every level passed, arrows panning while zoomed, a log clipped after Panel, log text not copyable, word-only levels with no zoom-out, gestures surviving a level, Panel's pressed state inverted); each is fixed and the first five that a check can reach are held by XP16 to XP19.

## Folders: what changed

- The tab is one column: a one-line head (Folders, who is connected, Connect OneDrive, Track in a repository, How it works) over the explorer, which fills the rest of the tab; only its tree and its list scroll inside, as Windows' panes do.
- The right side column is gone. Its steps (what is ticked, Make the repository, Track) are in a dialog, Track in a repository, with every element id kept, so the tracking code is unchanged.
- The saved catalog (catalog.json on a private repository's drive-catalog branch, written by the drive organizer through the connector) is no longer read or shown by the tab, and no text offers it; with no drive the explorer says only to connect OneDrive. The Notebooks tab still reads catalog.json, which is that tab's design and the sign-in thread's.
- No new read or write: the layout change needs no privacy statement change.
- An adversarial review found that the modal dialog hid Track's answers at the foot, that words still pointed at "this tab" and "step 1", that the panes grew without bound where the page scrolls, and that what is ticked in other folders could no longer be seen; Track's answers now show in a status line in the dialog too, Connect first closes the dialog, the words name Track in a repository, the explorer keeps a height of its own on a phone, and the head's button counts the ticks. It also found that the Apps' privacy statement still lists the catalog read the tab no longer makes, which over-states and leaks nothing; that was passed to the thread that writes the statement, for version 36.
- Checks: folders.test.mjs drops CAT1 to CAT9 and their mutants f56 to f63, and adds NC1 (no catalog read or word), NC2 (no side column, the explorer fills the tab, the page does not scroll), NC3 (the Track dialog), NC4 (Track's answers in the dialog, the ticks counted) and NC5 (bounded panes on a phone); all 59 pass, and the 61 mutants f01 to f69 that remain are killed.

## Folders: Explorer's own verbs

The owner answered the list of twelve with "do all" (2026-10-20, 06:49 UTC). All twelve are built (site claude/folders-actions, checks in symphony-app's folders.test.mjs FA1 to FA17) and ship with privacy statement 36, which the sign-in thread wrote and checked against the code before the merge.

- What writes, each only on the person's press, through Graph as the drive's owner with the Files.ReadWrite access the connection already had: New folder (named "New folder" and opened for its name, as Explorer does), Rename (F2, in place, the extension left out of the choice, OneDrive's forbidden characters and reserved names refused before any call), Delete (an in-page confirmation, then one DELETE per item, which OneDrive moves to its recycle bin), Upload (a button, or files dropped on the list, a folder's row or the tree; up to 4 MB in one PUT, larger through an upload session in 3.2 MB pieces sent with no token; a name already there asks Replace, Keep both or Skip), Cut, Copy and Paste (a move is one PATCH of the parent, a copy OneDrive's own background copy; dragging items onto a folder moves them; a folder is never pasted into itself or a folder inside it) and Share (a link made only on Create link, its kind chosen first; a personal drive offers only anyone with the link, a work drive its organization first).
- What reads: Download (the file's one-time download address, which the browser follows with no token; a folder downloads from OneDrive's page), Properties (one item's record, including who made and changed it; several items are summed from the list), the preview pane (off until Alt+P; Graph's preview page in a sandboxed frame, else the large thumbnail), search of the whole drive (Enter in the box; typing still narrows the folder shown), thumbnails with the list, and six views (Large icons, Small icons, List, Details, Tiles, Content) with multi-select by Ctrl and Shift.
- Kept in the page's memory alone, forgotten on reload: the view, sort, preview switch, selection, what is cut or copied, the search words and the previews' addresses.
- The adversarial review found twelve faults: the folder before drawn and actionable while the next one read, or after its read failed; a preview stuck on a second click; the phone's preview covering the list; keys on a heading or tick acting on the selection; a rename lost when the list was read again under it; a moved item left cut; writes from search results leaving the real folder's list stale; a folder pasted into its own subfolder; no guard on a double paste; a menu removed under a later click; shortcuts a browser keeps; and dialog quirks. Each is fixed, and FA14 to FA17 hold the first five.
- Not measured: Graph's preview page on a personal OneDrive, which may not exist there; the pane then shows the thumbnail.

## Folders: the start as cloud tiles

The owner wrote, relayed by the coordinator on 2026-10-20, that the start should be "icon based cloud synchronization", a "+ add" dashed tile opening a dialog with one-click sign-in to OneDrive first and other drives later; that linking made him make a new repository; and that Track's dialog did not close. Built on site claude/folders-start (site #103, merged 2026-10-20) and held by symphony-app's folders.test.mjs FS1 to FS6 (symphony-app #53).

- With no drive the explorer shows one dashed tile, "+ Add a cloud drive", which opens a dialog of tiles: OneDrive, one press to Microsoft's sign-in, and Google Drive and Dropbox shown as coming later. Once a drive is connected the head keeps a small Add button and names the connected account beside a cloud.
- Track uses the repository chosen in the bar; making a new one is a closed fold, "Need a new repository?", in the dialog. A repository that only walks the drive (it has drive-catalog.yml and no drive-sync.yml, as OpenSpineConsortium/onedrive does) is tracked with the secret alone.
- In an organization the drive's token is kept as one organization secret with visibility "selected", shared only with the repositories that track that drive, unless the repository has a secret of its own, the organization's key cannot be read, another drive already holds the secret (told by the variable SYMPHONY_DRIVE_ID), or a private repository is on the free plan, where an organization secret does not reach it; a refused write falls back to the repository's own secret.
- Track switches the half-hourly sync on with the repository variable SYMPHONY_DRIVE=on, between writing the file and starting the first run, and the dialog closes by itself once Track is done; it stays open only when something went wrong, with what to do.
- These writes are only on the person's Track press and are in the Apps' privacy statement 39 (symphony-start#86), which was live before the merge.
- Not built, by the owner's or the review's word: switching the sync on when the tab opens (a decision asked of the owner, which would need a later statement), an Untrack, and taking a repository off the organization secret's list, which no statement covers yet.
- Checks: FS1 to FS6 pass with the rest of the suite (82 checks), and the mutants f109 to f119 are killed (110 Folders mutants in all).

## Folders: the connection kept, and Track for a whole account or organization

The owner tapped "Yes, automatically" on 2026-10-20 to switching the half-hourly sync on when the tab opens a repository that already tracks a drive: built as site #108 with checks FS7 (symphony-app #55); a variable set to off is never turned back on. The same day OpenSpineConsortium/onedrive showed the secret empty after Track; Track now reads the repository's own list of organization secrets after writing the organization's, and saves a repository secret where GitHub withholds it (site #110, FS8 in symphony-app #56).

The owner then wrote, relayed on 2026-10-20: "if I authorize over depository to use my OneDrive next time I login it's no longer authorized ... I want to be able to authorize for an entire get hub account or even an entire GitHub organization", and chose "Both" on the card that followed.

- Cause: the page held the drive's tokens in its memory alone, and dropped them after Track, on a reload, on any sign-out and when a sign-in ran out. Nothing at Microsoft was lost.
- Now the page keeps the refresh token on the browser (localStorage, key symphony_panel_drive_kept), by GitHub login, with the day it was issued and the drive's name and id; never the access token. A reload, a sign-in that ran out or a new sign-in as the same account finds it; another account does not. It ends with Forget OneDrive (beside the connection's name), Forget the token, Microsoft refusing it (invalid_grant) or its 90 days. Track no longer drops it, so Track in any other repository needs no Connect, and dates drive.json by the day the connection was made. A page never writes its token over a newer one another tab kept, nor forgets that one when Microsoft refuses its own; Forget the token forgets every connection kept on the browser.
- The sign-in service's /drive/token returns no rotated refresh token, so the kept one lasts its 90 days from Connect, as the repositories' secrets do; keeping the rotated one would need the service to pass it on.
- An organization's secret is now written with visibility private, so every private and internal repository of the organization reads it and one Track covers the organization; public repositories and private ones on GitHub Free keep a secret of their own. Anyone who may run a workflow in any private repository of the organization can then reach the whole drive.
- GitHub has no secret that all of a personal account's repositories read, so on a personal account Track also renews the secret in each other repository of the account that holds one and tracks the same drive by its drive.json, where that file is dated earlier than the connection; a walk-only repository names no drive and is left alone, and a failure is named with the dialog kept open.
- Checks R1 to R3 and FS4 hold it, with mutants f134 to f145 and nine aimed again (symphony-app branch claude/remember-drive-checks).
