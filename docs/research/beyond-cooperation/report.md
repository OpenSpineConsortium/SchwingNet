A row's costs are per landed task (the pull request merged with its check green and no revert, or the task's tests passed), so a row whose outcome is not recorded, or where nothing landed, shows none; the means are over the row's records, one per transcript. Repeated calls and compactions stand beside every cost, the two MAST failure modes the records measure (section 3): an arm that lowers a cost by raising them has moved the cost, not removed it.
