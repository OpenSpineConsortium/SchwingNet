# Pre-registration: TotalSegmentator's vertebrae on the gold tier 4 CTs

Source: the thread "Train on randomized spine phantoms" of the project symphony_3, 2026-10-31, on the owner's word of 00:55 UTC, "you can start the totalsegmentator segmentation of those scans currently on the grid", which follows his rule of 2026-10-28 22:58 UTC that "even gold tier 4, which may lack masks can be segmented and used for studies" (docs/research/gold3-ct/README.md, section 2c), his word of 2026-10-30 21:44 UTC that the gold tier is for population priors only, and his rule of 2026-10-29 that every deep learning job on the grid runs on a launched flow under a frozen pre-registration; the protocol is the Lab Recipe (artifacts/the-lab-recipe-1t2gvp/index.md, "Pre-register and freeze"). Label: confidential-method, as the gold tier's own pre-registration (docs/research/gold3-ct/completion/PREREG.md).

This file is frozen. It is the design the gold tier 4 segmentation runs under, written in the Lab Recipe's seven parts before TotalSegmentator was run on any of these CTs and before any of its maps was read: on 2026-10-31 no job of this kind had been sent. Grid jobs need no count from the owner since his word of 2026-10-30 23:03 UTC; when a send may be made again is the gate's rule in part 6. Every departure from this file after the freeze goes into DEVIATIONS.md beside it, dated, with its reason.

## 1. The question

For each CT the census admitted as gold tier 4, one whose image shows C2's foot and the sacrum's top by the census's SPINEPS mask and which no release draws, does TotalSegmentator, run on the CT as it lies, draw C2, every level below it and the sacrum in their order, and does it name each bone as the SPINEPS mask names it? The two machines' maps, kept side by side with each CT's agreement known, are what the population priors and the owner's own decoder read of a CT no person drew.

## 2. Population and inputs

A unit is one CT, named by the sha256 of its path (tools/gold4_totalseg_grid.py select).
- At the freeze, by data/gold3-ct/members.json (census.gold4, by_collection), that leaves at most 1,328 CTs, 1,319 admitted and 9 look, in 16 collections: abdomenatlas3_mini 641, idc 443 (439 admitted, 4 look), panorama 57 (53 and 4), quadra_hc 47, curvas 47 (46 and 1), deep_psma 42, aortaseg60 40, amos22 2, waw_tace 2, and 1 each of carmlspine, cobra, curvas_pdacvi, kidney_thoraxabdomen, pengwin2026, ribfrac and spinesac. - The census's ends: each unit keeps the field test's head and foot (tools/gold3_field.py head_end, foot_end). Of the census's 1,388 new studies, 968 have both ends drawn (members.json, census.gold4, ends_and_flags); the gold tier 3 completion found that TotalSegmentator draws C2 in only 4 of 19 fields that show no more than C2's foot (docs/research/gold3-ct/README.md, section 2c), which is why part 4 reads its first bar on the units whose two ends SPINEPS draws.

- The CTs: each unit's CT as its tier list row names it (ct_path) on the data root, read and never changed, and never resampled before the segmenter, so its map comes out on the CT's own grid.
- The SPINEPS masks: the census's (spineps_vert), in VerSe's ids as the census read them, the reference each map is set beside; read and never changed. They stay what admitted these CTs; TotalSegmentator's maps are a second machine arm beside them, by the owner's word above, never pooled with them.

- Files and hashes: each map's sha256 is in its unit's run.json and in records.jsonl, with the segmenter's version, the GPU, the commit and the chain; the chain's jobs.json holds the sha256 of the census detail and of each tier list read. No CT, map or list leaves the grid; only counts by collection do.

## 3. The measurements

For each unit, on the CT's own grid:

- Each level's voxels and centre in world mm (the affine applied to the mean voxel), for TotalSegmentator's map and for the SPINEPS mask; a level is drawn where it has at least 200 voxels (MIN_VOXELS), as the field test and the matcher count one; the SPINEPS mask's 26 and 29 are both its sacrum.
- The kind, the first that holds (tools/gold4_totalseg_grid.py judge): no_c2 or no_sacrum where TotalSegmentator draws no C2 or no sacrum; sacrum_above where its sacrum's centre lies no lower than its lowest level's along the line from its C2's centre to that level's; no_comparison where the SPINEPS mask could not be read; variant where the SPINEPS mask draws a T13 or an L6, for which TotalSegmentator has no class; name_shift where a bone carries two names, the two maps' levels from C2 to L5 paired nearest centres first, each level once, no pair further apart than 10 mm (MATCH_MM); unmatched where no bone pairs, or where a level of either map has no partner and does not lie above every level the other draws; else concordant. C1 and the sacrum are left out of the pairing: C1 lies above the field the census admitted, and how much of the sacrum a field holds moves its centre. A level one map draws above the top of the other's is coverage, not a disagreement on names, since the census admitted fields that show only C2's foot; below that top no level is passed over, since the foot of the column is where the count's variants lie.
- Each level's Dice, of the levels both maps draw, where the two maps share one lattice: one grid (equal shapes and affines within 0.01 mm, AFFINE_SLACK_MM), or the SPINEPS mask reoriented where only the order or the sign of its axes differs, which moves no voxel. On another lattice the centres alone compare the two, since a resampling would put its own error into the Dice.
- The seconds each CT took, and every failure with its reason.

## 4. The hypotheses and their bars

The merge reads each from the records and says it in the report, met or not (tools/gold4_totalseg_grid.py bar_lines):

- H1: of the units segmented whose SPINEPS mask draws both C2 and the sacrum, at least 90 percent are drawn whole by TotalSegmentator, of a kind other than no_c2, no_sacrum and sacrum_above. The units with an end only in view or a border end are reported beside it with no bar, for the reason part 2 gives.
- H2: of the units segmented whose SPINEPS mask was read and draws no T13 or L6 (the kinds concordant, name_shift and unmatched), at least 80 percent are concordant.
- H3: over the concordant units whose two maps share a lattice, the median of the levels' Dice is at least 0.80 in the cervical region and at least 0.85 in the thoracic and in the lumbar; the cervical bar is lower since one boundary voxel weighs more in a smaller bone's Dice. The sacrum's median is reported with no bar, since the two machines' sacrum masks are not drawn to one definition. A region with no level on a shared lattice leaves H3 not tested.
- A bar is read once the merge ends 0, every unit segmented or given up; until then the report calls the bars provisional. A bar not met is reported as such; nothing is tuned, and no unit is run again to meet one.

## 5. What counts as failure

- A unit fails where TotalSegmentator raises, writes no file, writes labels outside 0 to 255, or where its map cannot be read or written; the reason is recorded whole in run.json and cut to 160 characters in the report. A unit killed mid-run (out of memory, out of time) leaves its run.json with no exit, which the next chain counts as that chain's failure.
- A unit that failed in two chains at one code (GIVE_UP_AFTER) is given up and left to the report; a new commit gives it its chances again. A task stops after three failures in a row (FAILS_IN_A_ROW) and takes no new unit once 11 hours have passed (STOP_AFTER), an hour short of its time.
- A SPINEPS mask that cannot be read leaves the unit segmented with the kind no_comparison; the map stands without its comparison.
- What this arm draws never raises a tier and is never pooled with a release's masks, with the census's SPINEPS masks or with another arm: it is a machine arm of its own, recorded by segmenter, version and sha256, read only where a method says it reads it.

## 6. The analysis plan

- The gate word `totalseg gold4` sends one chain: grid/totalseg-gold4.sbatch as a Slurm array of 16 GPU tasks (hpcworker's class gpu, 8 CPUs, 64 GB, 12 hours, not requeued), each one run of the launched flow gold4_totalseg (flows/gold4_totalseg.py) with --part run from a copy of the code at one commit, the tasks sharing the units by claiming each, then one CPU job afterany the array (class batch, 4 CPUs, 16 GB, 2 hours) with --part merge, which writes records.jsonl and the counts. Each part holds its run to this file at start and at end (flows/stage.py) and keeps a progress page.
- A send again carries on where the last stopped, since a unit segmented is passed over. The gate queues one only where the last chain's report does not end in exit 0 and the chain made headway: label maps grew since the last send's tally, the commit changed, or the last chain never started; or where TOTALSEG_RERUNS in grid/gate.sh names gold4:<job>. A report that ends in exit 0 is done, and the gate prints it and queues nothing.
- The report, logs/TOTALSEG-gold4.md, carries counts alone: the units by reason left out, the segmented, failed and given up, the kinds, the Dice medians by region, the bars, by collection, the time, the held out; no path under the data root, no case, no person key, no DICOM header.
- The counts and the bars' verdicts go into this folder's README by pull request, and the gold tier's README (docs/research/gold3-ct/README.md, section 2c) points to it.
- A use: a method that reads bone names from gold 4 reads the concordant units, unless its own pre-registration says otherwise; the person key and the licence classes of data/licences.json hold for every use as they do for the rest of the gold tier; the same scan held by two collections is not resolved here, so a use that counts persons resolves duplicates by the census's person key first; C-ArmLSpine's unit is never trained on.
- Size: about 1,328 label maps of a few MB each on the lab volume, the records and the report; nothing is fetched.
