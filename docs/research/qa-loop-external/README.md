# The QA loop with external real arms: where it stands

Source: the local session of 1 November 2026, on the owner's asks of that evening (real fluoroscopy in the validation of the 2D landmarks, DeepFluoro first then COBRA2026, and the sacral landmarks for the count's anchor). Confidential with the rest of this repository: the landmark direction stays private.

The design is PREREG.md beside this file: the QA loop of docs/research/qa-loop/ with two further sets of real X-rays read by its real naming and presence gates, never by the selection, each source's own result and the sacral anchor's presence recorded beside the pooled gates. It runs as the launched flow qaloop_ext (flows/qaloop_ext.py), sent by the grid word `qaloop qa_ext_smoke` or `qaloop qa_ext`.

- The pairs: tools/realx_pairs.py builds them from the realxray exports already on the grid (DeepFluoro's 2026-09-21 export, COBRA's Elekta export v3), into the data root's realx/deepfluoro and realx/cobra, each with a manifest of every person and the reason for every person not read. On 1 November: DeepFluoro 3 specimens and 142 frames, one specimen held out for test; COBRA 66 cases and 579 frames, 27 held out for test; frames more than 15 degrees from the anteroposterior or lateral view left out (48 and 1,005).
- The checks before any run: on the grid the projected landmarks of each level fell inside the export's own box for that level on 82 per cent of DeepFluoro's in-frame points and 86 per cent of COBRA's (each export drew its boxes from the same CT through the same pose; the rest are the transverse and spinous tips past a body box), and contact sheets of both sets were read by eye. The first sacral points took the top of S1 from the alae where they rise above the endplate; the top is now taken within 15 mm of the midline.
- Why: the base loop's real arm is 13 C-ArmLSpine validation persons, 6 frames for presence and 8 runs a view for real naming, too few for either bar's lower bound to pass however good the landmarks are.
- Where it stands: the first smoke (chain 40939308) failed in round 0 on a flat outline of the four sacral points in a lateral view, fixed in #549 by widening it with the lowest body. The smoke sent again (chain 40939352, an A30 on reqp, 13.5 minutes) holds, verdict 0. After 25 steps a round, so as a smoke and not as a result: the real gate runs were 16 from C-ArmLSpine, 169 from COBRA and 1 from DeepFluoro, whose frames show the lumbar column only at their top edge. Real naming, unanchored, named C-ArmLSpine's anteroposterior runs all right and its lateral three of four, but COBRA's only 0.23 and 0.41. Presence: 3 of 6 C-ArmLSpine frames, 163 of 330 COBRA's and 31 of 49 DeepFluoro's at or above the 0.6 percentile. The sacral anchor: 2 of 6, 134 of 529 and 69 of 127. So the 13 C-ArmLSpine validation persons flattered the naming, and on the larger arm a count with no anchor rarely names two or three lumbar bodies right; the anchor, which would settle the count, shows on about a quarter of COBRA's kV frames and half of DeepFluoro's C-arm frames. The full loop (chain 40937444's sibling, qa_ext chain 40939478) was sent the same night, six links on reqp's A30s.
