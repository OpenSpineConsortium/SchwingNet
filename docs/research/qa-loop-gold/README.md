# The QA loop on the gold tiers: where it stands

Source: the thread "Build self-propelling QA Metaflow pipeline", 31 October 2026, from the owner's ask of 03:24 UTC that day (once the QA loop works, start at once on gold tier 3 and gold tier 4 to see how they work end to end, building the X-ray and the evaluation parts as well), and the split of the work agreed that night with the thread "Train on randomized spine phantoms", which keeps the gold tier 4 TotalSegmentator run, the correlation prior and the plan issues. Confidential with the rest of this repository: the landmark and phantom direction stays private.

The gold arm is the QA loop of docs/research/qa-loop/ asked three more questions of real CTs whose bones are named: gold tier 3, ENHANCE.PET's CTs with a person's annotation of each bone, the persons the auto-anatomist screen planned (grid/screens/gold3_latent_screen.json); and gold tier 4, the CTs where TotalSegmentator's map agrees with SPINEPS's on which bone is which (the records of tools/gold4_totalseg_grid.py, the kind concordant). It runs under PREREG.md beside this file, frozen before any run, which adopts the loop's sections 1 to 6 and adds what follows.

- The kind: qaloop_gold, the loop's flow under a kind of its own (QaloopGoldFlow, flows/qaloop_gold.py), so that it is held to its own frozen PREREG while the loop's stays as it was frozen; started only through flows/launch.py, by grid/qaloop_gold.sbatch, from the grid word `qaloop qa_gold_smoke` or `qaloop qa_gold` (grid/gate.sh, QALOOP_GOLD_PLANS). The word refuses a gold loop plan, in words, until gold tier 3's CT and label folders are on the data root and gold tier 4's records.jsonl stands under its run folder (the word `totalseg gold4`); the smoke reads three stand-ins a tier where a tier is absent. Its folders, record and report are the loop's, the report written as the study qa-loop under this PREREG's design (tools/qaloop_report.py, DESIGNS).
- The X-ray stage (tools/gold_xray.py): each CT is read once a chain into a cache in the chain's work folder. The oracle the phantoms use places its eighteen landmarks a level on the CT's own label map, T12 to L5, in the CT's world millimetres, and a level with a landmark more than 8 mm from its own bone is read as one it could not place (DEVIATIONS.md, item 2); the sacrum's top is the mean of its body's voxels within 10 mm of the head-most along the column, its body the voxels within 15 mm of the nearest the mask comes to the column's line through the lowest level read, since on a real CT the sacrum's wings and the processes that meet L5 rise beside and behind L5 above the top of its body (DEVIATIONS.md, item 1); and the column's axes are the oracle's level frames' mean, as the phantoms' are laid out. Radiographs are cast through C-arm poses drawn about the anteroposterior and lateral views as the phantoms' are, at an image intensifier's 23 or 30 cm field, the CT's attenuation summed along each pixel's ray from the source and dark outside the field's circle. The poses are drawn from the person alone, so a person's frames are the same in every round.
- The evaluation, three gates asked tier by tier, each tier its own arm and never pooled: gold naming, the fingerprint's chosen landmarks naming the held-out persons' levels in runs of the radiographs, against the whole pool's; gold span, the same runs named by the model the loop fits on its phantoms alone, with no anchor and with the bottom anchor, which asks whether the drawn columns span real spines; gold presence, the chosen landmarks' percentile on the held-out radiographs. A gate passes where every tier passes and lacks its data where any tier lacks it, so a tier that could not be read stops the loop with its reason. The network's reading of the held-out CTs (Dice, surface distance, named right) is recorded beside the phantoms' and gated on by nothing.
- What the gold arm changes in the loop: the fingerprint's objective weighs each tier as an arm alike with the phantoms and the real pairs; the presence floor keeps a landmark only where every source that saw it shows it; the controller adds two landmarks for gold naming, takes the fingerprint's levers (its persons or its landmarks) for gold span and raises the presence floor for gold presence, none of them twice in a round where another gate's change already serves it; and the loop's progress is judged on the gold measures too.
- The rules it keeps: the persons of data/splits/test-persons.json and of data/splits/latent-prior-test-reserve.json are never read in either tier, their keys read only to leave them out; a gold tier 3 person is left out of gold tier 4; every person is in one part; no gold CT, radiograph or real frame enters the network's training, which is phantoms alone; the case list is chosen at a chain's first round and kept, so the TotalSegmentator records, which grow while a chain runs, never change a round; and no path, case or person leaves the round, whose record holds counts by tier and part and the reasons a case was left out.
- Where it stands: built and tested in this thread's container on 2026-10-31. Run there end to end through the launcher on stand-ins, QaloopGoldFlow ended its two rounds in exit 0 in about 8 minutes with every gate asked; its numbers, of stand-ins and a network trained for minutes, answer no question. The smoke's six gold tier 3 CTs, fetched from ENHANCE.PET's public bucket into the session's scratch space for that check alone and never committed, were then read through the X-ray stage, each in about a minute with its four radiographs: the oracle placed 35 of their 36 levels, and every landmark of 33 of them lay within 6.1 mm of its own bone; the reading found the sacrum's top on its wings and two levels misread off their bone, which DEVIATIONS.md items 1 and 2 correct, and was repeated under both. With those six as gold tier 3, and gold tier 4 still on stand-ins since its records stand only on the grid, the smoke then ran again through the launcher and ended its two rounds in exit 0 in 13 minutes: every gate was asked of both tiers, three levels were left unread, and the network, trained on phantoms for those minutes, read the two held-out CTs at a Dice of 0.34 and a surface distance of 6.9 mm, against 0.87 and 1.3 mm on a phantom stand-in, a gap the smoke cannot size and the full loop records every round. Gold presence is a rank within each radiograph, so the radiographs' low contrast of bone against soft tissue does not enter it; on the six it was lower than on the stand-ins, a median percentile of 0.38 against 0.58 over 7 and 4 frames, which the gas, ribs and pelvis that overlap a real spine may explain, not yet measured. The tests: tests/test_gold_xray.py reads phantom CTs written as the releases write theirs (an int16 map with slope and intercept, a label map with the sacrum apart and wings beside L5) and checks the landmarks, the sacrum's top, the radiographs' geometry and the case lists' exclusions; the gold gates, the controller's levers and the report are tested in tests/test_qaloop.py and tests/test_qaloop_report.py, and the word's refusals and queueing in tests/test_gate.py. The grid send waits on GitHub Actions, which stopped starting jobs for the organization's private repositories at about 06:20 UTC on 2026-10-31 for the account's billing; then the smoke goes first and qa_gold only once the smoke ends in exit 0.
