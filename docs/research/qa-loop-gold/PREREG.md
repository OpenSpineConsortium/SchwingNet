## 1. What this design is

The QA loop's design, docs/research/qa-loop/PREREG.md as frozen with sha256 c588988049e1c983ee551f9d5c1177f0b8c7db8083bf2dfd73bea6a656e9da55, is adopted whole for everything its plans hold: its question, population and inputs, checkpoints and bars, controller, failures and verdict, training watch, analysis plan and known limits (its sections 1 to 6, with 5a) hold here word for word, except where sections 4 to 6 below add to them. That file is frozen and is never edited, so this design is a file of its own, run by the loop's flow under the kind qaloop_gold (flows/qaloop_gold.py, grid/qaloop_gold.sbatch, the gate's word `qaloop <plan>` for a plan grid/gate.sh's QALOOP_GOLD_PLANS names).

What it adds is a gold arm: the gold tiers' CTs, whole spines whose bones are named, rendered as a C-arm sees them; three checkpoints asked of them; and the network's reading of their held-out CTs. The question it adds: do the landmarks the loop chooses name the lumbar levels of real spines, on radiographs cast through real CTs at a C-arm's geometry and fields, and does the model the loop fits on its phantoms name them too? Gold tier 3 answers it with a person's annotation of every bone, gold tier 4 with two segmenters that agree, each tier on its own.

## 3. Population and inputs of the gold arm

- Gold tier 3: ENHANCE.PET's CTs and the annotated vertebra masks of its Vertebrae folder under the data root (aws_enhance_pet_ct/raw, aws_enhance_pet_labels/raw), for the persons of the auto-anatomist screen's rows, grid/screens/gold3_latent_screen.json: 120 persons of the gold store's normal class, 96 in its training part and 24 in its validation part, the test lists and the repeat-scan units already kept apart by that screen, each person's part the screen's. A row whose CT or mask is not found exactly once is left out and counted. Its labels are the gold store's (tools/gold3_ct.py): T12 19 to L5 24, the sacrum 28, the sacrum one mask, never an S1 of its own.
- Gold tier 4: the CTs TotalSegmentator's run segmented (records.jsonl under the run's folder, docs/research/gold4-totalseg/PREREG.md), read only where its map agrees with SPINEPS's on which bone is which (the record's kind concordant), as that design's part on its use says; never one it marked held out, never one without a person key (unknown is not clean), never a person gold tier 3 reads, so no person is in both tiers. Each person's part is dealt by data/splits/rule.json (salt schwingnet-2026-10-21, validation share 0.1), the test part never read, and at most 200 training and 60 validation persons a chain are read, chosen by a hash of the person alone (tools/gold_xray.py, person_order), every CT of a person kept together. Its labels are VerSe's: T12 19 to L5 24, the sacrum 26, TotalSegmentator's S1 and sacrum one mask.
- Never read: the persons of data/splits/test-persons.json and of the latent shape priors' test reserve, data/splits/latent-prior-test-reserve.json, in either tier; their keys are read only to leave them out.
- Never pooled: every sample's view carries its tier (ap_gold3, lat_gold4 and so on), so no cell, gate, measure or interval pools gold tier 3 with gold tier 4, nor either with the phantoms or the real pairs.
- Never trained on: the network trains on phantoms alone, as the loop's does; no gold CT, no radiograph and no real frame enters its training. The gold tiers' training persons serve the fingerprint's selection as the real pairs' training persons do, and their held-out persons serve the gates.
- One case list a chain: the cases are chosen at the chain's first round and kept in the chain's work folder under a hash of what chose them, so the TotalSegmentator records, which grow while a chain runs, never change a round.

What is read from a case (tools/gold_xray.py). Every case is read as a typical column, T12 to L5 over the sacrum. The oracle's eighteen landmarks of each of its levels are placed on the CT's own label map as on the real pairs' masks, in the CT's millimetres; the sacrum's top is the mean of the sacrum's voxels within 10 mm of its head-most voxel along the column; the column's axes are the mean of the oracle's level frames. A level the oracle cannot place is left unread, counted, and breaks the runs at it; a case whose map draws no sacrum, of which the oracle places no level, or whose CT or map cannot be read, is left out and counted by its reason.

The radiographs. For each view (anteroposterior and lateral) and each field of the plan (230 and 300 mm across at the detector, an image intensifier's 23 and 30 cm), one frame of 512 pixels from a C-arm pose drawn about the view as the phantoms' are (tools/qaloop_samples.py, draw_pose: yaw, tilt and roll uniform within 10 degrees, the source 600 to 700 mm from the beam's centre and 1,100 to 1,200 mm from the detector, the beam aimed at one of the case's level centres moved by a normal draw of 20 mm standard deviation on each axis), the pixel the field over the frame's size. The CT is brought down to about 2 mm by whole blocks, read as attenuation, (HU + 1000) / 1000 with nothing below 0, and summed along each pixel's ray from the source in 2 mm steps, trilinearly; outside the field's circle the frame is dark, and bone is bright as the loop reads a real frame. A person's poses are drawn from the person alone, so a person's radiographs are the same in every round.

The fingerprint's samples. frames_per_view frames a view (two in the smoke, eight in the loop), each a pose drawn as above with one of the fields, from the person alone; the landmarks projected with 2 pixels of noise and a point outside the field's circle by 4 pixels read as unseen; the bodies in the frame the longest stretch of consecutive levels whose ten body points are all seen, the lower of two of one length, and the sacrum in the frame where its top is seen; and the runs as the loop draws a phantom's (tools/qaloop_samples.py, runs_of): for each length k from 2 to k_max (five in the smoke, six in the loop) that the bodies in the frame hold, one stretch of k of them at a place drawn at random with no anchor, and, where they end at L5 with the sacrum in the frame, their last k with the bottom anchor. The landmarks' presence is read on each radiograph as the loop reads it on a real frame (the loop's section 3), a radiograph with no body wholly inside its field read as none.

Stand-ins (the smoke alone). Where the data root holds no CT of a tier, three typical phantoms a tier, rendered by the vendored phantom and read the same way, the sacrum's top 15 mm below their last disc as the phantoms' anchor is, two in the training part and one held out. Every answer asked of a stand-in says so, and its numbers say nothing of a real CT.

## 4. The checkpoints the gold arm adds, and their bars

Each is asked of each tier on its own. A gold gate passes where every tier passes, and lacks its data where any tier lacks it, as an arm the fingerprint's gate asks with no cell does, so a tier that could not be read stops the loop with its reason and never drops out of a gate unseen. The bars are the plans' and the same in both.

- Gold naming: the fingerprint's chosen landmarks and its namer (the loop's section 3), fitted per view on the selection's runs, which hold each tier's training persons' runs under the tier's own views, and judged on the tier's held-out persons' runs. The tier's views hold typical columns alone, so its cells are the runs with no anchor: with the sacrum in the frame the count from it names every level of a column that can only be typical, and such a cell would say nothing of the landmarks. Each cell (view, no anchor, typical) passes where the Wilson 95 per cent lower bound of its accuracy is at or above 0.90 and the lower median of its runs' margins at or above log 9 (2.1972); the ceiling is the whole pool's namer, fitted and judged alike, and a failing cell is beyond it only as the fingerprint's are.
- Gold span: the tier's held-out persons' runs, with no anchor and with the bottom anchor, named by the model the loop fits on its phantoms, the runs' views read as the phantoms' own (the namer of the phantoms' views, every column type and prior of the loop's section 3); the same bars and the same ceiling rule, each cell (view, anchor, type) a cell of the tier's span arm. It asks whether the phantoms span real spines: whether a model that has seen only drawn columns names a real column's levels.
- Gold presence: on the tier's held-out persons' radiographs, the chosen landmarks' mean percentile per radiograph; a radiograph shows the fingerprint where that mean is at or above 0.6, and the tier passes where the Wilson lower bound of the share of such radiographs is at or above 0.6, as the loop's presence gate is held.

## 5. What the gold arm changes in the loop's own steps

- The fingerprint's objective is the mean over every arm its selection holds, the phantoms', the real pairs' and each gold tier's, each arm weighed alike, an arm the runs hold with no cell counting 0, as the loop's section 3 weighs its two.
- The presence floor (the loop's section 4) reads each landmark's mean percentile on the real pairs' training frames and on each tier's training radiographs, and keeps the least of those that read it, so a landmark stays in the pool only where every source that saw it shows it.
- The controller: gold naming adds two landmarks where a failing cell is within the ceiling's reach and the search took every landmark it was allowed, as real naming does, none where another gate's change this round added them; gold span takes the fingerprint's change as the loop's section 4 gives it (landmarks while a failing cell is within reach, then the selection's persons as the memory holds), none where a change this round already added landmarks or grew the fingerprint's persons; gold presence raises the floor by 0.1 as presence does, none where presence's change this round raised it. Every change stays within the ranges and the latency's cap the loop's section 4 gives.
- The loop's progress (the loop's section 5a) is judged on these measures too, each read off the same held-out persons and the same drawn poses in every round: each tier's cells' mean lower bound, its span cells' mean lower bound, the gold presence share's lower bound, and each tier's held-out Dice (section 6).
- The verdict is the loop's section 5: a loop ends 0 only where every gate, the gold ones among them, was met and the report holds; the smoke ends 0 where the loop decided, every gate it asks had its data in every round, it went round at least twice unless the first round met every gate, and the report holds.

## 6. The analysis plan

Each round's gates.json holds, beside the loop's, each tier's cases found, read and left out by reason, its persons by part, the gold gates' answers tier by tier, the span test's cells and their ceiling, and the network's reading of each tier's held-out CTs: each CT's label map kept to its typical levels, cut into the auto-anatomist's crops in the oracle's frame as the loop's own rows are, and segmented by the round's network, its Dice, surface distance, centroid distance and names right and within one, by tier. That reading is recorded and gated on by nothing, since the network's crops are cut where the CT's own labels place them; it counts among the progress measures of section 5. The flow's end writes the record and the CLAIM 2024 and REFORMS report as the loop's does (tools/report.py --study qa-loop), the gold gates said tier by tier and the gold arm's limits restated after the loop's. Only counts, means and intervals leave the grid: never a person, a path, a CT or a radiograph.

## 7. Known limits, stated before the run, beyond those of the loop's section 6

- The radiographs are digitally reconstructed from CTs, with no scatter, no detector and no image intensifier's distortion, so they are not fluoroscopy, and the presence read on them is not the real frames'.
- Every gold case is read as a typical column, so the gold arm says nothing of the anomalous classes.
- The gold persons' CTs are not yet warped onto columns drawn from the latent prior, so each tier holds as many columns as it has persons.
- The gold tiers' training persons steer the fingerprint's choice, and the controller changes the loop on their held-out persons' answers, so the gold gates are asked of persons the loop was steered by.
- Gold naming reads the runs with no anchor alone, since a column that can only be typical is named by its count from the sacrum.
- The span test's bottom anchor is the sacrum's own top on a gold CT and a point 15 mm below the last disc on a phantom.
- Gold tier 4's names are two segmenters' agreement, not a person's, and the persons whose maps disagree are left out, which may leave out the hardest spines; a scan two collections hold under two person keys is not resolved to one person, as the gold tier 4 design says.
- The network's reading of the held-out gold CTs crops them in the oracle's frame from their own label maps, so it measures the outline and the name, not finding the vertebra.
- The smoke reads stand-ins where the data root holds no CT of a tier, typical phantoms in place of its CTs, which say nothing of a real CT.
