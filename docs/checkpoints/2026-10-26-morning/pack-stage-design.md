# Design: stage 3, pack, of the SPINEPS to FleXray anomaly route (#391), revised after review

Read-only design for the implementer of `schwingnet-anomaly-pack`. Sources: the three maps (flexray, records, conventions), the route note section 5 item 3, two skeptics' reviews, and confirming reads of the FleXray sdist and the repositories (cited as file:line). Nothing here was run. Every flaw the reviews raised is folded in below or listed under "Flaws declined" at the end.

## 1. Decisions where the maps disagree, say "not found", or the ground forces a choice

1. **Whole volumes, not crops.** `fxr-dataset pack` never resamples and refuses any CT whose x,y extent differs from `crops.size` (packaging.py:475-479), while the selection's CTs vary in plane. The manifests carry no `crops` block: `storage_layout "subjects"` (packaging.py:1229-1230), HU window and float16 as item 3 asks. Render computes centroids itself (`compute_fg_centroids: True`, render.py:106-115); only a `sample_weighting` other than uniform asserts the crops layout (runtime.py:673-675), which the tune leaves at uniform. The whole-volume layout passes `check`: its smoke load builds `CTTrainingDataset(label_mode="native")` with crop_mode none and uniform weighting and loads item 0 (packaging.py:1676-1715; runtime.py:607-651). The `--crops X,Y,Z` switch on `manifest` adds the block and refuses unless every admitted CT is X by Y in plane (counted), so the released recipe stays one flag away.
2. **HU window [-1000, 2000]**: the route note's words; FleXray's files give no number (preprocessing.py:3-6, README.md:190).
3. **Mask per collection, the expert mask first.** VerSe and CTSpinoPelvic1K take their release masks (route note :113); where such a scan also has a SPINEPS ctd, the ctd is read for countability only. Every other row takes a SPINEPS mask from a named store or a relabelled copy, in the order of section 4; a raw-id store, any `seg/<case>/ts`, `ts/`, `tsbody/` or `ts_total/` folder is never read. Two ids the first design missed are mapped: **80** (TPTBox Metal, written by SPINEPS into otherwise named masks, 590 in the census, spineps_ids.py:95-96, passed by the guard :264-265, :329-330, and left in every passthrough and source store) goes to background, counted per mask, with a second count of masks where it held 5 ml or more (the voxel volume from the mask's pixdim), since those the census could not explain; **29** (the retired S1 of CTSP's release, ctsp_registry_census.py:291-292, DATA_MANIFEST.md:600) goes to canonical 27, counted.
4. **No relabel runs in this stage.** The route note's "renamed from the agreed column" names no column; the existing relabelled copies under `DATA/spineps/ctd_relabel/` are that renaming, and the owner asked for existing masks. The stage runs only the guard, `spineps_ids.py check --strict` (lines `OK   <path>: <verdict>` / `FAIL <path>: ... [<verdict>]: ...`, :905-914), and refuses every SPINEPS mask not listed `OK`. A refusal for `veridah_skipped_name` (:410-416, raised strict or not) is counted apart from the other verdicts, because it is a wrong mask, not a partial one: every time a reader could check one, the names on one side of the skip were one level off (:402-406, REPAIRS.md section 4), so it is never packed (Flaws declined, 3).
5. **Countability and completeness are measured, with the records read.** A row is countable when the field holds C1 or C2 and the sacrum: `top_from` is `mask` when canonical 1 or 2 is present, else `ctd` when the ctd names point "2", else `none`; `sacrum_from` is `mask` when 27 is present, else `record` when `junction_ls_in_fov` is True in registry.csv (column 30) or, for a harvest person, in `anomaly_harvest/<collection>/rows.csv` (acquisition_screen.py:901-903), else `ctd` when point "26" is named, else `none`. A row is **complete** when both come from the mask and the presacral ids present have no gap (section 4.3); every other countable row is **partial**: a body the records say is in the field is unlabelled, or a body between two named ones is. Partial and complete rows go to two packages (item 11).
6. **The package lands under the data root** as the landing `flexray_pack`, `$DATA/flexray_pack/`, so the size rule credits its growth as a bank's (size-rule.sh:857-862, 869, 888-892); `$ANOMALY_ROOT/pack/` keeps the stage's own records, with `landing` a symlink. Nothing in the files admits a folder outside `$DATA`.
7. **The guard runs in the spineps conda environment** as the timing stage does (anomaly.sbatch:109-126); everything else of ours runs under `$PYDRV` (train-cpu), standard library, with numpy imported lazily for float masks alone (section 3).
8. **The pack has a venv of its own.** The bench's pin `flexray==1.0.2 h5py` carries neither thunderpack nor nibabel: FleXray's base dependencies lack both (pyproject.toml:36-45), both live in the `train` extra (:71-78), `pack` imports thunderpack lazily at the ThunderDB open (packaging.py:2113-2130, :1247) and every NIfTI read imports nibabel lazily (nifti.py:62-78), and pixi.lock holds none of them. So `PACK_PIN="flexray==1.0.2 h5py thunderpack nibabel"` keys a new venv through `venv_for` (moved from bench.sbatch:66-78 to `grid/venv.sh`), leaving the bench's alone, and a probe `import thunderpack, nibabel, fxr.datasets.cli` runs before any long step, so a broken environment costs minutes.
9. **Every row is packed; only countable complete and partial rows train.** Splits: `train`, `val`, `test`, `holdout`, `uncounted` (uncountable, or a distrusted collection: CTSpinoPelvic1K rows by the owner's words of 2026-10-26 00:16 UTC, `--distrust ctsp` default) and `g3_excluded` (item 10), the last two read by nothing in the tune; `split --uncounted pack|drop` is the switch of the decision card. No row leaves the pack over a decision that may change, so no second pack follows one.
10. **The G3 exclusion** resolves a person through `group_of` and `raw_to_group` before `side_of_person` (make_split.py:54-57 keys sides by group representative), and calls a person transitional by the registry's or rows.csv's `subtype` (anything but none or ordinary: the G1 subtypes, castellvi, sacralised and lumbar rib among them, which the selection's variant calls normal) or by variant in {L6, T13, four_lumbar, both_L6_T13} (anomaly_route.py:369-379). Such a person on G3 val or test is packed under `g3_excluded`; the normals it costs are counted.
11. **Two packages, one landing, one job.** `schwingnet_spine` holds the complete rows and `schwingnet_spine_partial` the partial ones, each with every split, because FleXray routes the loss per dataset name (base.yml:310-359): under `standard_seg` a mask lacking a sacrum in the field teaches sacrum = background, and under `partial_labeled_seg` (`ignore_empty_labels`, losses/segmentation.py:263-267) the L6 and T13 channels stop learning absence on normal spines, which is the negatives' whole point. Each sample's metadata records `completeness`.
12. **The pack binds no protocol; the protocol fits the tune's head.** The manifests omit `protocol_name` (optional, packaging.py:333), since readiness refuses a package whose `_attrs.protocol_name` differs from the training protocol (readiness.py:484-489) and the first design's 28-label protocol gave the model 28 channels where the tune copies FleXray's 61 head rows into 63 (route note :120). `schwingnet_spine_v1` is the released 63 labels plus `vertebra_t13` and `vertebra_l6` (65), with `models/schwingnet_spine_v1.yml` of the 61 released model labels plus the two (63); the 28 stored names are a subset of both, so the remap compiles (remap.py:150-212) and the head copy has somewhere to land.
13. **No byte lands before the admission.** The admission is read from the CT header census of rows.csv, before the rewrite; it never subtracts an existing package, only masks already written and verified; FleXray's orphaned temp and backup folders are removed before the room is read, and an old package whose digest differs is removed, with its bytes reported, before the new one is built, since `--overwrite` keeps old and new side by side at the swap (packaging.py:1738-1794).
14. **The stage holds no checkout.** A three-day job writing HOLD would make every GPU, bench and sweep job wait on it (gate.sh:287-296) and stop every merge reaching the grid. The stage copies what it runs into `$OUT/code-<commit>/` at start, runs from that copy, writes no HOLD, and `finish` treats a moved checkout as a note, not a failure, when `RAN_FROM_COPY=yes`.
15. **Test and holdout rows pack whole or the stage fails.** Any refused or unresolved row of role `test` or `holdout` ends the stage `FAILED: N test/holdout rows cannot be packed (see rows.csv, masks.csv)` with exit 1 after the counts are reported, unless `ALLOW_PARTIAL_TEST=yes`, a default in anomaly.sbatch flipped by pull request, since no option reaches a gate word.
16. **The validation split**: fold persons out of the pool, a count per variant drawn in rank order; rule, salt and counts frozen now, the drawn list's digest copied in by the next pull request (section 6, open question 1).

## 3. tools/nifti_label.py

Module docstring first (living_docs.py:313-317). NIfTI-1 only: header 348 bytes, `sizeof_hdr` at [0:4] gives the byte order (sample_index.py:343-349); `dim` 40:56, `datatype` 70:72, `bitpix` 72:74, `pixdim[0..3]` 76:92, `vox_offset` 108:112, `scl_slope`/`scl_inter` 112:120, `qform_code`/`sform_code` 252:256, the quaternion and offsets 256:280, `srow_x/y/z` 280:328. Data starts at `int(vox_offset)`; the bytes from 348 to there are copied through.

- `open_volume(path)`: `gzip.open` when the first two bytes are `1f 8b`, else `open`.
- `read_header(path) -> Header`: `(order, dims, datatype, bitpix, vox_offset, scl_slope, scl_inter, pixdim, qform_code, sform_code, quatern, qoffset, srows, head)`; reads only up to `vox_offset`, so the CT census costs one gzip block per file.
- `grid_of(h) -> tuple`: the twelve srow values when `sform_code > 0`, else pixdim[1:4] with the quaternion and offsets when `qform_code > 0`, else pixdim[1:4], the choice nibabel's `affine` makes, which FleXray reads from the image alone (packaging.py:712, :752-769); `same_grid(a, b, tol=1e-3)` requires the same choice on both sides and every value within `tol`, the tolerance harvest_verse_census.py:697 uses.
- `voxel_count(dims)`: product of the first three; `LabelRefused("not_3d")` unless `dim[0] == 3`, or 4 with `dim[4] == 1` (nifti.py:53-56 squeezes the same).
- `label_bytes(h, data) -> bytes`, one byte per voxel: datatype 2 as is; 4/512 and 8/768: every high-byte slice all zero (`count(b"\x00") == len`) else `high_bytes`, the low slice taken by byte order; 16/64: `import numpy` inside the function (as tools/bank.py does), `frombuffer`, refused `float_data` when numpy is missing or any value is not an integer in 0..255, else `astype(uint8).tobytes()`; `scaled` when `scl_slope` not in {0, 1} or `scl_inter != 0`; `length` when short.
- `classify(data, mapping, dropped) -> Ids`: no 255 marker. `known = bytes(sorted(mapping))` (0..29 and 80); `rest = data.translate(None, bytes(range(30)))` holds only dropped or unknown voxels, usually small; `unknown = rest.translate(None, bytes(dropped) + b"\x50")`; any unknown byte refuses `unknown_id` naming `sorted(set(unknown[:1 << 20]))`; `ids_in` = `{v for v in range(1, 30) if bytes((v,)) in data}` (29 probes, a present id stops at its first hit) plus `{v for v in dropped if bytes((v,)) in rest}`; `metal_voxels = rest.count(b"\x50")`. Two translate passes and about ten full memchr passes on a 100-400 MB mask: under two seconds.
- `translate_table(mapping)`: 256 bytes; 0 to 0, dropped and 80 to 0, 29 to 27.
- `rewrite(src, dst, mapping, dropped, ct_header) -> Rewritten`: reads, refuses `shape_mismatch` (first three dims) and `affine_mismatch` (`same_grid` against the CT), classifies, translates, refuses `empty` when no foreground remains, writes `dst`; returns `(ids_in, ids_out, dropped_counts, metal_voxels, metal_ml, voxels, dims, seconds)`.
- `write_label(path, h, data)`: header copy with datatype 2, bitpix 8, slope 1.0, inter 0.0, extensions kept; `gzip.GzipFile(filename="", mode="wb", fileobj=f, mtime=0, compresslevel=6)` (level 6, since level 9 on 100-400 MB of mostly zeros costs 10-30 s for a few percent); written to `path.part` then `os.replace`.
- `LabelRefused(Exception)` with `.reason` from the closed set: not_3d, high_bytes, float_data, scaled, length, shape_mismatch, affine_mismatch, unknown_id, empty.

## 4. tools/anomaly_pack.py

Standard library only; `csv`, `json`, `hashlib`, `argparse`, `pathlib`, `statistics`, `time`. Every subcommand prints a markdown section of counts to stdout and writes its CSV under `--out`; case ids and paths go only into the CSVs. Errors exit 1 with one fixed sentence on stderr that names a count or a reason, never a path or a case.

Constants: `CANONICAL` = background, vertebra_c1..c7, vertebra_t1..t12, vertebra_t13, vertebra_l1..l5, vertebra_l6, sacrum (ids 0..27). `VERSE_TO_CANONICAL`: 1..19 -> 1..19, 28 -> 20, 20..24 -> 21..25, 25 -> 26, 26 -> 27, 29 -> 27 (spineps_ids.py:93-94; ctsp_registry_census.py:289, :291). `DROPPED = {27} | {30..33} | {34..61} | {62..68} | {80}` -> 0, counted per id (DATA_MANIFEST.md:599-601; spineps_ids.py:95-96). `TRANSITIONAL = {"L6", "T13", "four_lumbar", "both_L6_T13"}` (anomaly_route.py:369-379); `ORDINARY = {"", "none", "ordinary"}` for the subtype column. `PRESACRAL = [1..19, 21..25]` with 20 and 26 optional.

### 4.1 `rows`

`rows --selection CSV --holdout JSON --data DIR --home DIR --registry CSV --harvest DIR --out DIR` -> `rows.csv` (person, collection, case, variant, subtype, tier, role, fold, ct, mask, mask_kind, ctd, status, ls_record, voxels, dims) and `## rows`.

- `read_selection`, `holdout_rows` (one row per scan of each `persons[]` entry, anomaly-holdout.json:26-415, role `holdout`), `fold_of` as before.
- `resolve(row, data, home, exists)`; status `ok`, `no_ct`, `no_nifti`, `no_mask`, `unknown_collection`. Stores, first found wins (records map section 2): `verse` ct `home/VerSeFusion/data/unified/scan-<case>/scan-<case>_ct.nii.gz`, mask `_msk` (`release`), ctd `home/bench_ts/spineps/native/VERSE_<case>_ctd.json`; `ctsp` ct `home/data/CTSpinoPelvic1K/ct/<case>_ct.nii.gz`, mask `labels/<case>_label.nii.gz` (`release`), ctd `data/spineps/ctsp/CTSP_<case>_ctd.json`; `totalsegmentator` ct from `home/bench_ts/cases.csv` else `home/data/TotalSegmentator/<s>/ct.nii.gz` (never `segmentations/`), mask `data/spineps/ctd_relabel/bench_ts_native/<case>_seg-vert_msk.nii.gz` then `home/bench_ts/spineps/native/...` (`spineps`), ctd beside it; `cobra2026` ct `lstv/nifti`, `varian/nifti`, `seg/<case>/ct_def.nii.gz`, else `no_nifti`, mask `lstv/seg`, `data/spineps/cobra_varian`, `data/anomaly_harvest/cobra*/spineps`, never `seg/spineps`; `carmlspine` ct from `data/carmlspine/cases.csv`, mask `data/carmlspine/spineps`, `anomaly_harvest/carmlspine*/spineps`; `ctspine1k` and `ribfrac` as other_ct_screen.py:164-174, :253-256 lay them out; **any other collection**: ct from `anomaly_harvest/<collection>/cases.csv` (columns case, ct, acquisition_screen.py:279-283, where a source already in NIfTI keeps its raw path, :231) first, then `data/<collection>/nifti/<case>_ct.nii.gz`, `no_ct` only after both; mask and ctd `anomaly_harvest/<collection>/spineps/<case>_seg-vert_msk.nii.gz`, `<case>_ctd.json`. `deepfluoro`, `geneva`, `spinesac`, `ljubljana` resolve to nothing in this version: `unknown_collection`, counted.
- `record_flags(registry, harvest) -> dict[person, (ls, subtype)]`: `junction_ls_in_fov` and `subtype` per `patient_key` from registry.csv, and from every `anomaly_harvest/*/rows.csv` keyed by `registry_key(patient_key)` as anomaly_route.py:397-405 keys them, since harvest persons are not in the registry.
- `census(rows)`: `nifti_label.read_header` of every `ok` row's CT for `voxels` and `dims`; a header that cannot be read is `no_nifti`.

### 4.2 `guard-list` and the guard

`guard-list` writes the `mask` of every `ok` row whose `mask_kind` is `spineps`, deduplicated. `read_guard(path) -> dict[mask, verdict]`: an `OK   ` line splits on the last `: `; a `FAIL ` line names the path before `: SPINEPS ids` and the verdict in the brackets (spineps_ids.py:413, :427); masks not listed are `guard_unlisted`. Refusals are counted by verdict, `veridah_skipped_name` on its own line.

### 4.3 `rewrite`

`rewrite --rows CSV --guard TXT --masks DIR --out DIR` -> `masks.csv` (person, case, sample_id, src, dst, status, ids_in, ids_out, dropped, metal_voxels, metal_ml, top, top_from, sacrum_from, gap, countable, complete, voxels, dims, seconds) and `## masks`.

- `sample_id(collection, case)`: `f"{collection}__{safe(case)}"`, never starting with `_` (packaging.py:566-567).
- `ctd_ids(path)`: the integer keys of `J[-1]` (spineps_ids.py:149-174); empty and counted `no_ctd` when missing.
- `rewrite_one`: skip when `masks_dir/<sample_id>.json` records the source's size and mtime and the written file's size (resume); else `nifti_label.rewrite` against the CT's header, then the sidecar.
- `countable_of(ids_out, ctd, ls_record)`: as item 5; `gap` when an id of `PRESACRAL` between the highest and lowest present is absent; `complete = countable and top_from == sacrum_from == "mask" and not gap`.
- Timing: `seconds` per mask; median, least, most and the sum over the masks rewritten in this run.

### 4.4 `validation`, `split` and `trainval`

- `validation_rank(person, salt)`: `sha256(f"schwingnet-anomaly-validation|{salt}|{person}")[:8]` big-endian over `2**64` (anomaly_route.py:37, :71-74).
- `draw_validation(pool, rule) -> set`: per variant, the persons with the lowest rank up to `rule["counts"][variant]`, the holdout's model (anomaly_route.py:38); a variant whose pool is smaller gives what it has, the shortfall counted. The pool: countable train-role persons, not distrusted, not G3-excluded, **with no fold** (the 73 fold persons are the folds' test material, anomaly-holdout.json `folds`).
- `g3_key(person)` as make_split.norm_key (make_split.py:152-171); `g3_side(person, g3)`: `k = g3_key(person)`; `rep = raw_to_group.get(person) or raw_to_group.get(k) or group_of.get(k) or k`; `side_of_person.get(rep)` (OUTPUT CONTRACT :54-57). ctspine1k and ribfrac persons have no side (open question 10).
- `assign(row, measured, g3_side, drawn, distrusted, uncounted_mode)`: role `holdout` -> `holdout`; role `test` -> `test`; role `train`: distrusted -> `uncounted/distrusted`; not countable -> `uncounted/uncountable`; `(subtype not in ORDINARY or variant in TRANSITIONAL) and g3_side in {val, test}` -> `g3_excluded`; `person in drawn` -> `val`; else `train`. With `--uncounted drop` the uncounted rows leave the list.
- `split --rows --masks --g3 JSON --validation JSON --out [--distrust ctsp] [--uncounted pack]` -> `splits.csv` (sample_id, person, split, why, countable, complete, variant, subtype, collection, role, fold) and `## splits`: per collection, role, split and completeness: rows, L6, T13, four_lumbar, both_L6_T13, countable; the G3 line (`N persons excluded, M of them variant normal`); the validation line (`rule, salt, counts, drawn per variant with shortfalls, sha256 <16 hex of the sorted drawn persons joined by newlines>`); `subject_disjoint` asserted per package (exit 1, as packaging.py:370-376 would).
- `trainval --splits CSV --out DIR` writes `trainval.csv` (person) of the `train` and `val` rows for `tools/anomaly_route.py check --holdout ... --list` (anomaly_route.py:468-470, :509-512), which the route note names as a pack step (:115).

### 4.5 `manifest` and `size`

- `manifest --splits --masks --rows --dataset schwingnet_spine|schwingnet_spine_partial --hu -1000,2000 [--crops X,Y,Z] --out DIR` writes `manifest-<dataset>.yml` as JSON text (JSON is YAML; `yaml.safe_load` reads it, packaging.py:316). Top-level keys exactly `schema_version: 1`, `dataset_name`, `dataset_type: "ct-seg"`, `stored_labels` (28 names, ids contiguous from 0), `samples`, `preprocessing: {"hu_window": [-1000, 2000]}`; **no `protocol_name`** (item 12); `crops` only with the switch. Each sample: `sample_id`, `subject_id` (the person), `split`, `image` and `label` absolute, `metadata` {person, collection, case, variant, subtype, tier, role, fold, countable, completeness, top_from, sacrum_from, gap, mask_kind, why}; no `affine`/`spacing`. Prints `## manifest`: samples per split and the manifest's sha256; a dataset with no sample writes no manifest and says so.
- `size --rows CSV --landing DIR` reads rows.csv alone (the manifest does not exist yet) and prints one last line `<total> <need> <how>` for `size_and_admit` (size-rule.sh:1056-1066): `total` = Sigma over `ok` rows of `voxels * 4` (float16 image and uint8 label in the package, uint8 mask file under the landing, all uncompressed; thunderpack stores arrays as msgpack + lz4, thunderpack/common.py:63-76, so this over-admits, which is safe) + 64 per row; `need` = total less the bytes of masks whose sidecar matches their source and file, never less a package; `how` = `header census of N CTs: float16 image, uint8 label and uint8 mask, uncompressed`. A row whose uncompressed payload passes 3 GiB is refused `payload_too_large` and counted, since lmdbm doubles its map at most twelve times from 1 MiB inside one write (lmdbm.py:58, :121-136).
