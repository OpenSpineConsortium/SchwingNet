# FleXray paper facts for the pack and tune stages (arXiv 2609.26756v2, source uploaded by the owner 2026-10-26 00:28 UTC)

Source: the LaTeX source extracted at the extracted source of arXiv 2609.26756v2 (not in git; see README.md) (main.tex, tables/). Read as data.

- Preprocessing (main.tex line 440, section "Preprocessing"): each CT volume is divided into overlapping 512 x 512 x 256 crops to cut disk-to-RAM transfer during online DRR rendering; a crop with z < 256 is padded with the HU of air; crop overlap is limited to 50 percent; each crop stores the CT, the label map, the affine and the foreground centroids used to sample DRR views. MOOSE, HaN-Seg and Shoulder-CT were segmented with MOOSE-Z; RSNAFrac got a skull label from TotalSegmentator; PedsCT from RTSTRUCT; ElbowCT from STL meshes.
- Splits (line 442): CT-derived data in fixed 90/10 train/validation splits with no test set; every split subject-disjoint and fixed across experiments; evaluation only on held-out real X-ray sets.
- Training data (tables/methods-training-data.tex): MOOSE 1,437 / 160 / 0 CTs with whole-body skeleton labels (skull, long bones, ribs 1-12, vertebrae C1-L5, hips, sacrum) and organs; the MOOSE and enhanced-DRR streams together fill 75 percent of every batch; the supplemental CT and real X-ray sources the remaining 25 percent. No L6, no T13 anywhere.
- Loss (lines 473-480): soft Dice on foreground channels present; BCE on every channel unless the source is partially labelled, in which case BCE runs only on channels with annotated pixels, and channels with no annotation are excluded rather than supervised as background. Targeted negative supervision reinstates BCE on channels anatomically impossible in the image for five partially labelled sources. For the tune stage: a vertebra-only pack goes on the partial_labeled route; negative supervision on impossible channels (hand and foot bones, long bones of the limbs) can be reinstated for a spine pack.
- Online DRR geometry (tables/methods-online-drr.tex): MOOSE isocenter at a random label, SDD 1150-1250 mm, 1.25-2.25 mm per pixel, cone 0.9 / orthographic 0.1, detector 256 x 256, alpha in [-180, 180], beta in [-30, 30], translations x [-100, 100], y [500, 900], z [-100, 100] mm.
- Optimization (line 623): 250 epochs of 50 iterations at batch size 16, 256 x 256, AdamW peak 3e-4, weight decay 3e-5, 5-epoch warm-up; every model evaluated at its final epoch without checkpoint selection.
- Enhanced DRRs (lines 393-414): 90 DRRs per MOOSE CT from a fixed grid of C-arm poses, refined offline with flux.2-klein-9b; a U-Net trained on raw DRRs alone filters the pairs.
