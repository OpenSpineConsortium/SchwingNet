When a message or a repository text gives a count of things, say what it is made of. On 2026-10-27 the phrase "the 36 specimen-summary numbers in three papers' Methods still need two prose passes" made greg fear the Panjabi specimens were in doubt. They are not: all four Panjabi papers (1991 cervical and thoracic, 1992 lumbar, 1993 facets) use the same twelve C2-L5 spines, S3 to S24 in the thoracic paper's Table 1; the 36 are one 12-number summary (12 spines, 8 men, 4 women, mean/min/max of age, weight, height) printed in each of the three later papers. Their only disagreement with Table 1 is the weight range, 54-85 kg printed against 84 kg for S15.

**Why:** greg checks the science by the words; an unexplained number or a deferred item reads as a hidden problem.
**How to apply:** write "one 12-number summary printed in three papers (36 numbers)", not "36 numbers"; when deferring something, say in the same sentence that it is bookkeeping and what is already verified. Related: [[owner-working-style]].
