**Why:** a vertebral level name is verified only by a count from C2 to the sacrum; a lumbopelvic or abdominal CT cannot give that count, so an L6 or T13 call on it is a phenotype, not truth. TotalSegmentator masks are not the lab's segmentation; SPINEPS is.

**How to apply:** every reported number rests on CTs whose field holds C2 (or C1) and the sacrum; rows without that field (CTSpinoPelvic1K among them, in the anomaly holdout too) are reported apart or left out, never pooled into a headline. Masks come from SPINEPS (the anomaly harvest's spineps/ folders, the VERIDAH-named stores) or from a release's expert masks (VerSe); never from TotalSegmentator's seg/ folders; a CT that already has a SPINEPS mask is never re-run.
