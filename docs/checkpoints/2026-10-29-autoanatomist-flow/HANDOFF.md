## 3. bordercall: the blocks and the resumable session (to build first)

- autoanatomist/blocks.py: every training part as KEY=VALUE, parsed and refused in words, canonical words and a digest naming the configuration; no block named is the smoke run to the bit. Keys and defaults: optimiser adamw|adam|sgd (adamw), lr 0.002, weight_decay 0.0001 (optax's), clip 0 (global norm, 0 is none), warmup 0, batch 2 (phantoms a batch), knee 2.0 mm, spread_floor 0.5 mm, w_landmark 1, w_spread 0.1, w_value 1, w_feature 1, w_naming 0.1, naming on|off, schedule lambda-then-w|joint|w-first|fixed, lam_span 0.15:0.55, w_span 0.45:0.85, lam_fixed 0, w_fixed 1, anneal 0 (the steps the spans are shares of; 0 takes the first budget), intensity off|gain-offset|gain-offset-noise (host-side HU gain, offset and noise on the encoder's input only, never on the HU the ladder thresholds), normalise clip|clip-zscore (a normalise field in model.Config), widths 16:32:64, groups 8, blur_mm 4, threshold_hu 200 (gates.BONE_HU), threshold_width_hu 60, calibrate none|scale|quantile, coverage 0.68, seed 0.
- autoanatomist/session.py: a Session built from blocks that trains from its step, saves itself as plain arrays (nnx.split, nnx.to_flat_state, one key per leaf, model and optimizer alike, into state.npz; the step, the schedule, the numpy Generator's bit-generator state, the calibration and the blocks' record into session.json) and is restored from them, so a search's trial, a continued annealing and a run on more data are one session taken further. The schedule is an Anneal in absolute steps (lambda from 0 to 1, w from 1 to 0, half cosines over spans of `anneal` steps, by the schedule's kind); continue_anneal(until) rebases both ramps from the current (w, lambda) at the current step to their ends by `until`, which is how "continue annealing" works without a new run. Examples come from rows (kind phantom: id, phenotype, start, count, seed, spacing, sub, noise, read by the stand-in oracle; kind volume: a CT and a label NIfTI with its names, read by the oracle, the engine's export once PR 30 lands) and are cached as an npz keyed by the rows' digest, so adding data is appending rows. train.py takes the weights, the knee, the floor and the augmented input as arguments with the defaults unchanged.
- Calibration, fit on validation only and applied unchanged afterwards: per measure k, with s = sqrt(sigma^2 + 0.1^2) the delta-method spread floored, z = |error| / s over the validation crops; scale: factor sqrt(mean z^2) (the Gaussian likelihood's closed form); quantile: factor quantile(z, coverage) / Phi^-1((1 + coverage) / 2), split-conformal, so plus or minus one calibrated spread covers `coverage` of the validation errors. evaluate reports the landmark distance, each value's MAE, the raw and calibrated spreads, the coverage at one and two calibrated spreads, and bodies named right three ways: with the calibrated spread (the reward's), from the values alone, from the oracle's values.

## 4. SchwingNet: the Metaflow-native search (flows/autoanatomist.py, kind autoanatomist)

- Registry line: autoanatomist AutoanatomistFlow AUTOANATOMIST_RC autoanatomist-driver.sbatch docs/research/autoanatomist-search/PREREG.md. The launcher's words: --plan grid/searches/<plan>.json --out <search root> --environment train-gpu; key_reads names the plan's bytes and prereg_part returns (plan name, sha256), as flows/sweep.py does, so the PREREG's Covers line holds each plan by its bytes (the first plan phantom_smoke).
- The plan file: the search id; the data source (phantom rows by seeds, or a folder of volume rows) and the split shares (validation and test by the salted person hash of data/splits/rule.json's form, the test part read once at the end by the best trial only); the block space (per key, the values the controller may choose); the rungs (steps per rung, for example 100, 300, 900; the share kept, one half); the trials a round; the controller's settings (learning rate, baseline decay, entropy floor); the objective's weights; the W&B project; the job class.
- The ledger, <out>/<search id>/ledger.json, is what tells Metaflow where to resume: the plan's history (each run's plan digest and data digest), every trial (id, blocks' words and digest, parent, checkpoint folder, step, (w, lambda) at that step, rung results on validation with their metrics, W&B run id), every round's (configuration, reward) pairs, the controller's policy (one categorical per block over its allowed values, as logits) and its baseline, the best trial by the validation objective, and the data manifest's rows with their split. Metaflow's own resume handles a cut job; a new run with --continue reads the ledger and carries the search on: another controller round, more steps on kept trials (continue_anneal where the plan's anneal grew), or the same with rows appended to the manifest (new volumes are split by hash, no checkpoint is thrown away since the network's shape does not depend on the data, and every trial's next rung trains on the grown train part). A trial's checkpoint is the session's state.npz and session.json, so a trial resumes at its recorded step with its schedule position and optimizer.
- Steps: start (hold_to_prereg with the plan as the part; load or create the ledger; refresh the manifest and split; make or reuse the examples cache; Beat line "search <id>, round <n>, <trials> trials") -> propose (the controller samples the round's configurations from its policy, or takes the plan's seeds in round 0, writing them into the ledger) -> fan_1 / trial_1 (foreach; the session trains in the step itself, importing the vendored autoanatomist; W&B through tools/wandb_log.py's Telemetry with the trial's id, resume allow, offline pushed by the flow where no key is found; the Beat line moves with the step and the last loss) -> join_1 (successive halving on the validation objective, the losers retired with their checkpoints kept) -> fan_2 / trial_2 / join_2 -> fan_3 / trial_3 / join_3 (three rungs unrolled, since Metaflow's graph has no loop; a rung whose list is empty is stepped past by a conditional next, as flows/sweep.py does) -> update (REINFORCE on the factored categorical policy with an exponential-moving-average baseline over every recorded round, an entropy floor so no value is ever certain, the policy and the (configuration, reward) pairs into the ledger; the best trial's test part read once, written beside it) -> end (still_held; the ledger's summary; the report; stage.finish). One flow run is one controller round; the gate word submits the next round as a continuation, so a reinforcement run is a chain of launched flows over one ledger.
- The reward on validation: bodies named right with the calibrated spread, less a weight times the landmark distance in millimetres, less a weight times the coverage's departure from its nominal, the weights the plan's; latency per crop with its p95 is measured per trial and recorded, never rewarded until the owner sets its bar.
- The report: tools/report.py reads a run folder of tools/flow.py's shape (RUN.json, train/, evaluate/, PROVENANCE.json); a search folder is another shape, so either the best trial writes a run folder in that shape at the end or report.py takes a study mode for the search, as it took one for the DRR study; this is open and is decided before the PREREG is frozen.
- The environment: no change to pixi.toml or pixi.lock; grid/venv.sh's venv_for with the pins of bordercall's requirements-autoanatomist.txt (jax 0.12.0.1 as jax[cuda12] on a GPU node, flax 0.12.12, optax 0.2.8) over train-gpu's packages, which hold metaflow, numpy and wandb, and the launcher run with that venv's python, so the flow's steps import autoanatomist in their own interpreter; XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false where --max-workers is above 1.
- The pieces around the flow, each as the other launched flows have them: grid/autoanatomist-driver.sbatch in the shape of grid/v1-driver.sbatch (the launcher in the background and waited on, the decision line, the rc line AUTOANATOMIST_RC); the gate word `autoanatomist <plan>` with its refusals (no such plan, a run of the plan live, the PREREG not covering the plan's bytes), its class from the plan, and `autoanatomist <plan> continue` for the next round; tools/run_code.py's kind autoanatomist (the flow, flows/stage.py, flows/stage_registry.py, src/schwingnet/autoanatomist_search.py, grid/bordercall/autoanatomist/ and the driver) with its LEFT_OUT reasons; the pure logic (plan parsing, the ledger's round trip, the split, the halving, the controller's update, the objective) in src/schwingnet/autoanatomist_search.py under beethoven-qa's contracts with pytest tests under tests/schwingnet/, and the flow tested through the launcher with a stand-in autoanatomist package, as tests/test_realism_flow.py stands in for tools/realism.py; docs/research/autoanatomist-search/ with PREREG.md (the seven parts, the question being which blocks give the lowest validation landmark error with a calibrated spread and the most bodies named right on phantoms, the bars none, as the owner chose for version 1), PREREG.md.sha256 with Covers: phantom_smoke=<sha256>, README.md and prior-art.md; a line in docs/research/README.md, a bullet in flows/README.md, a memory file with its MEMORY.md line; tests/test_gate.py for the word. scripts/check.sh before the pull request.
