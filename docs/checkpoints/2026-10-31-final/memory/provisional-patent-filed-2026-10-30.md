Greg's provisional patent application was filed about 2026-10-30 through Wayne State tech transfer's outside counsel (docket 66174-0372; Tech ID 26-1901; US 64/188,238; NIH F30 5F30GM147909 named as government support). Title: "System and method for determining vertebral level from limited-field projection images". This is SchwingNet's provisional, the one docs/project.md waited for before the CNS talk, not a competitor's.

Filing form (read from the PDFs' second XFA data copy): sole inventor Gregory John Schwing; applicant Wayne State University as assignee; attorney Kameron F. Bonner, Fishman Stewart PLLC; signed 2026-10-30.

Greg's account (00:17 UTC): he conceived it months after graduating, on guest access to WSU computing, and signed nothing; he only submitted an invention disclosure. Open questions for his own attorney: the guest-account terms, the disclosure form's wording, and the F30 government-interest line (an F30 normally ends at graduation).

Conception record: the claimed sweep first appears 2026-09-17 22:11 EDT (spinesurg-ct-nnunet L6_Class 6984446); F30 ended 2026-05-31. Timeline for his attorney: /mnt/project-files/patent-ownership/conception-timeline.md.

What it covers: overlapping C-arm sweep (about 2 bodies of overlap) linked into a chain by image content, consecutive naming anchored on a landmark (sacrum or C2), confidence = lowest probability x link margin, a commanded move in mm from the patient's own vertebral spacing, persistent digital marker, biplanar check, and a CT classifier that flags transitional anatomy from any segmenter's masks.

What it does NOT cover: the bordercall #51 to #58 direction of 2026-10-30 (a 2D landmark set that names a single vertebra from one limited field, Panjabi phantoms with the gold-tier correlation prior). That is new matter.
