Full text with sources: FINDINGS.md sections 6 to 8, beside this folder; SchwingNet docs/research/flexray-audit-companion.md, docs/research/flux-realism/README.md, docs/research/drr-domain-gap/README.md.

- FleXray (the owner's fork OpenSpineConsortium/FleXray, upstream 1.0.2): one 2D UNet at 256 px with 61 sigmoid channels, one per vertebra C1 to L5 plus the sacrum and other anatomy; no L6 or T13 channel (training sends L6 to background, so a sixth lumbar body was learnt as not a vertebra); routed losses, 2,000 epochs, an ensemble of five. Code MIT; weights and FluXray CC BY-NC 4.0; the FLUX.2 edit is not in the repository.
- On C-ArmLSpine (Slurm 40781570, commit fc027a6), given the truth boxes, the flagship named 99.14 percent of 1,627 boxes (Wilson 98.56 to 99.49), the ensemble 99.63 percent, flux000 (trained with no FluXray) 98.22: on these frames FluXray is worth at most about 0.9 points. It is naming given a body, not detection; the split by view is unread; L6 boxes were left out. The reported stack, for comparison, names 96.4 percent of detected lateral bodies zero-shot and 43.7 percent of AP.
- #391, settled by the owner on 2026-10-25 20:49 UTC: the window is the exposure field in vertebral bodies and the offset the commanded step; the test sweeps 0.5 to 4 bodies of overlap in quarter steps (15 settings); the non-optimal settings are the minima of accuracy left and right of the maxima, rounded to half bodies; nine-inch field (140 mm) main, twelve-inch secondary. "The other one" is arXiv 2412.08020's 80 cadaver frames. The linker (spine-program's sweep_map.py) must be ported into SchwingNet first (item 13).
- FluXray: 138,063 edited renders kept of 143,730 (1,597 MOOSE-segmented ENHANCE.PET CTs x 90 poses). CSAIL trained no realism model: FLUX.2 klein-9B ran zero-shot (4 steps, bf16, guidance 1.0, one prompt, SDD 1250 mm, 0.6 mm pixels at 1024, stored at 256), filtered by a paired soft-Dice rule whose raw-only U-Net is unreleased. Landed on the grid 2026-10-26 (Slurm 40788810, 19 GB); never fetch it again.
- Flux realism programme: reproduce the edit on ENHANCE.PET CTs at FluXray's poses, measure the gap at 256 px with one verdict word per feature space, train a LoRA on klein-base-4B by subject split (src/schwingnet/realism.py, tools/realism.py, grid word `realism <stage>`: edit, stats, lora, eval). Code on main (#406, #417); 5 fetches and 4 GPU jobs approved, none sent; klein-9B needs the owner's licence click; no realism word passes hpcworker's gate until an hpcworker PR admits it, then `update`. ENHANCE.PET is non-commercial in data/licences.json (autoPET and Tuebingen sites CC BY-NC 4.0).
- DRR comparison: 22 groups (data/domain_gap/groups.json), six render groups on the same 250 CTs; C2ST AUC with a person bootstrap, MMD, FD64 and KID64, precision/recall/density/coverage, split-half calibration; "replicated" only where the C2ST AUC's lower bound is at most 0.6 and the MMD's q at least 0.05; twins of one CT are a paired sample. No result yet: the seg job 40799505 was running at 2026-10-27 04:08 UTC; the gap job is unsent; the mask-free version (owner, 04:15 UTC) is SchwingNet #418, draft.
- Renders transfer for naming, not detection (textbook chapters 12 and 13): a render-only detector finds 71.67 percent of bodies on film against 99.14 for a film-trained one, and with its boxes count exactness fell from 100 to 30.06 percent on 79 spines.
