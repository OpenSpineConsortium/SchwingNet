The auto-anatomist screen runs on the grid as the word `autoanatomist <plan>`, the smoke on the owner's count of 2026-10-30 03:20 UTC and the screen's two jobs on his word of 08:56 UTC, each plan sent once.

- The flow is AutoanatomistFlow (flows/autoanatomist.py), a trial or the join of a plan under grid/screens/, held to docs/research/autoanatomist-screen/PREREG.md and to the plan's bytes; the job script is grid/autoanatomist.sbatch and the network bordercall's copy under grid/autoanatomist/ (tools/bordercall_vendor.py copy, never a hand edit).
- grid/gate.sh queues a plan only where AUTOANATOMIST_COUNTED names it, sends gold3_latent_screen only once the smoke's report ends in exit 0 (AUTOANATOMIST_AFTER), and sends a plan again only after a fix moved the checkout, after a send Slurm ended before any part started, or on the owner's word in AUTOANATOMIST_RERUNS; gold3_latent_smoke is one GPU job, gold3_latent_screen a GPU array of eight trials and a CPU join afterany it, the join waiting for the setup as the trials do.
- Constraint: the grid's train-gpu Python is 3.11, so the trials' venv pins jax[cuda12] 0.10.2, flax 0.12.8, optax 0.2.8 and orbax-checkpoint 0.12.8 (schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.PINS, held by tests/test_gate.py).
- Constraint: flows/launch.py reads a relative path parameter from the folder it started in, so the job passes every path absolute, the plan as its own copy's.
- The first run takes normal spines alone, the store's class normal confirmed by the masks (the owner, 2026-10-30 09:43 UTC); the anomalous spines are split by family across test and training by the latent-prior evaluation, for later runs.
- The plans keep one person split with the latent-prior evaluation: every test list of schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.TEST_LISTS (data/splits/test-persons.json and that evaluation's reserve, data/splits/latent-prior-test-reserve.json) stays out of them, and every study of a unit of probable repeat scans (UNIT_LISTS, data/splits/latent-prior-repeat-groups.json) keeps its unit's part; tools/autoanatomist_plan.py check, in scripts/check.sh under pixi and in the gate lane's tests/schwingnet/test_autoanatomist_screen.py, fails a plan that breaks either, and each run's RECORD.json carries the lists' sha256.
- Decision: the null trains with w_feature=0, the CT branch alone on the mask and name terms, since at w 0 the mask encoder takes no gradient and the feature term would pull the CT encoder towards a random network's features; a trial whose loss, weights or CT-only outputs stop being finite stops with exit 1 and entry_problems refuses a reading with no finite loss, so a diverged trial is failed, never a finite worst distance (the second review, 2026-10-30).
- Decision: the annealed arms keep the feature term to the last step, so once w reaches 0 it distils from a frozen teacher, KD-Net's form; w_first is read as a handover on clean masks followed by that distillation, not as the ladder's order, and a term that ends with the handover is a later pre-registration's, with a change to the vendored network (the third review, 2026-10-30).
- A plan's row names one study as its item and its person, and its source is one the gold 3 store spells (enhance_pet, totalsegmentator, verse), since the trial reads files by the item and the split's guards check the person (schwingnet.autoanatomist_screen.plan_problems).
