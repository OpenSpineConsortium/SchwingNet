Every fetch onto the lab volume reads its exact size from its source before it downloads a byte and is refused whole when it would pass the volume's CephFS quota after what the running fetches still owe; no floor and no margin (the owner, 2026-10-23, issue #160).

The owner's words: "just make sure any datasets that are selected for storage know exactly how much space they will require and block those which would cause overflow, all or nothing datasets", asked so that the quota of /rs/rs_grp_oschome can be filled without the 5 TB floor acquisition_fetch.sh kept.

- The room is the quota less its use, getfattr's ceph.quota.max_bytes and ceph.dir.rbytes on /rs/rs_grp_oschome (the acq environment's Python, XATTR_PYTHON, where getfattr is missing), read with no walk. Never df or statvfs: on CephFS they report the whole Ceph pool, 5,881,937 GB free on 2026-10-23 under a quota of 21,991 GB; where no reader runs nothing is read, and df answers only on a mount `stat -f` names as neither ceph nor fuse. A value is read up to its first NUL and taken only as a count of at most 18 digits (is_count): on 2026-10-23 the owner's own getfattr printed the volume's ceph.dir.rbytes as 48 digits and ceph.dir.rfiles as 25, which bash's arithmetic wraps without a word (the use read as less than nothing and the room as 2.5 EB, so every fetch would have been admitted); a reading that is no count admits nothing, and the readout names the attribute. WSU's `df-ceph oschome` prints the same quota as 20 TB (it is 20 TiB) with its use and share, and acquisition_fetch.py's own floor reads it.
- The need is what the fetch will land, read by grid/fetch_size.py from the Hugging Face tree, the Zenodo record, the S3 listing (every object under the prefix, which s5cmd syncs whatever --include says) or idc-index's series sizes, a zip by its central directory and a .tar by its own size once its first block shows a plain tar, with room for the largest archive while it unpacks, less what an earlier try landed whole; the curl-link route reads a one-byte range's Content-Range. A source it cannot size exactly, a compressed tar under either name or a server that answers no range, is refused, and the gdown, Kaggle (the competition route and #165's kaggle-dataset) and Redivis routes have no reader yet and are refused until issue #163.
- grid/size-rule.sh decides inside the job, under a flock on <data root>/.size-rule.lock: admitted when the need fits the room less what is owed, and owed sums, over the fetches squeue still holds, what each admitted one has left to land (meta/SIZE beside its landing), an X-ray chain's fetch whole at the sizes grid/fetch-xray.json states (grid/logs/XRAY_SIZES.txt), and a platform fetch the gate sent before the rule (no meta/QUEUED) at its line's stated size less what it landed. A live fetch nothing sizes, a squeue that does not answer or a landing whose bytes cannot be read admits nothing. A job Slurm requeued (SLURM_RESTART_COUNT above 0 and its own id in meta/SIZE) keeps its admission and is not decided again. The X-ray chain is decided at submit under the same lock, held until its sizes are recorded, at the list's sum rounded up to the whole GB. A refusal writes meta/REFUSED and ends the job with exit 4 and one line the status word keeps; acquisition_fetch.sh runs with FLOOR_TB=0, so the rule stops nothing part-way.
- The readout: `bash ~/SchwingNet/grid/oschome-quota.sh` on the login node prints the room in the layout of WSU's wsuquota, which reads only the home directory, then what is owed and the room a new fetch has, and a file limit where the quota sets one; the gate word status opens with the same block, and the data and shared totals it lists under the block are two folders of the volume, not its use, which nothing read before #161. Both call quota_readout, the reading the rule decides by, so the readout and an admission never disagree. The line `if [ -f ~/SchwingNet/grid/wsuquota.bash ]; then . ~/SchwingNet/grid/wsuquota.bash; fi` in ~/.bashrc makes WSU's `wsuquota` print the same block after its own (the owner's ask of 2026-10-23), whatever wsuquota is in that shell, an alias, a function or the command, exported or not; the hook runs no cd or pwd, which a ~/.bashrc may redefine, and its own names all start _schwingnet_wsuquota, since the original runs inside it.
- Not counted, so a set can still stop part-way for them: the bank and catalog stages' writes, the jobs' TMPDIR on the volume, other users' writes, how far rbytes lags a landing and a file limit; an X-ray set whose size the list leaves null counts as nothing (#163); and a fetch sent before the rule runs the script Slurm copied, with the 5 TB floor, until it ends or is cancelled and sent again.
