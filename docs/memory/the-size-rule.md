Every fetch onto the lab volume reads its exact size from its source before it downloads a byte and is refused whole when it would pass the volume's CephFS quota after what the running fetches still owe; no floor and no margin (the owner, 2026-10-23, issue #160).

The owner's words: "just make sure any datasets that are selected for storage know exactly how much space they will require and block those which would cause overflow, all or nothing datasets", asked so that the quota of /rs/rs_grp_oschome can be filled without the 5 TB floor acquisition_fetch.sh kept.

- The room is the quota less its use, getfattr's ceph.quota.max_bytes and ceph.dir.rbytes on /rs/rs_grp_oschome (os.getxattr where getfattr is missing), read with no walk. Never df or statvfs: on CephFS they report the whole Ceph pool, 5,881,937 GB free on 2026-10-23 beside the lab's 8,845 under a quota of 21,991 GB.
- The need is what the fetch will land, read by grid/fetch_size.py from the Hugging Face tree, the Zenodo record, the S3 listing or idc-index's series sizes, a zip by its central directory with room for the largest archive while it unpacks, less what an earlier try landed whole; the curl-link route reads a one-byte range's Content-Range. A source it cannot size exactly, a compressed tar or a server that answers no range, is refused, and the gdown, Kaggle and Redivis routes have no reader yet and are refused until issue #163.
- grid/size-rule.sh decides inside the job, under a flock on <data root>/.size-rule.lock: admitted when the need fits the room less what is owed, and owed sums, over the fetches squeue still holds, what each admitted one has left to land (meta/SIZE beside its landing), an X-ray chain's fetch whole at the sizes grid/fetch-xray.json states (grid/logs/XRAY_SIZES.txt), and a platform fetch the gate sent before the rule (no meta/QUEUED) at its line's stated size less what it landed. A live fetch nothing sizes, or a squeue that does not answer, admits nothing. A refusal writes meta/REFUSED and ends the job with exit 4 and one line the status word keeps; acquisition_fetch.sh runs with FLOOR_TB=0, so nothing stops part-way.
- The readout: `bash ~/SchwingNet/grid/oschome-quota.sh` on the login node prints the room in the layout of WSU's wsuquota, which reads only the home directory, then what is owed and the room a new fetch has; the gate word status opens with the same block. Both call quota_readout, the reading the rule decides by, so the readout and an admission never disagree.
- Not counted: the bank and catalog stages' writes, other users' writes, and how far rbytes lags a landing; an X-ray set whose size the list leaves null counts as nothing (#163).
- Tests: tests/test_fetch_size.py, tests/test_xray_chain.py (SizeRuleTest, QuotaReadoutTest) and tests/test_gate.py.
