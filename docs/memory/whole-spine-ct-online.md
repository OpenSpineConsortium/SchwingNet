What the public sites hold of whole-spine CT against the grid, and what to fetch next (2026-10-31).

The catalogue is docs/research/whole-spine-ct-online/: IDC v25 counted by CT field length from idc-index (2,852 persons at 60 cm or more, 2,195 in collections the grid holds, 657 in about 155 GB not, roughly half of the 70 cm persons passing rule 3 by the census's yield), and the sites outside IDC. NMDID (about 15,000 post-mortem whole-body CTs with living height, weight, age and sex for every person) was excluded by the data platform for its DUA and is reopened by the owner's word of 2026-10-31 that licences do not matter; its registration and research request are the owner's. autoPET's longitudinal CT (300 Tuebingen melanoma patients, 54 GB) is not in the records and needs a person-overlap check against FDG-PET-CT-Lesions. Nothing was fetched; each fetch waits for the owner's word. idc-index runs in a scratch virtual environment, never as a repository dependency.
