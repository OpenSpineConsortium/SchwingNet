The Artifact Textbook is a website built into the members-only docs site under textbook/ by tools/textbook_site.py, from the artifacts and the synthesis's records, on every push to main and once a day; nothing of it goes public before the provisional is filed, and then only an allowlist the owner approves.

The owner asked on 2026-10-24 for the textbook as "a living textbook website composed of public artefacts" (issue #196). It is laid out, with no model and no new dependency, from the table of contents' JSON (artifacts/_synthesis/work/out/toc-hand.json), the inventory, the clustering and the concept ledger: a page per chapter, which shows the chapter's narrative from docs/textbook/ once one is written and below it every artifact the chapter reads with its record, its place in its cluster and the figures the ledger has settled; a page per artifact, with the artifact itself served beside it from artifacts/ and its claude.ai link; the synthesis's pages; and a page of what the book does not hold yet (a folder with no record, an artifact changed since its record was read, a record no chapter reads). Each build runs the ledger's own checks (artifacts/_synthesis/work/tools/synth_concepts.py) on every narrative, with a figure's anchors counted equally rather than by rarity, and flags a superseded or open value given without its disclaimer.

.github/workflows/docs.yml builds it after the docs site and deploys both with Pages, whose private site (the pages.github.io name GitHub gives an access-controlled site) only people who can read the repository open. The daily run at 08:23 UTC is there because a merge GitHub's auto-merge makes starts no workflow, and the synthesis's pull requests merge that way.

The owner chose on 2026-10-24 that a public edition waits until the provisional is filed (by 30 October), and then carries only artifacts on an allowlist the owner approves, Part III's method chapters last, once counsel says the method may be shown: a method made public before the filing date can cost the patent outside the United States. The inventory's confidentiality tag of each artifact (schwingnet-method, schwingnet-other, mixed, symphony-beethoven, personal-outreach) is where an allowlist starts, never the allowlist itself.

To preview it from a clone: `python3 tools/textbook_site.py _textbook`, then open `_textbook/index.html`; `--root` builds from another checkout, such as a branch's worktree holding chapters not yet merged.
