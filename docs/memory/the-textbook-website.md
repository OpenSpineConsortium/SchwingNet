The Artifact Textbook is a website built into the members-only docs site under textbook/ by tools/textbook_site.py, from the synthesis's records of the artifacts, whenever the owner runs the Docs workflow; nothing of it goes public before the provisional is filed, and then only an allowlist the owner approves.

The owner asked on 2026-10-24 for the textbook as "a living textbook website composed of public artefacts" (issue #196). It is laid out, with no model and no new dependency, from the table of contents' JSON (artifacts/_synthesis/work/out/toc-hand.json), the inventory, the clustering and the concept ledger: a page per chapter, which shows the chapter's narrative from docs/textbook/ once one is written and below it every artifact the chapter reads with its record, its place in its cluster and the figures the ledger has settled; a page per artifact, with its record and the links that open the artifact itself, on claude.ai and in the repository; the synthesis's pages; and a page of what the book does not hold yet (a folder with no record, an artifact changed since its record was read, a record no chapter reads). Each build runs the ledger's own checks (artifacts/_synthesis/work/tools/synth_concepts.py) on every narrative, with a figure's anchors counted equally rather than by rarity, and flags a superseded or open value whose line gives no disclaimer. The pages leave out what the records' own rules leave out (artifacts/_synthesis/work/tools/redact.py), and the owner's account names and a person named by title as well. The first version served no copy of an artifact, since an artifact's own text holds what the records leave out and its scripts would run at the site's own address; an adversarial review found both (PR #197). Since issue #233 (the owner's ask of 2026-10-24: the artifacts themselves, interactive, not screenshots) every exported artifact that has a page, index.html, is copied into the site under artifacts/<folder>/ with its tree, dotfiles and Markdown left out; the artifact's own page shows it above the record in an iframe sandboxed with allow-scripts and allow-popups and never the site's origin (the twelve scripted pages were read for what they need: storage in try/catch, no XMLHttpRequest, one page fetching its own source only for a save that needs the artifact runtime), with a link that opens it full-page, and each figure whose record traces it to such an artifact carries the button Open the artifact's page beside Explore and Re-run; a Docs artifact, exported as index.md alone, keeps its links. The pages are the owner's own and the site is members-only; their text is not put through the records' privacy pass.

.github/workflows/docs.yml builds it after the docs site and deploys both with Pages, whose private site (the pages.github.io name GitHub gives an access-controlled site) only people who can read the repository open; the deploy job asks GitHub before every deploy whether the site is public and deploys nothing unless the answer is that it is not. It runs only when the owner starts it (Actions, Docs, Run workflow on main), never on a push or a schedule, the owner's call of 2026-10-25 to save Actions minutes; until 2026-10-25 it built on every push to main and once a day at 08:23 UTC, the daily run there because a merge GitHub's auto-merge makes starts no workflow and the synthesis's pull requests merge that way, so what any merge brought now reaches the site at the owner's next run.

The owner chose on 2026-10-24 that a public edition waits until the provisional is filed (by 30 October), and then carries only artifacts on an allowlist the owner approves, Part III's method chapters last, once counsel says the method may be shown: a method made public before the filing date can cost the patent outside the United States. The inventory's confidentiality tag of each artifact (schwingnet-method, schwingnet-other, mixed, symphony-beethoven, personal-outreach) is where an allowlist starts, never the allowlist itself.

Every chapter page and every figure has an Ask Claude button (issue #220): a prefilled new issue whose body begins "@claude" and names the chapter or figure and its record, which .github/workflows/claude.yml answers for the owner, members and collaborators once the reader types the question and submits. The page research.html shows the research loop (docs/memory/research-loop.md): the claims register's queue with each item's status, the switch RESEARCH_LOOP and the cap RESEARCH_RUNS_PER_DAY.

To preview it from a clone, into a folder outside it so the clone stays clean: `python3 tools/textbook_site.py /tmp/textbook`, then open `/tmp/textbook/index.html`; `--root` builds from another checkout, such as a branch's worktree holding chapters not yet merged.

Figures (issue #215, the owner's words of 2026-10-24: "I WANT TO KNOW WHERE THE FIGURES CAME FROM SO I CAN ONE CLICK ON THE FIGURES TO RETRIGGER EXPERIMENTS OR AT LEAST EXPLORE FURTHER THE METHODS THAT GENERATED IT"): each lives under docs/textbook/figures/<chapter>/ as an image, or an interactive page of the same name, beside a JSON record of where it came from (artifact, script, data, commit, run, notes) and how it is drawn again (rerun.where: actions, grid or none). A chapter places one with an image line and an italic caption line, which the book's lint accepts and leaves out of a chapter's length. The site shows the figure with its grade, status and source panel, an Explore button (the method and the data) and a Re-run button, a prefilled issue labelled figure-rerun with the line `figure-rerun-id: NN/slug`; .github/workflows/figure.yml answers it with tools/figure_run.py from the committed record alone, for an author who may write: a runner figure is redrawn and comes back as a pull request, a grid figure gets its exact command and the grid workflow's link and nothing is submitted, since a grid job waits for the owner's word, and a figure with no command gets its Explore links. figures.html lists every figure with how far it is traced. The 83 figures first copied from the artifacts were traced through spine-program's manifests (MANIFEST_LOCAL.md rows of the same byte size), RESUME_2026-09-22_PM.md, RECIPES.md and FEASIBILITY_REPRODUCE.md; the scripts are in the private spinesurg-ct-nnunet, so none of them reruns in this repository's Actions.
