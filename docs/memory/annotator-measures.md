The oracle's eighteen landmarks make Panjabi's chords and the corner ratios but no wedge; through a real X-ray within 15 degrees of the vertebra's own view they keep their values, and past 25 the lengths across the turn do not (docs/research/annotator-landmarks/measures.md, 2026-11-01).

- The oracle (grid/autoanatomist/autoanatomist/oracle.py, scanimport's chords) sets all four points of a plate at its slab's median height in the vertebra's frame, so the eighteen points' anterior and posterior corner heights are equal but for the anterior ends' offset and their two depth chords are parallel: Genant's wedge and biconcavity ratios, the wedge angle, the inclinations, the concavity and the discs are read from the engine's rows (h_ant, h_mid, h_post_inset, body_index_ant_post, body_index_mid_post, wedge, endplate_incl, the concavities, disc_h_*), never from the eighteen points.
- The angle that matters is the ray's against the vertebra's own axes, not the export's view angle: DeepFluoro's pair frames, within 15 degrees of anteroposterior in the patient's axes, are a median 43 degrees off their vertebrae's own views.
- tools/annotator_measures.py `project`, `gold`, `census` and `report` reproduce the projection table, the gold tier's per-level aggregates and bordercall's census ranking in one CPU job; the census is read from a bordercall clone at main as a library, since it needs xarray, which the bench venv on the grid holds.
