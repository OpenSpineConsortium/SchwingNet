The anomaly route's pack stage (#391, stage 3) packs the selection as two whole-volume FleXray packages under the landing flexray_pack, every mask rewritten to 28 canonical ids, countable measured per CT, the validation rule frozen, the admission before any byte and no HOLD.

- The gate word `schwingnet-anomaly-pack` queues one CPU job (four CPUs, 64 GB, three days at most) once select's and timing's reports ended well, the validation rule, the spineps conda, spineps_ids.py, G3's split_final.json and the selection stand; it runs grid/anomaly.sbatch's pack branch, which copies tools/anomaly_pack.py, tools/nifti_label.py, tools/anomaly_route.py, grid/venv.sh, grid/box.sh, grid/size-rule.sh and grid/flexray/configs under anomaly/pack/code-<commit>/ and runs from there, so it holds no HOLD and a checkout that moved under it is a note.
- Masks: VerSe's and CTSpinoPelvic1K's release masks; every other row its existing relabelled SPINEPS copy, listed OK by spineps_ids.py check --strict in the spineps environment; never a raw-id or TotalSegmentator store and never a segmenter run (the owner, 2026-10-26: SPINEPS for production, MOOSE only when replicating FleXray's dataset, the segmenter a block named `segmenter`). tools/nifti_label.py, standard library, rewrites each to the 28 canonical ids (0 background, vertebra_c1..c7, t1..t12, t13, l1..l5, l6, sacrum), dropping the coccyx, the hips, the ribs and the metal marker 80 to background with counts, refusing float, scaled, 4-D, mismatched-grid and unknown-id masks by a closed set of reasons.
- Countable: C1 or C2 and the sacrum in the field by the mask, the record (junction_ls_in_fov) or the ctd; complete: both from the mask with no presacral gap. Complete masks go to schwingnet_spine, the rest to schwingnet_spine_partial, since FleXray routes its loss by dataset name. The manifest keeps FleXray 1.0.2's keys (schema_version 1, dataset_type ct-seg, stored_labels, preprocessing hu_window -1000..2000, samples) and binds no protocol; the protocol schwingnet_spine_v1 (65 labels) and the model labels (63) sit under grid/flexray/configs, and the stage compiles the remap before a GPU is spent.
- Splits: train, val, test (paired real-frame sets), holdout, uncounted (uncountable, or a distrusted collection: ctsp by default, the owner's word of 2026-10-26 00:16 UTC that only C2-countable spines are ground truth; packed, trained on by nothing) and g3_excluded (G3's val and test transitional persons). The validation draw is a rank under data/splits/anomaly-validation.json's salt and counts over the countable train persons with no fold; the stage reports its digest and the render pull request copies it into `drawn`, after which split refuses another draw. A test or holdout row that cannot be packed fails the stage unless ALLOW_PARTIAL_TEST=yes in grid/anomaly.sbatch.
- The admission: the uncompressed bytes of every ok row (float16 image, uint8 label and mask) less the masks already written, never a package, decided by grid/size-rule.sh under the lock before any byte lands and recorded in flexray_pack/meta/SIZE; settled when the job ends, or by the next gate word when it died (settle_banks reads the anomaly_pack rows); exit 5 on a refusal, which the gate queues again at the same commit. A mask written once is kept by its sidecar; a package by its manifest digest (packed-<dataset>.sha256), rebuilt when it moves.
- The report, logs/ANOMALY-pack.md, carries counts, seconds and digests only; rows.csv, masks.csv, splits.csv, guard.txt and the packager's logs, which name cases, stay under anomaly/pack on the lab volume.
- The render and tune stages pass `--set protocol.name=schwingnet_spine_v1 --set protocol.config_root=<repo>/grid/flexray/configs`, route schwingnet_spine as standard_seg and schwingnet_spine_partial as partial_labeled_seg, and read only the train and val splits for training.
