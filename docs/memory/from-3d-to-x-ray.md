The programme that carries the segmented CTs to X-ray (counting prior, renders, COBRA, harmonisation, the anomaly screener, the corner format) is docs/research/xr-from-3d/README.md with issues #56 to #70.

- The X-ray annotation format is four corners per body in a fixed order (superior-left, superior-right, inferior-right, inferior-left, head up), the level from catalog.LEVELS, the column call and findings once per image, every reader named, COCO keypoints as the export (docs/research/xr-annotation-format.md).
- Anomalies are labelled on CT and carried to X-ray: readers' column calls (scan, column, reader) feed tools/chain_stats.py --columns, and odds come only from collections screened whole.
- The counting prior's settings (scale, weight, margin) are chosen on validation frames only (tools/chain_eval.py).
- Variables beside the images come from tools/covariates.py, aggregates only, ages as decades.
- COBRA is CC BY-NC 4.0; commercial use is the owner's lawyer question.
- The screener (#64) runs over NHANES II's unannotated films first, the owner's choice of 2026-10-22; BUU-LSPINE is held (3,600 AP and lateral pairs), not requested.
- The benchmarks the programme is measured against are docs/research/benchmarks-to-beat.md (2026-10-22): FluoroSAM names vertebral levels at 0.04 Dice on a real cadaver and bridges renders to real fluoroscopy by intensity randomisation alone; the contour registration (0.67 mm) needs the CT, a fiducial and two views. Workstream O (#127) makes gold masks on every paired set (the owner counts nine, of which five are spine, all lumbar or lumbosacral: C-ArmLSpine, COBRA, DeepFluoro, Ljubljana, LUMOS; the IPCAI femur set, the IEEE DataPort pig lower limb, Veriserum and the Geneva hip phantom only tune the registration): TotalSegmentator, the owner in ITK-SNAP, Dr Tech verifying, projection and DiffDRR registration; the annotated X-rays without a CT then train an independent pseudolabeller or test the network, one role per person.
- Workstream P (#135) carries level naming from the lumbosacral gold to C2 (docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md, 2026-10-22): no paper has shown cross-region transfer on radiographs; render-trained labelling does transfer to real intraoperative Long-Film images (Huang 2022, 88.3 %, regions not stated); so the anatomy comes from whole-spine renders and only the render-to-real correction is learned on the lumbosacral pairs and carried upward, and a collimated mid-thoracic frame with no anchor in view is the open risk, measured apart.
