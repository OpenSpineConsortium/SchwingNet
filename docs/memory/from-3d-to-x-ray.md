The programme that carries the segmented CTs to X-ray (counting prior, renders, COBRA, harmonisation, the anomaly screener, the corner format) is docs/research/xr-from-3d/README.md with issues #56 to #70.

- The X-ray annotation format is four corners per body in a fixed order (superior-left, superior-right, inferior-right, inferior-left, head up), the level from catalog.LEVELS, the column call and findings once per image, every reader named, COCO keypoints as the export (docs/research/xr-annotation-format.md).
- Anomalies are labelled on CT and carried to X-ray: readers' column calls (scan, column, reader) feed tools/chain_stats.py --columns, and odds come only from collections screened whole.
- The counting prior's settings (scale, weight, margin) are chosen on validation frames only (tools/chain_eval.py).
- Variables beside the images come from tools/covariates.py, aggregates only, ages as decades.
- COBRA is CC BY-NC 4.0; commercial use is the owner's lawyer question.
- The screener (#64) runs over NHANES II's unannotated films first, the owner's choice of 2026-10-22; BUU-LSPINE is held (3,600 AP and lateral pairs), not requested.
- The benchmarks the programme is measured against are docs/research/benchmarks-to-beat.md (2026-10-22): FluoroSAM names vertebral levels at 0.04 Dice on a real cadaver and bridges renders to real fluoroscopy by intensity randomisation alone; the contour registration (0.67 mm) needs the CT, a fiducial and two views. Workstream O (#127) makes gold masks on every paired set (the owner counts nine, of which five are spine, all lumbar or lumbosacral: C-ArmLSpine, COBRA, DeepFluoro, Ljubljana, LUMOS; a tenth, the digital twin's cadaver of arXiv 2412.08020, 80 Loop-X frames registered to 4 CTs up to T2, is the one real C-arm pair above L1; the IPCAI femur set, the IEEE DataPort pig lower limb and Veriserum only tune the registration, and the Geneva hip phantom stays the unseen-device test): TotalSegmentator, the owner in ITK-SNAP, Dr Tech verifying, projection and DiffDRR registration; the annotated X-rays without a CT then train an independent pseudolabeller or test the network, one role per person.
- Level naming on real images with no CT is published (Huang 2022 on cadaver Long-Film images; Zeng et al., Electronics 2024, on CM Spine's intraoperative AP lumbar G-arm images of one device, per-level AP and no latency), so the talk's first-time claim is the whole of it, any angle and the measured latency included, and a detector of its own is the reported stack's, not yet version 1's; Zeng's row, VDVM (Zhang et al. 2023, which needs a DR radiograph of the patient), named C2 to C4 corners on swallow videofluoroscopy (Zhang et al. 2021, the Pittsburgh corpus), the NIH and Yale denoiser and a granted patent on learned denoising of live fluoroscopy (US 11,769,229 B2) as prior art, and the denoiser ablation's arms, are docs/research/fluoroscopy-list-prior-art.md (2026-10-23).
- Workstream P (#135) carries level naming from the lumbosacral gold to C2 (docs/research/lumbosacral-to-cervical-extrapolation.md, 2026-10-22): no paper has shown cross-region transfer on radiographs; render-trained labelling does transfer to real intraoperative Long-Film images (Huang 2022, 88.3 %, regions not stated); so the anatomy comes from whole-spine renders and only the render-to-real correction is learned on the lumbosacral pairs and carried upward, and a collimated mid-thoracic frame with no anchor in view is the open risk, measured apart.
- The staged plan from the latent shape model to a real-time C-arm level classifier is bordercall #67, stages #68 to #75 (2026-10-31), with six decisions left to the owner listed in #67.
