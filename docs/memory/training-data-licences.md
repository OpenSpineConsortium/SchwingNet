Every run records the licence of each dataset it trained on, and a recipe cuts a catalog to the licences a network may use.

data/licences.json holds one entry per dataset (data/sources/ records and registry sources), each with its licence line copied word for word and a class: open, share-alike, non-commercial, agreement, no-derivatives or unknown. tools/licences.py proposes the class by the strictest reading of the line; only the owner's reading ("reviewed": "owner YYYY-MM-DD") may loosen it, and the commercial recipes take owner-reviewed sources only. tools/flow.py run writes PROVENANCE.json and PROVENANCE.md before its first step and refuses a catalog outside `--recipe`; `tools/licences.py derive` cuts a catalog to a recipe. A new dataset or a changed licence line needs `python3 tools/licences.py propose --write` and `render`, or scripts/check.sh fails. docs/research/training-data-licences.md says why and where the sources stand.
