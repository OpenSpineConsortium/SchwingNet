The gold tier 3 CTs are one dataset of their own (the owner, 2026-10-27 22:49 UTC): ENHANCE.PET, VerSe and TotalSegmentator in a read-only versioned SQLite store that tools/gold3_ct.py builds from data/gold3-ct/ with no grid job.

- Members, by the field test of rule 2 since 2026-10-29 (a CT is admitted when its field holds C2 and the top of the sacrum, whatever its masks draw; tools/gold3_field.py writes data/gold3-ct/admission.json and docs/research/gold3-ct/ADMISSION.md, and check.sh holds both): ENHANCE.PET 1,557 whole of 1,597 (autoPET 979 = 865 to 900 persons; Leipzig 379 and Careggi 199 carry no person id, so at most 1,478 persons; 37 with no C2 silver 3; 1134, 1283 and 1347 repaired and gold 4, rows 1134r, 1283r, 1347r); VerSe 18 of 374 series (the 17 the records read and verse607), each provisional while a segmenter adds the sacrum VerSe never draws; TotalSegmentator 156 of 1,939 subjects (149 whole by its masks, 7 to complete; 162 draw no vertebra). 21 border cases are silver 3 until a person's look.
- ENHANCE.PET's facts (data/gold3-ct/enhance-pet/facts.jsonl, FACTS.json) are made by bordercall's holdings/enhance_facts.py, since reading masks needs numpy and scipy; SchwingNet's side is standard library only.
- Two column calls per study, by names and by ribs, never merged; one primary class each; the status confirmed_by_masks is agreement of two masks the same readers checked, never a reader's read of the CT; reader_confirmed comes only from a recorded bench verdict; needs_ct is set on every lumbosacral question.
- The person key is `<member>:<native id>`; an ENHANCE.PET study is its own person for splits; holdout and fold persons of data/splits/anomaly-holdout.json are carried with training_allowed false. Nothing new goes under data/splits or data/registry, which would change the bank and catalog keys.
- `python3 tools/gold3_ct.py check` in scripts/check.sh fails on a stale STATS.md, QA.md or page data; after the facts change, run `stats`, `qa` and `page`, which build in memory; `build` makes a store only where `--root` or GOLD3_ROOT names a folder outside the repository.
- Rows: VerSe's and TotalSegmentator's facts (data/gold3-ct/verse/, data/gold3-ct/totalsegmentator/, each with FACTS.json) are made by bordercall's holdings/fullspine_facts.py from the public releases (OSF, Zenodo), never the grid; the check fails when a member's rows are short of the studies members.json lists. Run the reader with --workers 1 where memory is under 16 GB, since it decompresses each whole mask.
