The X-ray datasets and the data platform's unfetched sources reach the grid through grid/: one paste submits two afterany chains of reqp/requeue jobs.

grid/README.md is the owner's paste (issue #38, 2026-10-21). grid/run-xray-chain.sh submits three jobs, 01-fetch (tools/fetch_sources.py over grid/fetch-xray.json, the data platform's reacquire.py helpers, FETCH.json with every file's sha256 at <data root>/<id>/), 02-bank-ingest (tools/bank.py by a recipe grid/banks/<id>.json, then tools/ingest.py per source) and 03-catalog (tools/catalog.py build over every MANIFEST.json under the data root, then split-check), each stage writing a STAGE-REPORT.md under grid/logs/. grid/run-platform-fetch.sh submits one grid/platform-fetch.sbatch per source of spinesurg-ct-nnunet's data_platform/sources.json that is not on the grid, through acquisition_fetch.py (zenodo, s3, hf, idc with a manifest grid/idc_manifest.py writes from idc-index). Both read the volume's free space with df and refuse to submit beyond it minus 1 TB; CT-RATE (21.3 TB), NLST full (2.3 TB) and MIMIC-CXR-JPG (570 GB) are separate lines the owner runs or skips. Each open set has a registry entry data/registry/<id>.json; a recipe field spelled "to confirm" stops that source until the page is read; DICOM sources wait for pydicom in pixi.lock; tokens come from mode-0600 files, never a command line. Tests: tests/test_xray_chain.py and tests/schwingnet/test_bank.py.
