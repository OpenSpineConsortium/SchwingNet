The QA loop (grid word `qaloop <plan>`) is the owner's self-propelling pipeline of 2026-10-31: QaloopFlow trains the auto-anatomist round after round on six column classes' phantoms and asks six gates until every one is met, a gate lacks its data, no failing gate has a change left in the plan's ranges or the measured memory, or the plan's rounds run out.

- The flow is QaloopFlow (flows/qaloop.py), held to docs/research/qa-loop/PREREG.md and to the plan's bytes (grid/qaloop/qa_smoke.json, grid/qaloop/qa_loop.json); a round is tools/qaloop_round.py, the gates and the controller tools/qaloop_gates.py, the report tools/qaloop_report.py through tools/report.py --study qa-loop; where it stands is docs/research/qa-loop/README.md.
- The next round is the round step again (Metaflow's recursive switch), so the loop is one run; grid/gate.sh queues it as a chain of 12-hour GPU links on galhp or gmsap, whichever Slurm frees first and never gvohp's V100s (QALOOP_PARTITION, a list hpcworker's framework 29 takes; one link for the smoke, QALOOP_LINKS=6 for the loop), each afterany the one before and resuming the run at the round a time limit cut; a send after an unended chain carries it on at the chain's own commit, and a chain that ended in failure is sent again only after a commit; a chain a failed link (exit not 143) or the cancel word stopped starts anew at the checkout once a commit has moved it.
- Decision: the owner's rule of 2026-10-31 01:57 UTC, no artificial limit, only an understanding of each block's space so the memory never chokes: each round measures every block's bytes on disk and peak memory per unit and sizes the next round's rows and fingerprint persons by them; the loop plan's limits for those two levers are null, and its other limits (the name head's weight, the landmarks, the presence floor) are the ranges the controller searches.
- Decision: a fingerprint cell is beyond one frame of the pool's landmarks only where the upper Wilson bounds of its own accuracy and of the whole pool's are both below the bar, and only once the memory holds no more persons, since the first smoke called perfect cells of a few runs beyond and the ceiling is measured at the selection's size.
- The real arm is C-ArmLSpine's paired CBCT and spot frames: the landmarks are placed on each case's masks in 3-D and projected through each frame's registered pose (the owner's ask of 02:03 UTC); the test persons are never read.
- Decision: the owner's asks of 2026-10-31 04:18 and 04:36 UTC: a training watch (PREREG.md section 5a) writes the loss and the held Dice and distance every check to TREND.jsonl, the round's live Metaflow card and Weights and Biases, stops the loop at once on a number that cannot be right (verdict stopped, never retried), stops training after patience_epochs with no gain (50 in the loop plan, counted across rounds) and ends the loop no progress after patience_rounds rounds that raise none of the steady measures; the round step carries Metaflow's own `@card` (refreshed while it runs) and `@retry`, its artifacts and the run's tags plan:, chain: and loop:.
- The gold arm (docs/research/qa-loop-gold/, the owner's ask of 2026-10-31 03:24 UTC): the kind qaloop_gold, QaloopGoldFlow (flows/qaloop_gold.py) under its own frozen PREREG, plans grid/qaloop/qa_gold_smoke.json and qa_gold.json, sent by `qaloop <plan>` (QALOOP_GOLD_PLANS); tools/gold_xray.py reads each gold tier 3 and gold tier 4 CT once a chain, places the oracle's landmarks on its label map and casts radiographs through drawn C-arm poses, and the round asks gold naming, gold span and gold presence tier by tier. Constraint: the tiers are never pooled (each view carries its tier), a gold tier 3 person is left out of gold tier 4, the test persons and the latent prior's test reserve are read only by key to leave them out, no gold CT or radiograph is trained on, and a chain's case list is frozen at its first round. On real CTs the sacrum's top is its body's, not the wings' that rise beside L5, and a level the oracle reads more than 8 mm off its bone is left unread (qa-loop-gold/DEVIATIONS.md, items 1 and 2).
