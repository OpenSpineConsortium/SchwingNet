The frame catalog is a store of read-only, versioned SQLite files built from append-only content-hashed shard manifests and the registry under data/registry/, with the person-keyed split under data/splits/; images stay as files on the grid at their content addresses, Metaflow steps read a version by path and pass its sha256 and a query's answer as artifacts, and redundancy is the volume's.

- The design is docs/research/data-catalog.md (task #10 and #33, the owner's asks of 2026-10-21); the comparison with Google, Meta, the medical consortia and the table formats is docs/research/dataset-organisation-at-scale.md; the pure rules are src/schwingnet/catalog.py under the gate; the tools are tools/catalog.py (check, part, build, versions, query, similar, embed, split-check, summary, export) and tools/ingest.py (plan, run) on the standard library's sqlite3.
- A store is one directory: versions/catalog-NNNNNN-<12 hex>.sqlite, each 0444, opened mode=ro&immutable=1 and hashed against its log line on open (the mode guards a slip, the read-only mount and the hash guard the bytes); one writer at a time under the store's LOCK; VERSIONS.jsonl, one append-only line per version with its parent, when it was written, what it added and dropped and its counts; CURRENT, the latest. A build or embed that changes anything is a new version; one whose bytes equal the latest is dropped (the code commit is an input, so naming another makes a new version).
- One tree: the data root lists sources alphabetically, since a registry entry's grid_root is `<data root>/<source>`; shards are `<grid_root>/shards/<date>-<12 hex>/`, frames `<aa>/<sha256>.<ext>`; the tool refuses anything elsewhere. A new source is one registry entry, one folder, one ingest; a re-ingest is a new shard that supersedes the old one, never a rewrite; a shard is one kind (train, test, review), and ingest and build both refuse a train or review shard holding a frozen test person and a test shard holding anyone else.
- The split: a person on data/splits/test-persons.json is test; everyone else is validation where the first 8 bytes of sha256("schwingnet-split|<salt>|<person>") fall below validation_share of the hash space, training otherwise; `split-check` is the REFORMS disjointness rule over a version's person table and exits 1 before any training step reads it.
- The device and exposure facts (modality, device class, make, model, kVp, mAs, pixel size, view band) are columns, so the owner's one-domain hypothesis is measured per device and band, not assumed.
- Measured 2026-10-21 on synthetic banks in the cloud container: 20,000 frames build in about 6 s, queries in under a second, 20 readers at once on one version; nothing touched the grid, whose first ingest waits for the owner's word and runs from the grid's shell or one CPU job (data-catalog.md, "The first ingest on the grid"); a frames-as-files loader against a packed shard over CephFS is not yet measured.
