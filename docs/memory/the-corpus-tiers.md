The owner's gold and silver tiers of 2026-10-27 sort every dataset by whether one study shows C2 to the sacrum (gold) or less (silver), then 1 to 4 by a real CT and X-ray pair and a person's annotation; the table is data/corpus-tiers/sources.json, the per-case sort is the grid word `census tiers`.

- The tiers (the owner, 2026-10-27 03:06 UTC): 1 a CT and a real X-ray of the same person with a person's annotation of the vertebrae on at least one, 2 the same pair with none, 3 a CT or an X-ray alone with a person's annotation, 4 alone with none; gold for a whole spine, silver for a partial one. The owner wrote 3 and 4 as "CT paired or full spine XRay", read as one modality alone, which the note says wherever it prints them.
- A person counts once, at the best tier of their studies, gold 1 to 4 before silver 1 to 4; whole means countable from C2 (tools/bordercall_corpus.py's reading) with the sacrum in the mask; a pair is gold only when both images are whole, so real C-arm frames pair a CT only as silver.
- A machine's masks or names (SPINEPS, VERIDAH, any segmenter) never raise a tier; a report that only mentions a level is report-only and does not either; a DRR, edited or not, is no X-ray, and an edited DRR stands in for one only after the domain-gap study shows it cannot be told apart, reported apart and never pooled.
- The experiments use gold whenever they can, and the count from C2 is the truth on a whole CT whether or not a person annotated it; the power is the Wilson interval over gold persons and the exact zero-error bound (tools/corpus_tiers.py power).
- The per-case sort is one CPU job, `census tiers` (grid/census.sbatch, tools/corpus_tiers.py census), over the existing masks the transitional census lists, which waits for the owner's word and count; until it reports, a CT source's split between gold and silver is an estimate marked needs_grid_read.
