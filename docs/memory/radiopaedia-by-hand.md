Radiopaedia's Spine cases are held under the owner's agreement 202610-9517 and are saved by hand, since the site refuses scripts; tools/radiopaedia_import.py lays them out for the two bank recipes, and keeps a case saved as a CT too as a paired case.

- The agreement: non-commercial machine-learning licence, countersigned by Andrew Murphy on 2026-10-23, the signed copy received from Radiopaedia's licence manager on 2026-10-26; the ID 202610-9517 goes in every publication (Appendix A). Clause 3 forbids downloading that degrades the site; there is no export and no DICOM. The record is data/sources/radiopaedia.json.
- The site's bot protection refuses any non-browser request (HTTP 406, 2026-10-31), and a session never disguises a client as a browser to get past it. A scripted fetcher waits for Radiopaedia to allowlist a named client; a request for that was drafted in the owner's Outlook on 2026-10-31.
- The owner saves images as <group>/<rID>/*.jpg, group fluoro or xr; `python3 tools/radiopaedia_import.py run --from <saved> --to <out>` names each image by its bytes, refuses one image under two cases, and writes CASES.csv, PAIRS.csv and SHA256SUMS, so the folder is carried to the grid (grid/carry/README.md).
- A paired case is one also saved as a CT, in the third group ct as ct/<rID>/[<series>/]<n>.jpg, each slice named by its one number (a name with none or two, or two slices of one number, is refused); PAIRS.csv lists each rID held as a CT and as fluoro or xr. No recipe banks the ct group, since the banks hold 2-D frames and a JPEG stack has no spacing: the CT gives the film's level truth and transitional anatomy, read by a person counting from C2 (the ribs a finding, never the anchor), never projected landmarks or millimetres; a level only the case text names labels nothing until Radiopaedia confirms that use in writing (docs/research/radiopaedia-paired.md).
- The sources are radiopaedia_fluoro (rf) and radiopaedia_xr (dx) in data/registry/ and grid/banks/, aliased to radiopaedia's non-commercial class in data/licences.json; the person is the rID. Before any frame enters a test split, the leak check runs against every held shard (grid/run-leaks.sh NEW=<source>/<shard>), since public sets reuse Radiopaedia images.
