The DRR domain-gap study (the owner's ask of 2026-10-25, #391): tools/domain_gap.py sets FleXray's DRRs and other synthetic sets beside the held fluoroscopy and film corpora, with the replication test of FleXray's renderer; two fetches and two GPU jobs, bench flexray_drr_seg then bench flexray_drr_gap; design and results in docs/research/drr-domain-gap/README.md.

- The groups are data/domain_gap/groups.json (salt schwingnet-drr-gap-v1, 23 groups): each picks files from the sample index, a folder walk, renders made in the job, or FluXray's ThunderDB, with a person rule; the pure rules (person keys, the salted choice, the words) are src/schwingnet/domain_gap.py under the gate, the numerics tools/domain_gap_stats.py, the renderer tools/flexray_render.py over tools/render_blocks.py.
- Rendering is a configuration of named blocks (src/schwingnet/render_blocks.py: DEFAULT is FleXray's released MOOSE recipe on the project's segmenter, FLEXRAY the same on MOOSE labels, PHYSICAL with the attenuation randomisation off, FLUXRAY_RAW the paper's 90-pose raw stream FluXray was edited from) run by one of two engines, this project's blocks or FleXray's own code; seven render groups on the same 250 CTs, six pairs each isolating one thing, and the word "replicated" where the logistic C2ST AUC's lower bound is at most 0.6 and the MMD's q at least 0.05, the verdict being flexray_replica against flexray_reference in the flexray space.
- The segmenter is a block, `segmenter` (src/schwingnet/segmenters.py, the one home of the names, the two alphabets, the ids and the maps, imported by the render blocks and the training blocks): spineps, the default and the production runs', reads SPINEPS's existing masks in the VerSe alphabet from the data root's spineps and never runs SPINEPS; moose, only when replicating FleXray's own dataset, reads moosez's masks, which bench flexray_drr_seg makes under the data root's moose from the weights `fetch moose_weights` lands; TotalSegmentator is no value (the owner's words of 2026-10-26). A label volume of either scheme carries MOOSE stored ids, what FleXray's own code reads: L6 and the thirteenth ribs are in it and dropped by FleXray's remap, T13, the coccyx and the trachea left out and recorded as unsupervised.
- Images are decoded one way for every corpus (percentile window at 0.5 and 99.5, as the paper did, centre square of 0.9, 256), and the primary reading is inversion-invariant (the mean of an image's features and its inverse's), since fluoroscopy is often bone-dark and DRRs bone-bright.
- Separation is read against calibration: split-half nulls within a corpus and real-against-real baselines; a synthetic set is "closer than two real corpora" or "separable" by the pre-registered words, never by a map.
- Only summary.txt (five record lines and at most 80 safe lines of findings) leaves the grid; figures and map coordinates go to W&B where the key is found; results.json, the plan and render-subjects.json name files and stay on the grid.
- FluXray needs the fetch hf_flexray_data (the Realism thread's row, #406); without it the group is skipped with its reason and neither job waits for it. The moose_weights row rides the url route of grid/platform-fetch.sbatch (each zip's length by a one-byte range request before a byte lands), and the segment subcommand unpacks the zips into moosez's own models directory inside the job's throwaway environment with the model_version.json moosez reads, so moosez never downloads. Every label record says what is present, absent (the supervised names its files could have carried), dropped and unsupervised and the order the files were written in; the subject listing asks for every MOOSE model's file on the CT's own grid; and the study reads only the CTs every render group on a folder kept. Two render groups on one folder are twins of one CT, so a render's person is its CT whichever group rendered it and their contrast is read as a paired sample (a person's two sides move together in the folds, the interval and the permutations); keyed as two persons, twins pushed the AUC far below one half and read every shift as "replicated" (the review of 2026-10-26). The verdict and replication lines carry the persons and rows a side, since the word is a non-rejection rule a small sample satisfies. The seg word is refused while fetch moose_weights is live or a zip is a part, and the segment run refuses a model whose dataset.json labels differ from the copy and a mask whose affine differs from its CT's.
- The study runs from the CTs alone (the owner's question of 2026-10-27 04:15 UTC): a DRR's pixels are the CT's projection, nanodrr splitting each ray's sum by label and render_drr adding it back, so a render takes segmenter none, a render-only value, where its recipe reads no label (render_blocks.reads_labels). flexray_drr_ct (the preset physical-ct: FleXray's physics, uniform crops, the crop's centre, no mask) is a primary endpoint, and the pair flexray_drr_ct against flexray_drr measures the labels' aiming; fluxray_raw and fluxray_raw_reference read no mask. Only flexray_drr, flexray_replica and flexray_reference (MOOSE) and flexray_spineps (SPINEPS) need masks: a group whose masks are not there (no folder, or one holding too few CTs' masks to test a group) or that --skip-masks leaves out is skipped with its reason while the others render, and bench flexray_drr_gap refuses nothing for a missing mask folder; a MOOSE folder that covers more CTs is read and holds every render group to them, so the segmentation job's report is read before the study word, and a short MOOSE run goes back to the owner.
- The study runs on the grid as three steps (the owner's word of 2026-10-27 17:51 UTC, to shard it on the H200s by the Lab Recipe): `prepare` (every stage to each reading's head and the list of units), `shard`, the tasks of a Slurm array on gmsap that claim units by an exclusive file create, run them in a process pool, beat every minute and take over a claim silent for 20 minutes, and `merge`, which assembles results.json exactly as the one-process `run` does (tests/test_domain_gap_array.py holds them equal) and refuses while a unit is open or failed. PREREG.md is frozen beside the README and every step checks its hash (exit 90); DEVIATIONS.md lists the 27 CTs, the sharding and the cancelled V100 run 40802322; PROGRESS.txt is the live state in safe lines, read by `progress` and the gate's status.
