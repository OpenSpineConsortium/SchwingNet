# autoanatomist: measurement lines from a CT, learnt from an oracle that reads them off the mask

Confidential with the rest of this repository: nothing here goes to a public place before the provisional is filed, and the method is not shown.

The owner's ask of 29 October 2026, 02:17 UTC: "finally start implementing this assuming i have an oracle for measurements on any spine which can render lines indicating how the measurement is made on the vertebral masks based on independently programmed utility code", with his pasted design notes: a differentiable degradation ladder through lambda with soft gates, the chain of levels as a soft-Viterbi layer, and a privileged-information "auto-anatomist", two encoders fused as F = (1 - w) F_CT + w F_mask with w annealed from 1 to 0, trained against a deterministic oracle and giving landmarks that draw the measurement lines, with low-latency inference. This package is that design as code, built and checked in the cloud container on synthetic spines.

Nothing in it is trained. No real scan, no grid job and no WSU data were used; the runs that fit weights are the smoke run below and the demo's at the end, each minutes long on the container's CPU, and no weight is written anywhere. Every number below comes from phantoms whose anatomy is drawn from the generator's columns (schwingcheck.columns) and names the record file it was read from.

## What the oracle is here

The oracle is an interface, `oracle.Oracle`: `read(labels, affine, names, sub=None)` takes a label volume, its affine and the map from label value to level name, and returns one `Reading` per vertebra: its frame (x to the patient's right, y anterior, z up the column), the canal's centre, and for each measurement, every key the oracle was asked for, a `Measurement` with its value and the points it was taken between, in world millimetres, so a line can be drawn for it on the mask or on the CT, or NaN with the reason where the level is not measured (C1, C2, the sacrum) or the mask too small. The "independently programmed utility code" of the owner's ask is whatever fills that interface. The real extractors are the work of the thread "Extract morphometrics from gold tier 3 CT"; until they land, `oracle.ChordOracle` stands in. It wraps scanimport's geometry (geometry.measure_whole and measure_labelled, the chords the import pipeline takes), keeps geometry's value for every measurement to the bit, and re-derives the points of each chord by the same steps; where its re-derived value differs from geometry's by more than 1e-9 it withholds the points and says why, so no line is ever drawn that does not belong to the number beside it. `oracle.check` holds any reading, the stand-in's or a real extractor's, to the interface: each value is its spec's rule applied to its points, each chord runs along its spec's axis with its named ends the right way round (right, anterior and superior being the larger coordinate), a value and its points are given together or withheld with a reason, every key is carried, and the frame is orthonormal and right-handed.

Each measurement is defined once, in [specs.py](specs.py): its kind (a length, an angle or an area), the names of its points in order ("endplate.right", "endplate.left", ...), the frame axis it runs along, the plane it is drawn on, Panjabi's mnemonic and how the stand-in takes it. `Spec.value(points)` is the one rule, written over numpy or jax.numpy, that gives the value from the points, so the oracle's number, the line drawn for it and the network's prediction all follow the same definition. The nine measurements defined are scanimport's: the seven chords a vertebral mask reads (the two end-plate widths and depths, the posterior height, the canal's width and depth) and the two it keeps for the record (the transverse span and a spinous length). An angle or an area is one more entry.

[render.py](render.py) draws a reading's lines on a coronal, a sagittal and an axial cut through the vertebra's own frame, on the mask or on the CT, each line in a colour checked for colour-blind readers, the witness voxels beside it and the value in its label; the cut positions are returned with the figure. One cut cannot hold every line of a plane (the end-plate widths lie through the body's middle, the transverse span through the processes some 25 mm behind), so each panel is cut where the largest group of its lines lie within 2 mm of one another, and a line farther from the cut is drawn dashed with its distance from the cut in its label. The figures of the smoke run are examples of its output.

## The phantoms

[phantom.py](phantom.py) draws a short column with a CT and a mask on one grid: each level's end-plate widths and depths, height, canal width and depth, transverse span and spinous length come from one draw of the generator (`draw_levels`, schwingcheck.columns.draw_columns with no measurement error), so the phantom's anatomy is as Panjabi's tables and the generator's size law make it, and every chord of every level is known before anything is measured. The body is an elliptic frustum with a cortex of 800 HU over trabecular bone of 230 HU, its end-plates flat and its sides sloping between the two end-plates' ellipses, the discs at 90 HU, the arch at 700 HU, soft tissue at 40 HU and air beyond the patient's outline; the column is tilted against the grid and the grid turned, and the CT is blurred and given noise (scanimport's own phantom gave the tissue values). The stand-in oracle reads the drawn values back within 1.34 mm at most and 0.44 mm on average over fifteen bodies of five columns (two typical, a six-lumbar, a T13 and a four-lumbar), the columns `tests/test_autoanatomist_oracle.py` runs, holding the mean under 0.6 mm and the worst under 1.5 mm: a check of the oracle against something outside its own computation.

[crops.py](crops.py) resamples a vertebra's neighbourhood into a crop of its own, 48 by 56 by 32 voxels at 2 mm along the vertebra's frame, its centre 16 mm in front of the canal's centre so the body and the arch are both in it, turned and shifted at random in training so the network does not learn the oracle's frame. The crop's frame comes from the oracle's reading in training and, in use on a raw CT, from scanimport's mask-free mode (`from_found`: the canal's centreline and the discs along it); how far the second lies from the first is not measured yet.

## The ladder and the gates

[gates.py](gates.py) is the smooth form of each hard operation the ladder needs, so that lambda has a gradient: a sigmoid in place of a Hounsfield threshold, a smoothed maximum over a soft ball in place of a binary dilation (and its dual for erosion), differentiable in the radius through the ball's rim, and a separable Gaussian blur differentiable in its width. The hard operations are the limits: the tests hold the soft dilation and erosion to scipy's binary ones within tau log N for a ball of N voxels plus what the rim leaves (a softplus rim of width `edge` is not 0 at the voxels nearest the radius: 0.020 against a tau log N of 0.014 at a radius of 2.05 voxels, so the test allows 0.01 for the rim), the blur to scipy's gaussian_filter within 1e-5, and every gradient to a finite difference within 1e-6 relatively. The Hounsfield levels are scanimport.intensity's, imported from it with their sources there.

[ladder.py](ladder.py) takes the privileged channels, the target vertebra's mask and every vertebra's, down three rungs as lambda goes from 0 to 1, each rung a smoothstep: the target's identity fades into every vertebra's; both channels blur, up to 4 mm; both blend into a soft threshold of the CT at 200 HU, so that at lambda 1 the branch sees a function of the CT alone, which a raw CT gives at test time. The rungs and their order are this package's reading of the owner's notes (a perfect mask, then semantic bone, then softer shapes, then HU thresholding), a design, not a published schedule. `Schedule` runs lambda from 0 to 1 over the middle of the run (from 0.15 to 0.55 of the steps) and w from 1 to 0 after it (0.45 to 0.85), each by a half cosine, the two ramps overlapping by a tenth of the steps, so most of the degradation comes before the branch is taken away. `train.error_curve` gives E(lambda), the landmark error of the fused pass with the mask taken down the ladder to lambda, and dE/dlambda by automatic differentiation through the gates.

## The naming layer

[naming.py](naming.py) is schwingcheck's chain of levels (schwingcheck/chain.py, the exact Bayes rule over Panjabi's tables with a missed body, an L6 and a T13 as its transitions) as a layer a network can be trained through, in JAX: given a field of bodies read in order down a column, each with its measurements, the posterior of each body's name and of the column's type, and the gradient of a loss on them in the measurements. The tests hold it to chain.py in float64: the chain it builds is chain.build's to the bit, its exact log evidence of every name path is chain.path_logev's within 1e-9 (they agreed within 1.5e-13 on 2026-10-29), its posteriors chain.py's, its forward pass at temperature 1 chain.forward_backward's, and with the typical type alone and one step it is rule.name's.

It adds four things. A fourth type, four_lumbar, the sacralized column of schwingcheck.columns (C3 to L4 with the typical column's values), with a prior weight of 0.05, this package's stated choice like chain.py's own weights; since its bodies carry the typical column's names and values, a field that shows neither end of the column cannot tell it from a typical column by the values, and only the prior speaks. Two anchors, `top` (the first body is C3, the body under C2) and `bottom` (the last body sits on the sacrum), put on the chain by the same h-transform that conditions it on fitting, under which a sacralized column ends on L4, a six-lumbar one on L6 and a typical one on L5, so with the bottom anchor the values do tell the three apart; neither anchor is on unless asked for. Each value's own error beside each measure's, so a network's doubt about one measurement enters the rule. And the forward pass with a temperature gamma, smax_gamma(x) = gamma logsumexp(x / gamma): at gamma 1 the forward algorithm, whose gradient in the emissions is every body's state marginals (the forward-backward algorithm by automatic differentiation); towards gamma 0 the Viterbi algorithm, whose value is the best path's score and whose gradient is that path (Mensch and Blondel, Differentiable dynamic programming for structured prediction and attention, ICML 2018; the tests hold it to the best path of the enumeration at gamma 1e-4). The forward pass needs emissions that factor along the chain, which the model's do only given the size and each measure's shared offset; with the offset's correlation rho at 0 the pass run at every node of the size and summed over them is exact, and the tests hold it to the enumeration at rho 0. At the model's rho of 0.5 the exact answer is the enumeration (`path_logev`), which is what `posteriors` and `body_loss` use: 92 name paths at three bodies, 339 at five, in 19 ms and 52 ms per batch of eight fields on the container's CPU once compiled (scratch timings of 2026-10-29, not a record). With each value's own error, as the network's loss gives it, the enumeration holds arrays of 7 measures by 161 size nodes by the paths by the bodies per field, in float32 1.2 MB at three bodies, 7.6 MB at five, 77 MB at eight and 1.15 GB at twelve (92, 339, 2,140 and 21,274 paths), a few of them per field of the batch, so a long field takes each measure's error, fewer nodes or a smaller batch. Three things hold it in float32, which is how the network runs it: each body's name posterior is kept as a logarithm (a logsumexp over the paths that give the body the name), so the loss far from the table, where a true name's posterior is below float32's smallest number, is still its value and still pulls, where a loss on the exponentiated posterior went flat; the smoothed maximum takes its scores about their maximum and holds an impossible move's score at NEG, so the pass stays finite with finite gradients at any gamma, where NEG / gamma passed float32's range below a gamma of about 3e-9; and a per-value noise is read only where the value is, so whatever it holds at a value not read counts for nothing.

## The network

[model.py](model.py) is the auto-anatomist of the owner's notes: two encoders of one architecture, E_ct reading the CT crop and E_mask the privileged channels after the ladder; the decoder reads the fusion F = (1 - w) F_ct + w F_mask at every scale and gives one heatmap per landmark at half the crop's resolution. The landmarks are read by the soft-argmax (integral regression): a landmark is its heatmap's expected position, differentiable and finer than its grid, and its spread is the heatmap's covariance. The values come from the landmarks by specs' rules, so a value and its line never disagree, and each value's uncertainty is the delta method: var(v) = sum over its points of J S J', with J the value's gradient in the point and S the point's covariance, the points taken as independent. `ct_only` is the path a raw CT takes at test time, E_ct and the decoder with no mask, which is the fused pass at w 0 to the bit. The design is learning using privileged information (Vapnik and Vashist, Neural Networks 22, 2009) in the form generalized distillation gives it (Lopez-Paz, Bottou, Schoelkopf and Vapnik, ICLR 2016): the feature-matching loss ||F_ct - stopgrad(F_mask)||^2 pulls the CT's features toward the mask's while w hands the decoder over. The network itself is a block (the owner's ask of 29 October 2026: the final architecture is not chosen): `ARCHITECTURES` names each one, the `architecture` word of blocks.py chooses it, and every entry keeps the fusion frame, features at a half, a quarter and an eighth of the crop from either branch and one heatmap logit per landmark at the half scale, so a new architecture is one entry and one value in the table. Three are in. `residual-unet`, the smoke run's: three strided residual blocks an encoder, the decoder up from the eighth with the fused features beside; its widths (16, 32 and 64 channels) and the crop are this package's choices, 579,230 parameters. `plain-unet`: the same layout with plain blocks, two convolutions and no residual. `probe`: the framework's test piece, whose answer is known before it trains; its mask branch has no parameter (the target channel under a fixed window round the body, pooled to the three scales), its CT branch two (a soft threshold sigmoid((c - tau) / sigma) of the normalised CT, so the only thing it can learn is where bone begins) and its decoder none (each landmark read at the blob's extreme along its direction), and the decoder passes no gradient back, so the CT branch learns from the mask branch alone, through the feature-matching term. So the probe's mask path gives one error with no seed in it, and at lambda 1, where the ladder shows the soft threshold of the CT at BONE_HU and THRESHOLD_WIDTH_HU, the CT branch's optimum is (tau, sigma) = (0.2, 0.06), those two over HU_SCALE, a zero of the feature loss, which 150 steps at lr 0.01 reach within 0.0004 on the test crop and 0.0003 on the full crop at 2 mm (measured 29 October 2026 in the container); tests/test_autoanatomist_probe.py holds these.

[train.py](train.py) makes the examples (phantoms read by the oracle, cropped with jitter, every vertebra of each phantom so that a phantom is one field for the naming layer), the losses (a pseudo-Huber on the landmarks with a 2 mm knee; a Gaussian negative log likelihood that teaches the spread to match the error with the error's gradient stopped; the values' absolute error; the feature matching; and the naming layer's loss on each phantom's predicted values with each value's spread added to the measure's error, its gradient stopped), one jitted AdamW step with w and lambda traced, the training loop on the schedule, the held-out evaluation of the CT-only path, E(lambda), and the timing of the CT-only path. The loss weights are stated choices (`LOSS_WEIGHTS`).

## The projection to 2-D

[project.py](project.py) carries any oracle reading, any engine line or any segment from world millimetres onto a detector, for the owner's ask of 29 October 2026, 14:59 UTC, that the same thing work in 2-D on FluXray's synthetics, and his word of 15:16 UTC that corners and landmarks come from the CT morphometry engine's constructions projected, never from a 2-D corner fit. The camera is nanodrr 0.1.7's to the bit, read from its source: pose = [R | R t + iso] with R the intrinsic ZXY rotation in degrees and t relative to the isocentre, the reorientation matrices of AP, PA and none, the intrinsics fx = sdd / delx and cx = x0 / delx + W / 2, and the pixel centre at u + 0.5; `Camera.fluxray(index)` is one of FluXray's 90 recorded poses (three source distances, six offsets along the column, five angles, 1250 mm to the detector, 0.6 mm pixels, 1024 stored as 256). `project_reading` gives each measurement's 2-D value from the projected landmarks by the same specs rule, beside its 3-D value, so the magnification enters a length as a factor of M and an area as M squared and cancels in a ratio; `project_segments` gives each line's detector and object length with its foreshortening, and the readability rules (a foreshortening of 0.9 or more for a length, a plane tilted 15 degrees or less from the beam for an angle, an area factor of 0.9 or more) say which measurements a view can carry at all; which it carries well is for the measured study, never for a rule. The 21 tests hold the camera to the convention's cases (the isocentre at the detector's centre, the magnification sdd / sod, the anteroposterior and lateral semantics of alpha, a round trip through back-projection, the FluXray index), and the projected readings of a phantom to what the views promise (the end-plate width whole in the AP view and the depth foreshortened, the reverse in a lateral).

## The smoke run

`python -m autoanatomist.smoke records/autoanatomist-2026-10-29` ran the whole path once on this container (4 CPUs, no GPU; jax 0.12.0.1, flax 0.12.12, optax 0.2.8, Python 3.13.17) and wrote [records/autoanatomist-2026-10-29/smoke.json](../records/autoanatomist-2026-10-29/smoke.json): its settings, every step's losses, the held-out evaluation before and after, E(lambda) and its derivative, the timing and the versions. It is the check that the path runs from the oracle to the named bodies and that its losses fall. It is not a result: 150 steps on eight phantoms say nothing about a landmark on a real CT, and no weight was kept.

- The examples: 8 typical columns of 3 consecutive levels to train on (24 bodies, starting at T11 to L3) and 4 held out (12 bodies, starting at T12 to L3), each at 1.25 mm with 20 HU of noise and a random tilt, read by the stand-in oracle; every landmark the oracle gave was inside its crop.
- Training: 150 steps of AdamW at 2e-3 on batches of two whole phantoms, lambda from 0 to 1 over steps 23 to 82 and w from 1 to 0 over 68 to 127, 313 s in all (the record is the run made after the second adversarial review's fixes; the run before it, the record of the previous commit, gave the same numbers, every step's loss to the bit, and the first run the same to the third figure, since nothing a fix touched was reached by this training). The total loss fell from 64.4 to 4.5 and the batch's mean landmark distance from 19.9 mm to 2.0 mm; the spread term stayed near 7.8 throughout, and the naming term near 5.9, about three nats per field under the prior's own loss on these fields (9.0 on the training fields, the loss with every value unread): the layer answers to the values at these spreads, but a 25 mm spread leaves each body's posterior a hump over the neighbouring levels (on the oracle's own values at that spread the true name's posterior is 0.13 on average against the prior's 0.05, and the argmax is right for 1 of the 12 held-out bodies), which is why so few are named right.
- Held out, the CT-only path (no mask at all): the mean landmark distance was 21.7 mm untrained and 2.98 mm after; the values' mean absolute errors after were 1.7 mm (EPWu), 1.7 (EPDu), 1.4 (EPWl), 2.3 (EPDl), 1.3 (VBHp), 2.6 (SCW) and 2.1 mm (SCD), against the oracle's; the mean delta-method spread of a value was 39 mm untrained and 25 mm after, uncalibrated and wide. The naming layer (three bodies, no anchor) named 9 of the 12 held-out bodies right from the oracle's own values, 5 of 12 from the network's values with the measures' own errors, and 0 of 12 with the network's spreads added, since a 25 mm spread flattens the posterior over the neighbouring levels (1 of 12 from the oracle's own values at that spread, with a loss of 6.1 against the prior's 9.1); the exact rule on the seven measures names 73.1 per cent of typical synthetic three-body fields exactly (schwingcheck's build-up study), so 9 of 12 from the oracle is that rule's own accuracy, not this network's.
- E(lambda) on the 12 held-out crops at w 1 after training, the mask branch alone: 4.6 mm at lambda 0, 6.2 mm at its highest (lambda 0.7) and 5.2 mm at lambda 1, with dE/dlambda between -8.7 and 6.5 mm per unit of lambda, finite everywhere; the whole curve is in the record.
- The CT-only path, jitted, took 38 ms per crop in batches of 8 on this CPU after a 1.5 s first call, compile and one batch included (35 ms in the run before it; 46 ms in the first run, with other jobs sharing the CPU); that is the container's figure, no device of the owner's, no ONNX parity and no p95, so it is no latency number under SchwingNet's 97 ms rule.
- The figures, [figures/](../records/autoanatomist-2026-10-29/figures/): the oracle's lines on a held-out L1's mask and on its CT, and the network's lines on the same CT from the CT alone.

## The blocks, the session and the calibration

The owner's asks of 29 October 2026, 13:53 to 15:30 UTC (the auto-anatomist as a configurable block in his Metaflow pipeline, searched over and trained by reinforcement, resumable, continued and fed more data without a restart) are built here as four modules, and the search itself in SchwingNet (its flows/autoanatomist.py, from the design in SchwingNet's docs/checkpoints/2026-10-29-autoanatomist-flow/HANDOFF.md):

- [blocks.py](blocks.py): every training part as a KEY=VALUE word (`optimiser=sgd lr=0.001 widths=8:16:32`), forty keys in one table with their kinds, bounds and defaults, parsed and refused in words (an unknown key, a key named twice, a value outside its bounds), spelled back canonically and digested, so one configuration has one name wherever it is written; with no block named every value is the smoke run's (the test holds the literals to train.py's, smoke.py's, ladder.py's, gates.py's, model.py's and optax.adamw's constants). The keys steer the optimiser (adamw, adam or sgd, the learning rate, the weight decay, a global-norm clip, a warm-up, the batch), the loss (the Huber knee, the spread floor, the five terms' weights, the naming term on or off), the schedule (lambda-then-w, joint, w-first, fixed, cyclic or adaptive, the two spans as shares of `anneal` steps, the fixed values, the cycle's values and the checks' spacing), the encoder's input (an intensity augmentation drawn on the host for the encoder alone, never for the HU the ladder thresholds; clip or clip-zscore normalisation, a field of model.Config), the network's architecture (residual-unet, plain-unet or probe, model.ARCHITECTURES), widths and groups, the ladder's blur and threshold and how far its rungs overlap, the restraint and its weight, the calibration (none, scale or quantile, with its coverage) and the seed.
- [session.py](session.py): a Session built from blocks that trains from its step, saves itself as plain arrays (every leaf of the model's and the optimizer's state under its path in state.npz, through nnx.state and nnx.to_flat_state; the step, the anneal, the numpy generator's bit-generator state, the calibration, the naming field and the blocks' record in session.json; the steps' rows appended to record.jsonl) and is restored from them by path, a file of another configuration refused by its paths or its shapes. Its schedule is ladder.Anneal, the Schedule in absolute steps: the spans are shares of `anneal` steps (the first training budget when anneal is 0), so a second call to train carries on where the first stopped, and `continue_anneal(until)` rebases both ramps from where they stand to their ends (lambda 1, w 0) by the step named, which is how annealing continues without a new run. A session restored and trained one step more is the session trained straight, to the bit, and with no block named three steps of it are three steps of train.train to the bit (tests/test_autoanatomist_session.py).
- [rows.py](rows.py): the data as rows, a phantom by its phenotype, start, count and seeds or a CT with its labels and their names, each rendered or read and cropped by its own generator and cached under its own digest, so a row renders the same wherever it stands, adding data is appending rows, and a row already rendered is read back; the list's digest names the data set for a ledger.
- [calibrate.py](calibrate.py): the spread's calibration, fit on validation only and applied unchanged: per measure, with s the delta-method spread floored at 0.1 mm and z = |error| / s, scale takes the factor sqrt(mean z^2) (the Gaussian likelihood's closed form) and quantile takes quantile(z, coverage) / Phi^-1((1 + coverage) / 2), so that the calibrated spread is the standard deviation whose Gaussian interval at the coverage holds that share of the validation errors (at the default 0.68, plus or minus one spread); evaluate then reports the raw and the calibrated spread, the coverage at one and two calibrated spreads and the bodies named right three ways, with the calibrated spread (the search's reward), from the values alone and from the oracle's values. The smoke run's diagnosis (a 25 mm uncalibrated spread, 0 of 12 named right) is what this answers; whether it does is for a run on the grid, after the owner's word and count.

train.py takes the weights, the knee, the floor, the ladder's settings and the augmented input as arguments with the defaults unchanged, and model.Config takes normalise, so the smoke run and its record are as they were.

## The fully latent objective

The owner's ask of 30 October 2026 ("since im not sure what the status of my morphometrics code is, start with one of the fully latent space approaches"), read with the planning thread on latent shape priors: `objective=mask` trains the same two encoders and decoder with no oracle, no landmark and no measurement anywhere. The decoder's two logits at the half scale are the privileged channels themselves, the target vertebra's mask and every vertebra's, so the mask is both what the ladder takes down and what the network must give back from the fused features. The loss is the binary cross-entropy of the logits against the clean channels pooled onto the logits' grid plus one minus their soft Dice (train.mask_terms), the feature-matching term as before, and the name head's cross-entropy (model.NameHead: the deepest fused features pooled to one vector a crop, a hidden layer and one logit per level name of naming.NAMES, weighted by `w_name`), so the chain's two questions, which vertebra and how many, are asked of the network's own representation, which is the latent a shape prior is fit on later (`Session.latents` writes it per crop). The frame comes from the mask too: `frame=centroid` on a row (rows.py) puts each crop about its label's centroid with the world's axes (crops.centroids, crops.from_centroid), jittered as the oracle frame is, the CT and the labels each sampled by their own affine, so a CT and a mask on two grids are read as they are; and only the box of a volume under a crop is read (crops.window), to the values the whole volume gave, since a vertebra is a small part of a CT. What the annealer leaves is read on held-out crops by the CT-only path (train.evaluate_masks): the hard Dice of the target's mask and of every vertebra's, the mean symmetric surface distance in millimetres (a crop with no surface on either side counts as the crop's longest side, and the report says how many did), the centroid error, the share named right and the share named right or by a neighbouring level (T12 and L1, T12 and T13, T13 and L1, L5 and L6 are neighbours, from the chain's columns), the feature gap, and all of these by level. `objective=mask` takes `naming=off` and `calibrate=none`, since it has no value and no spread, and never the probe, whose decoder reads landmarks; the smoke run and every landmark record are as they were. Nothing under it is trained: the first run is SchwingNet's gold tier 3 screen, on the owner's word and his count of jobs.

## The second screen's arms

The owner's question of 30 October 2026 about free energy perturbation's lambda schedules (Lambda-ABF, arXiv 2307.08006) and his choice at 16:52 UTC that day, "Build 4 arms after the demo": four blocks for a second screen, each a word that changes nothing when it is not named and each ending where every anneal ends, lambda 1 and w 0.

- `schedule=cyclic` (ladder.Anneal): lambda-then-w twice, each pass in half the steps, and between the passes back to lambda `cycle_lam` (0.5) and w `cycle_w` (0.3), a second anneal from where the first left the network.
- `schedule=adaptive` (ladder.Adapt, Session.check): w on lambda-then-w's clock, and lambda moved every `adapt_every` steps (20) from lam_span's start by the error's own slope, E and dE/dlambda on the batch just trained (train.make_error_slope): the even move to lambda 1 by lam_span's end, times the mean slope over this one's held between a half and two, so faster where E is flat in lambda and slower where it is steep; half the last move back where E rose by more than three times what the mean slope promised for it; and never behind a floor that reaches lambda 1 a tenth of the steps after lam_span ends.
- `ladder=overlapping` (ladder.rung's span): each rung over half of lambda, a quarter apart, so the target's fade, the blur and the threshold move together in places, as Lambda-ABF staggers its two lambdas on one path; `ladder=together` moves all three over the whole of lambda. At lambda 0 and 1 the channels are the rungs in turn's.
- `restraint=abf` with `abf_weight` (train.Forces, train.restraint_term): an adaptive biasing force on a collective variable. The estimate is ABF's, the mean force (reference minus value over a scale squared) in bins of the variable, a running mean of a bin's first 100 samples and a moving mean after, scaled up as a bin fills; it is applied as a restraint towards the reference, the opposite of ABF's flattening, at the weight (1 - cos 2 pi lambda) / 2, which is 0 where the mask is whole and where it is gone and greatest half way. Under the landmark objective the variables are the measures, the width among them. Under `objective=mask`, the screen's, there is no measure, so the variable is the target vertebra's predicted volume as a share of the crop, against the clean mask's. The estimate is saved with the session and restored to the bit.

## What is not done

- The Metaflow-native search with its ledger (hyper-parameter search by successive halving and a REINFORCE controller over the blocks, one flow run a round, resumed from the ledger) is SchwingNet's flows/autoanatomist.py, built from the design in its docs/checkpoints/2026-10-29-autoanatomist-flow/HANDOFF.md; nothing of it has run on the grid, and its first plan, phantom_smoke, waits for the owner's word and count. The volume rows of rows.py read a CT and its labels through the stand-in oracle until the engine's export (bordercall PR 30) stands behind the same interface.
- No 2-D network: project.py projects the oracle's and the engine's constructions; which of the 106 measures of the clinical reread survive projection is [research_notes/measures-to-2d/README.md](../research_notes/measures-to-2d/README.md), 51 keys drafted at the checkpoint and verified on 29 October 2026 (its verification paragraph and check_rows.py) and 55 still to write, from measures.md's radiograph parts, the engine's constructions and project.py's rules, and the pairing of each CT with its FluXray synthetics is designed in the handoff above.
- No real extractor fills the oracle: ChordOracle takes scanimport's chords on voxel coordinates in world millimetres (a surface lies between the last voxel centre inside the bone and the first outside, placed by geometry.edge_gap), not on surface meshes; the extractors thread brings the rest of Panjabi's measurements.
- No training beyond the smoke run, on nothing but phantoms, and no measured gap between the mask-free crop frame and the oracle's; a network on real CTs waits for the owner's word and count, on the grid through his Metaflow pipeline, and the hold on WSU data stands.
- The spread is uncalibrated after the smoke run: it is wide, and the naming layer fed the network's spreads flattens its posterior over the neighbouring levels (0 of 12 named right, three nats per field under the prior's loss); calibrate.py now fits a factor on validation and `evaluate` reports the bodies named right with the calibrated spread beside the other two ways, but no run has measured whether a calibrated spread names more bodies right.
- The ladder's dilation and erosion are built and checked but not yet a rung: the three rungs use the identity fade, the blur and the threshold.
- Sacralization enters as a type with the typical column's values; the owner's other account's item 6 (anomalous places in the exact rule) will replace L6's and T13's constructed table columns, and may replace this.

## The anneal on a slider

[demo.py](demo.py) trains a network of the smoke run's widths, on its phantoms, seeds, batches and schedule, on one measurement, the upper end-plate's width (Panjabi's EPWu), with the naming layer off (the smoke run trains all seven measurements with the naming layer on), and keeps a snapshot every fifth step: the step's lambda and w, and the two end-plate landmarks and the width the CT-only path reads on each of the 12 held-out crops. Its network is `train.train`'s for that measurement with the naming layer off, to the bit, every weight and every step's loss. `figure` draws a run at a fixed 1060 by 680 pixels, with a slider over the snapshots, a Play button and the numbers in its title. On the left is one held-out vertebra's CT as its bone surface at 200 HU (the one whose error after the last step is the median of the 12, unless another is named), and on it the width the network reads from the CT alone (orange) beside the oracle's (blue), each with its length in the legend, and that vertebra's own mask taken down the ladder to the snapshot's lambda (`ladder.degrade`, pooled two voxels a side) in green at the weight w, so that it fades out as w falls to 0: a picture of what training hands the mask branch, which the network's reading never sees. On the right, stacked and of one height, are lambda and w over the run, and the width on that vertebra over the run against the reference. The figure needs plotly, which neither requirements file pins (Colab has it); the command says so before it trains. Run as a command, it writes the figure as a page in a box of fixed width set in from the edges; with `--record`, the run's numbers as JSON (the settings, each snapshot's lambda, w and mean error, each held-out vertebra's reference and reading at the end, the seconds from making the phantoms to the last snapshot, and what ran it); and with `--save`, the run itself with its network's weights, which `--load` draws again for any held-out vertebra (`--vertebra`) without training. Its run is the one training beyond the smoke run's, on the same phantoms; the list of what is not done, above, was written before it.

With `--load` and `--test`, the kept network reads five test sets drawn after training, each six columns of three vertebrae, from the CT alone: new patients of the training phantoms' kind; the unseen levels T6 to T10, where training saw T11 to L5; six-lumbar, T13, four-lumbar and lumbar-rib columns at their transitions; the new patients with 200 HU of noise, where training had 20; and the new patients in 3 mm voxels, where training had 1.25 mm. Its page plots each vertebra's width against the reference and each set's mean error, with a 95% interval that resamples whole columns, beside always guessing the training phantoms' mean width, which reads no CT at all, and tables the numbers. The crops are still placed from the oracle's frame, as in training.

[notebooks/anneal_demo.ipynb](../notebooks/anneal_demo.ipynb) runs it in Colab and asks Colab for a T4 GPU when it opens. Colab's own GitHub sign-in, which opens the notebook, stays with Colab's page and cannot be used by the notebook's code, so the setup cell gets bordercall with the first of three: a GitHub token kept in Colab's Secrets, which makes this step automatic; else a sign-in with GitHub from inside the notebook, GitHub's device flow for the SymphonyBoard App, which needs Enable Device Flow ticked in the App's settings (the cell shows the code large beside a button that copies it and opens GitHub's page in a small window; the reader pastes the code and authorizes the App, and the cell clones with the token GitHub grants and prints it nowhere); else a ZIP of the branch uploaded by hand, which the cell also asks for, saying why, when git cannot clone the demo with the token. It installs the pinned JAX, Flax and optax. On a GPU runtime it installs the GPU build of the same jaxlib for the CUDA version whose build the image already carries, so that pip replaces the image's build and one build alone drives the GPU: on the GPU image Colab published in October 2026 (googlecolab/backend-info: Python 3.13.18, jax 0.11.1 with jax-cuda13-plugin 0.11.1, whose NVIDIA libraries already meet the 0.12.0.1 plugin's), that is jax-cuda13-plugin and jax-cuda13-pjrt 0.12.0.1; the other CUDA version's builds are taken out first. It then checks in a child process that JAX starts on the GPU; where it does not, the cell says why and the network trains on the CPU. On the GPU the network has the smoke run's widths, (16, 32, 64), and on the CPU half of them, (8, 16, 32). Run alone on two of this container's CPUs, from making the phantoms to the last snapshot, the half-width run took 5.6 minutes and read the 12 held-out widths within 1.7 mm on average at the end (39.2 mm untrained), and the run at the smoke run's widths took 8.6 minutes and read them within 1.4 mm ([records/autoanatomist-demo-2026-10-30/](../records/autoanatomist-demo-2026-10-30/), each with what ran it).
